## Conditional stop rule

At each stage, assess whether the next stage remains attainable with the supported
semantics and observed capabilities. Stop work and report if a sound contradiction,
missing indispensable capability that cannot be resolved within the goal, or another
fundamental blocker prevents progress. Describe the exact scope and evidence.
A timeout, failed run, weak learning curve or exhausted experiment allocation is not
proof of impossibility. Use bounded, diagnostic follow-ups rather than silently
raising budgets or declaring success from a selected easy case. Never weaken the final
request or change the acceptance criteria to conceal a blocker.
