## Goal and progression

Preserve original production obligations and validated incumbents. Construct a complete
valid factory first, then improve it within a declared work/time budget. Score priority
is used area, physical transport tiles, then route turns. Width/height remain outputs.

1. Complete case-specific curriculum to original-root improvement on A and B.
2. Demonstrate actual facility relocation with explicit reconnection and valid compression.
3. Generate varied curricula and demonstrate improvement on held-out layouts.
4. Expand observation and validation to varying facility counts, new production chains,
   branching/merging, pipes and required cycles, in separately verified slices.
5. After capability and cost evidence is sufficient, integrate bounded CLI improvement
   and evaluate diverse factories plus the original
   59-facility Heavy Xiranite request, including at most 1% empty XY area and visual quality.

The final request, external boundaries and visual requirements remain those of
[the acceptance contract](../benchmarks/layout-production/contract.md). Its provisional
benchmark thresholds must not silently become new game limits. Do not resume old
training streams or inherit their success claims.

## Conditional stop rule

At each stage, assess whether the next stage remains attainable with the supported
semantics and observed capabilities. Stop work and report if a sound contradiction,
missing indispensable capability that cannot be resolved within the goal, or another
fundamental blocker prevents progress. Describe the exact scope and evidence.
A timeout, failed run, weak learning curve or exhausted experiment allocation is not
proof of impossibility. Use bounded, diagnostic follow-ups rather than silently
raising budgets or declaring success from a selected easy case. Never weaken the final
request or change the acceptance criteria to conceal a blocker.
