# Add Transport Capacity Catalog

Status: Complete

## Context

Item and port transport kinds are fixed, but the integrated solver does not yet prevent one belt or pipe route from carrying more than the game's capacity.

## Completion Criteria

- Define and validate an external transport capacity catalog.
- Require exactly one positive capacity for belt and pipe.
- Generate normalized capacities from the vendored belt and pipe source tables.
- Load the transport catalog explicitly in the end-to-end CLI.
- Reject an independent logical route whose exact rate exceeds capacity.
- Keep shared-segment capacity out of scope until shared routing is modeled.
- Pass formatting, workspace checks, tests, Clippy, and manual CLI validation.
