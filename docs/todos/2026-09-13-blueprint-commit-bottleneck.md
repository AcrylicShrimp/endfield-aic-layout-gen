## Stage contract

Input: completed matrix plus results provenance, original frozen runtime/native
library, registered transfer eligibility, and source-bound final trace files.
Output: `blueprint-commit-diagnosis-v1`, containing per-episode opportunity and
commit classifications, per-arm/seed paired population totals, commit rejection
codes, first missed opportunity and successor action, and conditional anchor
results. Opportunity counts distinguish repeated observations of one physical
draft from distinct consecutive windows and independent episode executions.

Every eligible pre-action observation has a remaining original action slot.
Screen out pending/conflicting drafts. A facility-footprint bounding-box lower
bound at least as large as the current retained area excludes an area-improving
commit without claiming global optimality. Other surface-complete candidates
are reconstructed only as needed and committed once on a private copy. Cache
physical validity and area by original blueprint and exact draft geometry;
compare with each observation's actual retained best. Do not claim a scripted
commit intervention as autonomous policy success or modify historical outcomes.

All policy `commit` outcomes already carry native acceptance metrics and can
authenticate their exact draft without another native commit. A best draft is
updated only by an actually recorded improved commit. Missing references,
inconsistent recorded states, mismatched restoration, native action guards or
diagnostic budget exhaustion produce an explicit incomplete/error result with
stable code, severity, stage and entity, rather than a zero-opportunity claim.

Completion: close the continuity checklist, run targeted arithmetic/classification
tests plus the actual isolated CLI, record limitations and results, pass required
workspace and artifact checks, then commit the finished slice.
