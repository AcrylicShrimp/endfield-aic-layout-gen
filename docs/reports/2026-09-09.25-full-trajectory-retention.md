# Smaller-factory continuation retention with the large trajectory preserved

## Result

Rehearsing complete smaller-factory paths restores their useful follow-up compression
while retaining the learned 116-facility relocation and area gain. Both adaptations
reach **18.963% mean area reduction** on the six smaller training originals, compared
with report 24's 16.778% / 13.782%. Against the fixed pretrained parent, their twelve
smaller-original episodes have nine wins/three ties and ten wins/two ties in full
lexicographic score, with no losses. Both still reach `[57456,9868,697]`, 216x266,
on the prepared large factory in all four evaluations.

Retention is incomplete: smaller medical wiring regresses against the preceding
adaptations, both lose original-large wiring quality, seed 29 loses one original-large
contraction, and watered-grass development results remain worse. These are opened
training/development results, not unseen generalization or a production promotion.

## Verification, retained evidence and next decision

The evaluation audit independently replays **all 896 actions** and checks all 112
best witnesses against full original graph coverage, native physical validity,
independent metrics, ordinary action membership and exact incumbent hashes. It also
measures actual relative facility motion. Combined with the new 48 teacher actions,
this slice adds **944 independently checked transitions**; inherited lesson proofs
remain identified. All pass. Both full medical-rate-0 and food-rate-0 PNG/SVG views
are native-validated and inspected. They show F14 relocation, rerouting and reduced
bounds, while still containing large empty regions. Medical wiring increases from
the original despite its area improvement, consistent with the score priority.

Forty-eight native-backed Python tests pass. Fresh `cargo check --workspace` and
`cargo test --workspace` pass, with 538 passing Rust tests and two existing ignores.
Admission, learning, both audits, summary, comparison and view commands run
successfully. No Rust, production constructor, native operator, game data or objective
changes. The shared comparison records allowed training-configuration differences
explicitly; the shared summary separates initial and follow-up label counts.

Retain the 42 intermediate witnesses as required lesson inputs, both optimizer/RNG
checkpoints, teacher traces, all evaluation episodes/bests, audits, summaries and two
views under ignored artifacts. Remove only the duplicate progress file after checking
its rows equal the final report. The compact provenance manifest identifies all
lesson inputs and the audit containing each episode/best hash.

This verifies useful smaller continuation retention alongside the large learned
trajectory, with measurable residual regressions. Freeze these results before
expanding to fresh geometry and unopened evaluation; compare parent and both report
24/25 adaptations under declared inference budgets. Do not select a general checkpoint
from these already-opened roots or keep fitting their first actions without measuring
new factories. Retain the large pose/continuation lessons as anchors in the next
varied curriculum and inspect raw-large failure continuations when admitting new
training trajectories. No fundamental representation/operator blocker is established.
