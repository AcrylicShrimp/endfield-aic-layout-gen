# Direct area reward and future credit

Status: completed and audited. Direct reward with future credit did not improve
the final matched mean over the untrained policy for either seed.

## Method

The user approved direct best-area rewards, followed by a separate PPO, critic
and GAE experiment. This first comparison uses the unchanged action-only actor
and Adam settings with REINFORCE reward-to-go. It does not use PPO or a critic.
The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.42-direct-area-reward.md) was recorded before
implementation. Production construction, routing, native acceptance, valid
exploration, restore-best and lexicographic witness retention are unchanged.

For each action, reward is `(best_area_before - best_area_after) / initial_area`.
The total equals the final fractional area reduction. A temporary worsening,
failed edit or restoration earns no immediate reward; earlier actions receive
subsequent rewards through undiscounted reward-to-go. An accumulated prefix of
policy-score gradients computes the same gradient without retaining a separate
parameter-gradient copy for every action.

The comparison uses the stage-39 product/rate/setup distribution, actor seeds
17/29, generation/action RNG schedules, 32 batches of 32 factory attempts and 32
selections per admitted episode. The 16 evaluation factories per seed are held
fixed and regenerated at updates 0/8/16/24/32. Historical rank measurements exist
only at 0/16/32. Each seed starts from the matched untrained actor. Rejected
construction attempts remain in the denominator and are never replaced.

Reward definition and temporal credit changed together. Direct area reward also
removes the old transport/turn rank tie-breakers from learning. Adam learning
rate 0.0003, entropy coefficient 0.001 and gradient-norm clipping 0.5 are held
fixed, but the new reward scale changes their relative influence. This does not
isolate the effect of reward magnitude or credit assignment alone.

## Verification and observation

Six tests cover reward telescoping, restore behavior, proportional area rewards,
an independent float64 reward-to-go gradient, exclusion of past reward from
future actions, malformed payload rejection before mutation and paired plot
identity. The shared native-audit extraction preserves the earlier rank-gradient
audit. Its five existing fresh-factory tests also pass.

The real-native reduced check completed two training updates and three matched
evaluations. One/two-worker collection produced identical physics, samples,
reward gradients and reconstructed updates. The separate completed-run audit
checked 20 result records, reconstructed both updates and replayed four actions.
Its continuously refreshed server plots and local mirror both reached completed
status. Six observed plot publications advanced from zero to three measured
points; the final PNG was visually inspected.

The full runner checks every admitted start and best with the native engine,
reconstructs every Adam transition from a copied pre-update state, and reopens
the checkpoint including Adam and Torch RNG state before publishing a batch.
Both initial evaluation banks and both first training batches exactly matched
the historical rank physics and sampling. Trajectory replays restore global
Torch RNG state afterward so auditing cannot perturb learning.

Performance plots show measured area-reduction change against elapsed time,
selected training actions and optimizer updates. They expose per-case and
per-product scores and full lexicographic W/T/L counts in curve JSON. Atomic
individual files plus a final hash manifest let the local mirror reject mixed
refreshes and preserve its last verified snapshot. Refresh time is distinct from
evaluation time; elapsed time includes generation, evaluation and online audit.

Two actor seeds and 16 evaluation factories per seed do not establish a general
ranking or diagnose the cause of regression.

Training included all four products, all eight action kinds and factories with
2/3/16/18/23/26 facilities. Distinct training geometries were 868 and 892.
Only 34 and 33 episodes had zero total area reward. Nonzero reward alone is
therefore not evidence of useful learning. Reward-gradient norms ranged from
0.001244 to 0.007713; entropy contributions ranged from 0.000241 to 0.000302.
Peak sampled summed RSS was 32.83 GiB, including shared mappings counted in
multiple processes.

The separate final audit passed in 40.042 seconds. It checked all 2,208 result
records, including 35 construction rejections, native-validated all 2,173
admitted starts and bests, reconstructed every one of the 64 Adam transitions,
checked all scheduled checkpoint positions and replayed 128 selected actions.
The live server observer and hash-verifying local mirror both reached complete
status with all ten measured evaluation points.

The next authorized comparison is PPO with a separate critic and GAE under the
same direct reward and factory/action budgets. This result does not justify
claiming that PPO will improve performance.
