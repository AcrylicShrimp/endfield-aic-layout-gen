# Frozen factory-policy decoding

## Result

Lower sampling temperature improves the frozen trajectory-trained policy's sampled
results on the opened factories, without new training or action pruning. It does
not reach the existing geometry baseline or reduce Heavy Xiranite area.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.11-full-factory-decoding.md) fixes temperatures
0.25 and 0.05 before execution. Both use the final corrected-context checkpoint
from [trajectory learning](2026-09-09.10-full-factory-trajectory-learning.md), the
same twelve originals, two sampling seeds, eight actions, 30 seconds, 5000 route
expansions per action and history mask. Temperature divides logits before masking.
It is not exact greedy decoding; tied logits remain tied. Parent input/source/model
hashes are checked, and all model tensors remain unchanged.

The 48 new episodes complete 384 selections in 68.075 seconds, with no shortened
episodes. An independent audit validates all 48 best witnesses against their
original production graph and complete area/transport/terminal-aware-turn metric.
This adaptive development screen does not select a production model or open a
sealed test family.

Values are mean area reductions from the original starts. Two copper rates share
geometry; two sampling seeds are not independent training runs. Against T=1, each
lower temperature has nine wins/three ties on food/medical, two wins/two ties on
copper, three wins/one tie on grass and four heavy ties using the full lexicographic
score. Against geometry, each loses eighteen of twenty new-family comparisons and
ties two; all four heavy comparisons tie. Neither lower temperature establishes
reliable generalization or superiority to the available baseline.

## What changed and what remains

Each lower-temperature arm selects 140 contractions, 36 insertions, ten reroutes
and six port edits across 192 choices. The T=1 parent selected 115 contractions and
61 insertions. Executor rejection drops from five to zero in this sample. T=0.25
records 123 improvements, 59 accepted explorations and ten equal results; T=0.05
records 124 improvements, 58 explorations and ten equal results.

Sharper sampling reduces wasted exploration but does not resolve wrong preferences.
At T=0.05, both the 16-facility food original and the sparse Heavy Xiranite original
still choose five insertions and three contractions in the first sampling bank,
retaining their original best areas. Most other new roots choose seven contractions
and one insertion. The 48-facility food root chooses eight contractions and matches
the geometry score `[17061,2745,248]` in both banks. Lowering temperature further is
not a demonstrated remedy for the remaining incorrectly ranked actions.

Every dense Heavy Xiranite run retains `[2132,374,148]`, 41x52 with **30.5347% empty
XY area**. The sparse original retains `[12900,2401,268]`. No new dense incumbent
is found, and the requested 1% result is unchanged.

Two native-validated full before/after views were inspected for T=0.05 at seed
1310000: the 48-facility food root and lower-rate watered cycle. Food still has broad
gaps, long trunks and component banks. Grass narrows to 7x21 with score `[147,35,4]`,
leaving its long vertical cycle. Neither view supports a claim of polished packing.

## Verification and next step

Workspace check/tests pass: 536 Rust tests and two existing ignores. All 30
full-factory Python tests pass, including three new checks for temperature validity,
masking out the highest scaled logit, and preservation of tied logits/parameters.
All-best audit passes 48 episodes; full evaluation-action replay is not claimed.
Worktree and staged artifact checks pass before commit. Generated bests, traces and
figures remain ignored and are identified through the compact provenance manifest.

learning data should distinguish the currently misranked insertion/contraction
