# Frozen policy clock intervention results

Status: all 96 cells complete. This diagnostic changes inference clock conditioning
only, with no new training, using both audited final seed-17 actors and sixteen
training geometries. It does not change the six-stream capacity study or its gates.

Clamping the clock to the training horizon does not improve aggregate reliability.
At the same 128-command allowance it lowers translated area improvements from
492 to 455 out of 512 for width one, and from 468 to 446 for width two. Paired
lexicographic scores also favor the existing native clock overall. A selected
battery case improves markedly for width two, so the feature affects behavior;
its mismatch is not supported as a sufficient explanation or a standalone fix.

| 1 | Native 16 commands | 451 | 17 | 0.77968 |

Each row has sixteen cases and 32 paired sampling seeds per case. Medians are
upper medians of complete episode scores and cases have equal weight. These are
fresh diagnostic sampling seeds, so native128 frequencies need not equal earlier
retention-bank results. No policy is reselected from these outcomes.

| Width | Clamped versus native128 wins / ties / losses | Clamped versus native16 wins / ties / losses |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | 31 / 371 / 110 | 11 / 501 / 0 |
| 2 | 75 / 332 / 105 | 10 / 502 / 0 |

Wins use the preserved score order: used area, physical tiles, then turns. The
comparison to native16 is a budget-extension control, not an equal-budget contest.
For all 1024 paired episodes, the native16 trace exactly equals the first sixteen
clamped128 transitions, and the longer run never worsens its incumbent. Thus
differences against native128 arise despite equal command allowance and identical
starting roots, with the intervention confined to the clock feature.

The previously diagnosed `train-battery-four-gap0-turn2` case illustrates why a
single weak case cannot establish a general repair:

| Width | Native16 successes / 32 | Native128 successes / 32 | Clamped128 successes / 32 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 24 | 30 | 24 |
| 2 | 20 | 10 | 23 |

Width-two median area improves from 133 to 119 under clamping on this case, but
other cases offset that gain. After sixteen commands, clamping holds the feature
at zero, which is outside ordinary pre-action training values. These results do
not rule out a properly trained alternative clock contract; they reject promoting
this inference-only clamp as the reliability fix.

## Next decision

Keep the existing clock behavior. Finish the final capacity stream and queued
no-eligible-width confirmation. The third narrow stream's early qualifying
checkpoint followed by severe later regression makes update stability and
retention the next issue to investigate from saved training records. Neither more
data nor more updates alone has been established as the remedy. Do not introduce
another training matrix without a distinct intervention and declared comparison.
