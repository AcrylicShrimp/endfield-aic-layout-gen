# Frozen policy transfer on new factory sizes

## Result and admission coverage

Five newly constructed medical/food factories, containing **23, 46, 93, 23 and 46
facilities**, pass complete original-graph and native validation. All five frozen
policies and geometry finish both sampling banks: **60 episodes / 480 selections**.
Report 25's seed 29 beats its pretrained parent in all ten paired episodes, but
loses both food-23 comparisons against geometry. No policy achieves an improving
facility translation on these fresh geometries. Transfer is useful for contraction
choice and incomplete for learned relocation.

The [prospective contract](../designs/2026-09-09.26-fresh-factory-policy-transfer.md)
declares twelve attempts before construction. Only five are admitted; retain **5/12**
as the full admission result:

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medical | 30000 / 12000 / 5000 | 23 / 46 / 93 | All accepted |
| Food | 30000 / 12000 / 5000 | 23 / 46 / 93 | Two accepted; 93-facility construction deadline |
| Glass-bottled water | 10000 / 2000 / 500 | Not resolved | Three source-plan failures |
| Glass-bottled copper | 10000 / 2000 / 500 | Not resolved | Three source-plan failures |

Admission takes 77.086 seconds. The food-93 constructor exhausts its declared
60-second deadline. Its request is neither cropped nor retried, and the failure is
not global infeasibility. Both bottled requests stop before layout construction.
Follow-up `contextual-throughput` diagnostics identify the unresolved glass-bottle
producer at `/target/recipe:filling-bottled-glass-{water,copper}/input:item-glass-bottle`.
These are incomplete source selections, not evidence that the pipe graph cannot be
constructed. All six failed attempts remain in the denominator. No bottled factory
is evaluated or rendered in this screen.

The admitted cases therefore test fresh sizes/geometries in the already trained
enriched-consumables family. They have no internal pipe requirements. They do not
establish new-family, new-pipe-topology or sealed-suite generalization. Distinct
source-plan requests and complete original dependencies are retained for all attempts.
