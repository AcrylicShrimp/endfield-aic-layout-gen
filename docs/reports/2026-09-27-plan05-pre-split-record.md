## Goal and Scope

Make lower route/network candidate generation incremental at a stable outer node, with diverse anchor coverage, a shared prepared exact-graph scheduler, exact physical cost/resource metadata and bounded reusable work. Ordinary MCTS revisits must not rerun generation. ## Completion Criteria

- [ ] A new node receives a small stratified lower page; additional visits can expose more distinct ports, corridors, topology and repair candidates within explicit work limits while keeping a non-learned anchor.
- [ ] A first page with zero legal actions tries authorized later pages before declaring no macro action. Anchor and learned queues share prepared legal transitions, successor expansion work and found candidate identities under one scheduler, while cost-mode-specific path values remain separate so learned penalties cannot corrupt anchor correctness.
- [ ] Gumbel root selection binds its shortlist and sampled noise to immutable semantic action IDs at the start of sequential halving, resolves IDs after any action-vector change, and freezes shortlist membership for that root budget. PUCT and nonroot visits may add pages; new Gumbel root candidates enter only at the next reroot. The root cap and pending-page count are reported.
- [ ] Semantic deduplication preserves best exact cost and provenance; stable request/state/model/config identity controls a bounded cache or retained node workspace, with no stale candidate reuse after commit or version change.
- [ ] Every candidate reports exact length, turns, occupied cells, component IDs/costs, capacity use, congestion price, learned cost, overlap and charged work. Outer action encoding uses the actual candidate metadata.
- [ ] A local coverage fixture exposes a useful later candidate only after widening, and instrumentation shows no additional lower search on an ordinary revisit.

## Proposed Changes

Split `Factory::action_batch` into first-page enumeration and a stateful lower pager held on each expanded node. The pager carries request identity, per-task cursor, dedup set, charged work and a prepared exact-graph workspace; it generates a new batch only when visit schedule authorizes it. One scheduler interleaves anchor and guided queues, caches legal successors and candidate discoveries, but maintains separate path costs for modes with different edge penalties. Keep the existing CPU pool and batched model inference, and avoid reinitialization or full-state copying on each revisit. New route costs derive from exact committed geometry and catalog components, while guidance and congestion are separate reported terms. Score only newly exposed actions where possible and preserve existing visits/Q values when appending.

The fair first-stage window must cover the whole direct turn, not only its A* loop. After repair scope preparation, snapshot the remaining counts for all 16 cumulative work categories and active time once; give direct and repair first turns the same fixed half quotas from that snapshot. Open and close each temporary page window around guidance-input preparation, batched inference, native route preparation, search, replay and publication or the corresponding repair stages. Do not halve the remainder again for the second turn. A soft window stop retains its exact stage, owned partial result, charged-work cursor and memory reservation; a hard request stop remains typed unknown. Keep the held repair candidate and variant slots until repair has had its first opportunity. Guidance-input construction and native route preparation need owned stage cursors because they currently discard partial endpoint, raster, graph, blocked and crossing work when stopped. Retain the established batched inference path and measure its uncancellable per-batch time at upper scale; if it cannot fit the other stage's first time opportunity, report a named fairness blocker rather than claiming a fair page.

Across singleton tasks, do not leave one task's first-turn clock running while preparing another task's guidance input. Add a suspend/resume operation for `PageWindow` that retains its absolute category limits and remaining active-time credit without resetting either; suspend the task allowance's active deadline while it waits for its shared inference batch. Before invoking each bounded `predict_native` batch, resume all participating tasks and require every task's first-turn time credit and hard request remainder to exceed a conservative measured upper bound for that batch, including headroom. Partition the existing batch or yield selected tasks to repair first when temporary-window admission fails; retain already prepared inputs and their reservations, then retry them after both first opportunities under the full hard remainder. A single-input batch too large for the first half is a resumable yield, not final insufficiency. If no bounded batch can fit the hard request remainder, report typed resource unknown without claiming infeasibility and preserve the prepared input until the task is closed. Charge actual elapsed batch time to every participating task, and treat an unexpected overrun as a measured fairness failure and readiness blocker rather than silently consuming the repair opportunity. Reopen each suspended window with its original remaining quotas after the batch; ordinary post-repair pages use the shared hard remainder.

Inside the native route pager use explicit preparation, searching, completed-path and publishing states. A completed path remains owned and memory-reserved through publication; search/expansion limits must not discard it before publication. Advance pair/search counters exactly once after durable completion, and distinguish temporary deadlines or reservation refusal from hard limits in the reverse preflight and A* successor loop. Keep a resumable reverse probe and successor cursor, or admit only a measured bounded atomic work unit after its work and memory are reserved. An atomic reservation may refuse a temporary window and yield; if its conservative bound exceeds the hard remainder while actual work might fit, use the cursor fallback rather than treating the bound as a hard stop. The publishing state retains the path, semantic key, physical-cost/replay progress, charged category cursors and temporary reservations; perform validation and charge each work unit once, and mutate the semantic map, overlap penalties and candidate vector only at a final commit. A soft stop in replay, cost calculation or serialized-size measurement preserves that state, including any partial native replay. This prevents a page from consuming its partner's opportunity while also preventing repeated preparation, lost paths and duplicate charges.

Use the reviewed resumable native replay and completed-layout materialization/independent validator for direct and repair member publication; use the reviewed repair publication validator for complete repair transactions. Admit one bounded replay/materialization/validation visit at a time when an atomic step cannot soundly fit the temporary work and time window; do not inject a soft meter failure into the public one-shot `Session::step`, which may already have mutated its fork. The first native replay establishes legality and exact cost. Carry its source-bound trusted child and validation result through internal scoring and child admission instead of repeating the same replay; keep checked admission for external or deserialized actions. A completing candidate may commit only after independent final validation, and an incomplete repair candidate only after its existing draft publication gate. These requirements apply to all internal module boundaries: prove stable output contracts offline, keep routine runtime checks at genuine input and publication boundaries, and avoid per-sample distribution or duplicate invariant audits.

Replace root Gumbel's stored vector indices with stable semantic action IDs plus sampled noise/initial rank. Any placement/lower page sorting resolves IDs to current edges; a late candidate cannot inherit another candidate's noise or forced visits. Freeze the sequential-halving shortlist once a root search begins and expose this cap in diagnostics. A root with no legal action in its first page asks for later bounded pages before terminating. Root records retain exact exposed candidates and normalized priors for distillation.
