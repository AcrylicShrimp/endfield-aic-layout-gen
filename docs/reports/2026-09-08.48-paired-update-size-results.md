lower learning rate improves the worst observed current-level assessment in all
three pairs and improves pooled root area quality. It nevertheless fails the
predeclared follow-up criterion because one pair has fewer final root successes.
`candidate_for_larger_test` remains **false**; do not relabel this as a passed gate.

All branches start from the same audited width-one seed-43 checkpoint at 1179648
commands, with identical parameters, buffers and Adam moments except learning rate.

not three independently trained models. Each branch performs 32 updates, keeps the

## Decision

Three predeclared conditions pass: nonworse worst-assessment success in every
pair, strict improvement in at least two pairs, and nonworse pooled endpoint quality.
The per-pair endpoint-root-success condition fails (247 versus 249 in the first
pair), so the combined criterion fails. Do not increase the learning budget or
promote the lower rate as an established fix on this result alone.

A frozen-endpoint investigation can distinguish the first pair's case-level
quality tradeoff from sampling uncertainty without more gradient updates. Any
additional sampling must be separately declared and retain this original failed
criterion; it is not a retry that replaces these outcomes. The original six-stream
capacity result stays `not_met`, and no development or held-out outcomes have been
opened. Stage 4 and the original stage-5 factory/production requirements remain
incomplete; no fundamental impossibility has been demonstrated.
