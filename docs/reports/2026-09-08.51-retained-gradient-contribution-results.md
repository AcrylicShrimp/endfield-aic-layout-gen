# Retained gradient contribution results

Status: all sixteen declared update cells complete. Case gradients sometimes
conflict, but the two selected regressing cases improve their own mixed-data PPO
policy surrogate on every lower-rate update in the interval. Simple sacrifice of
those cases' recorded policy loss is therefore insufficient to explain the outcome
regression. Rehearsal contributes disproportionately large per-case gradients despite
its smaller sample share; its causal role remains unproven.

Counts below cover 128 case/update combinations per rate. They are correlated
observations on eight adjacent updates and sixteen fixed training cases, not

| Own mixed-data policy loss increases at complete outgoing actor | 8/128 | 6/128 |

The norm ratio is `||0.25 g_rehearsal|| / ||0.75 g_current||`, where each gradient
is the mean for its group's observations. Thus a 3:1 sample mixture does not impose
a 3:1 gradient mixture. This is a per-case Euclidean norm before Adam; it does not
measure the actual optimizer's allocation after moment scaling, changing gradients,
or cancellation between cases. It is not an instruction to remove rehearsal.

Pooled current/rehearsal policy directions oppose in one of eight ordinary-rate
updates and two of eight lower-rate updates. Local conflicts therefore do not imply
that the aggregate rehearsal direction opposes current-stage learning throughout.
The two cases still at level eight have a one-command prefix on current-stage
starts; current-stage and original-root are not interchangeable labels.

The pooled weighted value-gradient norm is 0.48–2.82% of the pooled policy norm
at ordinary rate and 0.23–1.52% at lower rate. Negative weighted entropy is
2.54–5.65% and 3.93–6.07%, respectively. Direct value/entropy terms do not dominate
this particular incoming gradient norm. This does **not** exclude the critic as a
cause: its predictions also determine advantages, and Adam plus 64 successive
optimizer steps may change component influence. The result does not justify dropping
value learning or entropy regularization.

For lower-rate glass gap0 turn0 and battery gap0 turn0, own mixed policy loss
improves in all eight updates, with and without the outgoing buffers. Their case
policy gradient dot actual parameter displacement is negative in all eight as well.
Nevertheless their independent short assessments decline over the interval. Fixed
on-policy training-batch surrogate improvement does not establish retention on new
trajectories. Negative gradient alignment alone is also not a reliable classifier:
ordinary-rate glass has more negative-other events (six versus three), while the
lower-rate actor has the worse subsequent factory success.

Splitting the two cases further, current-stage complete-actor policy loss increases
in one update each (glass update nine, battery update twelve); rehearsal loss
improves in every update. Hence the mixed loss can also conceal a within-case
current-stage tradeoff. Even this split improves in seven of eight updates, so it
still does not explain the full decline by itself.

## Consequence and next question

The user asked whether rotation and previous stages were being learned. All four
quarter-turn input variants are present, and legal facility rotations remain in
the action domain. Earlier stages are mixed at 25% throughout the selected interval.
The study still spans only two production structures, two gap settings and four
orientations, with policy-generated intermediate states; it does not establish
arbitrary-factory generalization. Repeating these inputs for many commands is not
the same as seeing many independent factories.

The new evidence supports inspecting the *source of the advantage signal* before
choosing gradient surgery, changing rehearsal proportions or enlarging the training
matrix. Next quantify saved value/return-to-go discrepancies and normalized-advantage
mass before and after the first improvement, separately for current/rehearsal starts
and every case. Distinguish sampled return from true expected value. An optimistic
critic can influence actor gradients indirectly even when its direct gradient is
small; this is a hypothesis to measure, not a confirmed bug. Preserve the complete
iterative objective and valid incumbents rather than stopping every successful edit.

No new training, fresh assessment, held-out evaluation, policy selection or change
to an earlier failed criterion occurs. The lower learning rate is not adopted as a
repair. Stages 4/5 and the original 59-facility acceptance remain incomplete, without
an established fundamental impossibility.
