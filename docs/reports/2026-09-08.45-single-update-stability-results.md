# Single-update stability results

Status: complete under design 33. This one private, fresh-data update does not
reproduce the selected historical 32-update regression. Reducing learning rate
reduces policy movement, but the ordinary rate gives the best area result here.
Do not adopt a permanent learning-rate reduction based on this probe.

The audited width-one seed-43 checkpoint at 1179648 commands supplies identical
weights, normalization buffers and saved Adam state to all four branches. All
branches use the same 1024 newly collected root observations and shuffled
minibatches, with four epochs and 64 optimizer steps. This is CPU diagnosis with
the corrected sampler, not replay of a historical CUDA update or a capacity study.

nonuniform movement, not a defective objective or a universal safe threshold.
The zero-rate control preserves every parameter exactly and isolates buffer-only
movement; its maximum sampled log-probability change is 0.10646.

Reliability and area need not move together: rate 0.1 increases successes but has
a worse mean median area ratio than the anchor. Rate 1 improves both here. No
branch is selected as a production actor or substituted into capacity retention.
One favorable update cannot establish stability across the thirty-two updates in
the observed regression. A bounded repeated-update diagnostic with these boundary
measurements is the next relevant test; more model capacity or longer training
without a distinct intervention remains unsupported.
