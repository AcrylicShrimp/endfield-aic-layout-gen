# Full-factory source constructor limit inventory

## Scope and evidence

This is a static audit of the `Source::generate` → `construct_production` path and its optional constructor strategies as Plan .07 calibration began. The port-assignment row records the before-state flaw; [Plan .08](../plans/2026-09-28.08-production-port-domain-routing.md) changes that path in the current worktree. The retained-history section likewise records the before-state; [Plan .09](../plans/2026-09-28.09-compact-production-source-result.md) now routes normal Source generation through a compact result in the current worktree. It distinguishes limits that can truncate a legal proposal from resource controls and from limits in a different pipeline. It is not an upper-scale feasibility result. The only completed local upper probe is the 249-facility medical release attempt in [the closeout](2026-09-28-plan24-medical-249-local-construction.md): one growth step reached 16,384 A* calls without publishing an input. The 64-call per-connection contribution was not isolated. This inventory belongs to [Plan .07](../plans/2026-09-28.07-full-factory-source-construction-scale.md); no server was used for it.

| Port-preservation variants | Eight queued variants in `network/first.rs` | Inactive in production's `connect_first`: queue expansion requires `nearest=Some`; `connect_nearest` supplies that only on the separate fixed-factory path. | No upper production sweep is warranted unless policy changes to reach this branch. |

Outside constructor placement, `Source::load` also admits at most 32 source attempts and a maximum of 32 randomized setup steps; the current native template uses eight attempts and 2–6 setup steps. The planned upper matrix uses one attempt per predeclared seed. Setup steps are applied after the physical constructor succeeds with a template `setup_elapsed_ms` of 120,000 and `route_work` of 50,000; setup failure can still reject an otherwise constructed input. These caps cannot explain the observed 249-facility constructor stop. They can affect the eventual training input-state distribution and belong in Plan 2026-09-26.08's actual model-exposure audit. The source's 4,096-facility and 16-worker admissions exceed the declared 256-facility/one-worker constructor comparison; neither is an observed upper restriction.

## Normal-path retained history cost

Before Plan .09, `construct_production` cloned the entire accepted node into every growth and external-connection `report.steps` entry. The normal `Source::generate` path retained that report until the constructor completed, took `final_node`, and dropped the report before randomized setup. A larger factory could therefore retain many progressively larger physical nodes at once. This was a peak-memory and copying cost, not a candidate cap or evidence of an invalid layout; it could not explain a specifically classified route-call stop without timing and RSS evidence. Plan .09 keeps the full history for CLI visualization and uses a compact result for normal Source generation and final-only CLI output. Its 26/59-facility local comparison and independent review are recorded in that plan. Plan .07's representative upper comparison must now measure the compact normal Source path and its actual constructor time/RSS; the local result does not settle the 240–256-facility gate.

## Physical port-choice counterexample (local)

The original, now replaced ignored diagnostic `diagnostic_six_output_ports_with_four_blocked_connections` in `crates/aic-data/src/layouts/constructive/production/tests.rs` used the real normalized six-output-port `filling-powder-mc-1-mode-normal` definition, a single belt sale edge, and four one-cell fixture facilities placed directly in front of output ports 0–3. The 32×32 fixed canvas leaves output ports 4 and 5 open. For each old `Constructor::fresh` variant 0–5, it called the actual `network::connect_external` with a 512-call allowance and independently validated every returned physical network. Before-state command: `cargo test -p aic-data diagnostic_six_output_ports_with_four_blocked_connections -- --ignored --nocapture`. Observed output:

| Variant | Selected output port | Route result | A* calls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | 0–3 respectively | no attachment; failed | 0 each |
| 4–5 | 4–5 respectively | connected and independently valid | 1 each |

This proves that the previous four-variant port policy could discard a legal physically routable candidate at a given placement. It does **not** prove that the full constructor could not find another placement, nor that this restriction caused the 249-facility budget stop. The repair must pass review before upper failures are interpreted; increasing A* allowances alone could not reach a port assignment that was never enumerated.

The completed [port-domain repair](../plans/2026-09-28.08-production-port-domain-routing.md) replaced that diagnostic with an active six-port/four-obstacle regression. The revised path retains all six compatible alternatives, routes through port 4 or 5, and independently validates the network. A separate actual-constructor test confirms the seed retains six alternatives when a deliberately one-call external budget leaves it pending. These local tests do not substitute for the 249-facility upper comparison.
