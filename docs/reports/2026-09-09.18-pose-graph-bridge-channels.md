## Result and implementation

The proposal graph now returns exactly **171 directed facility pairs** on the
complete medical 116 original. Independent incoming-heading traversal finds zero
extra or missing pairs; the old coordinate graph returned 283 with 112 false pairs.
`productionEdges` now gives each installed bridge a horizontal and vertical vertex
within its transport layer. Ordinary junction turns and coincident terminal edges
remain connected. This directly replaces the obsolete coordinate-only bridge path;
no graph adapter or fallback remains.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.18-pose-graph-bridge-channels.md) preserves native

and both frozen checkpoints. The graph only proposes facility groups; earlier
validated bests remain valid. Regression cases cover both same-item directions,
separate commodities, ordinary branches, transport isolation and direct terminals.
