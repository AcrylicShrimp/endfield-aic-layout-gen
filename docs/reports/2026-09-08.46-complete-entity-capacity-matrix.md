## Decision

Stages 1–3 remain established by their earlier scoped evidence. Stage 4's new
chain/capacity confirmation is not achieved, and stage 5 plus the original
59-facility target remain pending. Do not treat these tiny factories, a selected
checkpoint, or a valid unchanged output as completion of the full objective.

The [implementation audit](2026-09-08.44-training-implementation-audit.md) fixes two
verified defects but does not explain the regression. The frozen buffer and clock
interventions reject those isolated changes as sufficient repairs. The
[single-update probe](2026-09-08.45-single-update-stability-results.md) quantifies
optimizer/calibration movement but does not reproduce collapse: the ordinary
learning rate improves that fresh bank most. The next bounded investigation should
follow multiple updates through the observed regression interval, keeping source,
Adam state, command exposure and policy-distance measurements explicit. Do not
launch another full capacity matrix merely by extending budgets or adding width.
No fundamental contradiction has been demonstrated, so the overall goal remains
active without weakening its final acceptance requirements.
