# Existing policy versus MCTS under equal wall allowances

Status: all 128 declared fresh selected-case runs complete and independently validated.
The existing policy alone has the better observed result distribution in this follow-up.
Current PUCT does not establish a reliable benefit from adding tree allocation.

## What is compared

The existing model is the same frozen width-one PPO policy used in the first pilot.
Policy-only execution samples independent 32-command editing trajectories and retains
the best valid result. PUCT uses that identical policy to guide tree selection and
rollout; UCT uses uniform exploration/random rollout without a learned model. No arm
uses the neural value head, extra training, curriculum prefixes or scripted repair.

This case was selected after the prior pilot's isolated PUCT win. It is battery
gap-zero rotation-zero, initially score `(133,14,1)`. Each method receives six seconds
of hypothetical exploration for each of 32 fresh seeds, 1002000 through 1002031.
All runs use the same local M4 and one Torch inference thread. This is explicitly a
post-selection training-case diagnostic, not held-out generalization or a comparison
over all possible MCTS formulations.

## Complete results

Smaller area is better. Additional compaction means area below 119, the predeclared
reference from the first pilot's case median. It is not a new optimization objective.

| Method | Median area | Best area | Translated area improvement | Area below 119 | Mean area |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Random independent rollouts | 133 | 133 | 0/32 | 0/32 | 133.000 |
| Existing policy alone | 119 | 98 | 32/32 | 11/32 | 114.406 |
| UCT without a learned policy | 133 | 133 | 0/32 | 0/32 | 133.000 |
| Existing policy + PUCT | 119 | 105 | 32/32 | 5/32 | 117.031 |

Paired full-score comparisons at the same sampling seed use lexicographic area, tiles,
then turns. PUCT versus the existing policy is **4 wins, 17 ties and 11 losses**.
UCT versus random ties all 32 pairs. UCT loses all 32 pairs against the existing policy.
Every pair completes its wall allowance; none is excluded by a different resource guard.
These are observed counts on one selected case, not a statistical superiority claim.

The first transition-matched pilot had a single PUCT area-98 outcome against policy
area 105 at 8192 commands, with 47 other pairs tied. The fresh follow-up does not repeat
that advantage: the existing policy now reaches area 98, while PUCT's best is 105.
The earlier discovery is valid, but it is not exclusive to MCTS or evidence of a
consistent gain. Preserve both the original pilot and this less favorable follow-up.

## Cost and validation

Each arm uses about 192 seconds across its 32 six-second searches. Average hypothetical
transitions per search are 187770.5 for random rollouts, 183359.7 for UCT, 8824.3 for
the existing policy and 8750.2 for PUCT. The unguided arms therefore receive roughly
21 times as many native transitions under the same time allowance, yet still find
no area improvement in this sample. The learned arms share the same historical
1822.07-second training cost, plus its recorded assessments/evaluation/audit; UCT has
no model-training cost. Do not treat training and inference hardware costs as interchangeable.

The complete follow-up takes 768.87 seconds and charges 12438548 hypothetical native
transitions, 388771 restores and 562383 model calls. There are zero executed production
edits. All 128 retained layouts pass native and independent checks; complete selected
traces replay independently (895 replay commands total). All retained file hashes
were checked afterward. Policy weights and normalization buffers stay unchanged.

The largest total cell overrun beyond the exploration allowance is 9.22 milliseconds,
including runtime setup and final validation/replay. Reported total time is not an
exact six-second deadline. Every cell terminates on `wall_budget`; neither transition
nor node guard binds. Search/rule/policy code remains frozen at
`4fd8e81d8b39fe43957615e2bfd554b0e071e242`; later visualization changes do not enter search.

For now, retain the learned-policy baseline and keep this MCTS implementation as
diagnostic tooling. Do not promote it to the improvement path on the strength of one
best trajectory. A calibrated value evaluator or different tree strategy would require
its own declared experiment. The ongoing capacity study and its untouched outcome
gates remain the next main-line evidence; no development or held-out outcomes were opened here.
