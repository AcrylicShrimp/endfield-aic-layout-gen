## What is trained

Five complete report-28 originals and their five contraction-exhausted starts form
the reset curriculum: medical 23/48 and food 23/48/105 facilities. Each episode
resets the layout, while policy parameters accumulate across updates. Preparation
changes the starting geometry; its demonstration continuations are not consumed.
All original production obligations, terminals and facilities remain required.

One update samples four independent episodes from the same reset case with the
same unchanged policy. Each episode receives at most 32 selected actions and 120
seconds; rejected actions and incumbent restoration count toward that horizon.
Every selected action receives at most 5000 route expansions. Ordinary history
masking, action-kind balancing and temperature 1 define the behavior distribution.
They also enter the recomputed distribution used by the gradient.

For terminal best-incumbent score `S_i`, the relative return is
`A_i = sum(j != i, sign_lex(S_j - S_i)) / 3`. Score priority is used area, physical
transport tiles, then route turns. Every sampled action receives its episode's
return, including a temporarily worse move before a later improvement. The protected
valid incumbent cannot regress even though the current exploratory state can worsen.

The learner minimizes `-mean_i(A_i * sum_t(log pi(a_it | s_it)) / 32)` plus
entropy regularization with coefficient 0.001. This is on-policy relative-return
REINFORCE with one Adam step per four-episode group, not PPO. Learning rate is
0.0003; the combined gradient norm is clipped to 0.5. Gradient-norm clipping is
not PPO's probability-ratio clipping. Reward and entropy gradients are reported
separately. Tied terminal scores produce exactly zero reward gradient; entropy or
optimizer momentum may still change parameters.

Each initialization has 64 planned updates, 256 episodes and 8192 selected actions,
with an independent 5400-second arm guard. The guard covers sampling, observation,
inference, native execution/validation, gradient replay and checkpoint work. An
exhausted guard is an incomplete bounded experiment, not factory infeasibility or
proof that RL cannot learn. It must not be relabeled as 64 completed updates.

## Experience budget and limits of interpretation

The user highlighted that the plotted update count can obscure how little
experience this pilot contains. One update uses four 32-action episodes, or 128
selected actions. The random endpoint has learned from 8192 actions and the warm
endpoint from 6528; these are separate policies, not one learner with their combined
experience. Across ten reset cases, random initialization has only 24 or 28
episodes per case and warm initialization 20 or 24. The three unapplied warm episodes
contribute no update. The horizontal plot coordinate is the update count before
sampling, not cumulative episodes or a convergence measure.

An absent or mixed improvement at these endpoints establishes only the result at
this small experience budget. It cannot establish an RL performance ceiling or a
fundamental architectural failure. Nonzero return gradients likewise do not prove
that increasing experience will succeed. The 90-minute guard includes substantial
environment and replay work; elapsed time alone is not evidence of sufficient
learning exposure.

The policy receives structured geometry, connectivity and flow information and
selects provided edit operations. The native executor enforces factory rules;
random weights must learn useful choices and sequences, rather than discover the
entire simulator's rules without this structure. The warm initialization already
contains imitation experience. Neither fact establishes that the present sample
budget is adequate.

The registered initial/terminal evaluation is complete with unchanged endpoints.
Subsequent scaling should measure experience and environment throughput explicitly,
with checkpoint comparisons at increasing action counts. Treat the fixed 32-action
episode horizon as a separate variable: more reset episodes do not expose longer
within-episode improvement sequences. Any larger-budget or longer-horizon study
needs its own frozen contract and evaluation boundary; the current prospective
cases cannot remain unopened holdouts after this study evaluates them.

The subsequent [parallel-collection contract](../designs/2026-09-09.33-parallel-on-policy-experience-collection.md)
defines a bounded four-episode worker comparison without changing the update group
or reusing stale-policy experience. Its implementation and speedup remain untested;
it does not alter this completed registered evaluation.

## Verification boundary

Sampling retains no autograd graph. Before each update, the learner reconstructs
the observations and masked action probabilities from recorded pre-action states,
checks exact sampled/recomputed identities under unchanged parameters, and
accumulates one step's gradients at a time. Every completed group retains policy,
Adam state and RNG checkpoints. Observation-array and serialized-state bounds are
512 MiB each; neither is a total process RSS limit.

Seven focused tests passed before training. They cover lexicographic relative
returns, an analytically enumerated delayed-return example, tied-return reward
gradients separated from entropy, exact native-rollout recomputation, accumulated
versus retained-autograd gradients, immutable behavior parameters, and bounded
failure preservation. These test inputs are not teacher labels for the learner.

The checkpoint audit inspected all 118 unique model/Adam/RNG checkpoints, strict
initialization, sequential policy hashes and optimizer step counts. Both policies
have 39745 parameters; the terminal optimizer steps are 64 and 51. The completed
physical audit has replayed every selected action against the complete original request,
native physical rules, ordinary action membership, history masks, current/best
hashes and group returns. It measures facility translation and rotation relative
to canonical frame shifts. Its JavaScript implementation does not independently
differentiate the Python policy: gradient identity evidence comes from the learner's
exact replay and the focused gradient tests. Report these scopes separately.
