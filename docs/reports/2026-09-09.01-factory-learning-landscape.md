# Broad factory-learning screen

The existing frozen policy improved all 80 sampled runs on twenty previously
unopened geometries, including 32 runs on eight new three-facility chains. None of
seven short adaptations uniformly improved on that baseline. This removes the
practical reason to keep simple-root reliability as a prerequisite for exploring
larger or richer factories. It does not establish reliable generalization.

## Scope and results

Executed [the declared screen](../designs/2026-09-08.43-factory-learning-landscape.md)
at source `91eaa18225b1fd794c2ffa8404cf50bdb1fa1752`, on CUDA, in 154.015 seconds.
Every arm starts from the same trained width-one seed-17 final checkpoint. There
are 16 old geometries, 12 former development geometries, and 8 three-facility
geometries excluded from adaptation. Broad adaptation uses the first 28. Each
policy receives four evaluation samples per geometry, each with four 32-command
attempts and best-valid incumbent retention. All seven arms completed.

Counts below are sampled area-improving runs. Area ratios average each geometry's
median selected area divided by its original area; lower is better. Split labels
identify historical provenance, not a still-unopened evaluation split.

| Adaptation | Old /64 | Development /48 | Three-facility /32 | Old area ratio | Development area ratio | Three-facility area ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen baseline | 64 | 48 | 32 | .7630 | .7314 | .7836 |
| Old roots, horizon 16 | 56 | 44 | 25 | .8109 | .7473 | .8340 |
| Broad roots, horizon 16 | 61 | 46 | 27 | .7880 | .7314 | .8130 |
| Broad mixed starts, horizon 16 | 62 | 48 | 29 | .7696 | .7314 | .8046 |
| Broad roots, horizon 64 | 64 | 48 | 27 | .7630 | .7314 | .8011 |
| Broad mixed starts, horizon 64 | 59 | 46 | 24 | .7805 | .7473 | .8340 |
| Broad imitation | 64 | 48 | 32 | .7739 | .7314 | .8029 |
| Broad imitation then PPO, horizon 64 | 60 | 44 | 27 | .7989 | .7473 | .7808 |

Broader data, mixed starts at horizon 16, and root starts at horizon 64 each look
less damaging than short old-root-only adaptation in this screen. Mixed starts at
horizon 64 do not share that benefit. Different numbers of updates and episodes
prevent a clean causal attribution. These are directions to consider, not selected
hyperparameters or evidence that more training cannot work.

Imitation retains sampled improvement frequency but worsens area quality. It is
not a demonstrated replacement for PPO. Every arm is warm-started: this experiment
says nothing about imitation learning from scratch. The hybrid slightly improves
the three-facility mean median area ratio, but reduces success from 32 to 27 and
increases mean median physical tiles/turns from 5.75/.25 to 6/.5. It is not an
overall winner either.

## Cost, evidence and limits

Evaluation covers 288 policy/geometry cells, 1,152 sampled runs and 147,456 commands.
The six PPO arms consume 32,768; 32,704; 32,704; 32,256; 32,256; and 32,256 commands
respectively (194,944 total). Each imitation arm uses 252 unique teacher steps over
40 epochs, or 10,080 label exposures. Prefix/setup commands are separately recorded
in each training record. No additional full training stream or checkpoint search
was run. Final model, optimizer and recorded RNG/collector states are retained.

The evaluator independently accepts every selected layout and replays representative
traces. This pilot did not exhaustively replay training trajectories or establish
exact reproducibility. One adaptation seed and four evaluation samples per geometry
are coarse evidence; historical failed reliability gates remain failed. The cases
are small, simple path networks. They do not test shared trunks, pipe semantics,
required cycles, large-factory behavior or the 59-facility density target.

All 36 geometries now have opened outcomes. Twelve former development geometries
also entered broad adaptation. The eight three-facility geometries stayed outside
training, but their outcomes are now observed. Future unseen-geometry confirmation
must use newly reserved inputs. Successful sampling is not a new mastery gate.

## Next decision

Prioritize the missing stage-four structural capabilities: larger connected
factories and the representation, actions and acceptance needed for pipes and
shared networks. The current belt-only single-path backend is a concrete coverage
gap, whereas perfect small-root learning is not a prerequisite. Define a bounded
semantic extension against existing production DTOs and validators before coding;
do not assume this prototype's duplicate rules should become production authority.

Depth follows the decision at hand. Broad screening is useful when several unknowns
compete; a focused investigation is appropriate when resolving one issue would
change the architecture or unlock required behavior. Neither shallow-only work nor
exhaustive diagnosis of every failure is a new universal policy.
