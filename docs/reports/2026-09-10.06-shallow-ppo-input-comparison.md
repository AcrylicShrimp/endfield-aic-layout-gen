# Shallow PPO input-contract comparison

All 24 candidate/seed jobs completed the frozen four-batch comparison. The best
observed two-seed mean was **26.37% retained-best area reduction** for
`current-history-16`, **7.48 percentage points above its untrained checkpoint**.
`current-best-3` followed at 26.18% (+7.29 points), only 0.19 points behind.
The leading candidate changes with initialization seed. This short screen supports
an observed ranking, not a statistically reliable winner or long-run convergence.

## Protocol and observed work

The [frozen design](../designs/2026-09-10.06-shallow-ppo-input-comparison.md)
was committed before execution. Protocol SHA-256:
`14ccc5326c4a95da3b7f91922cd484159f31c28ca135e5c4b2a783d51d6579f2`.
The source commits were `5c530e4` (reviewed harness), `52417ae` (screen protocol)
and `fa8c832` (standalone record checker and comparison plotter). The full runtime
used by the experiment is preserved separately from the subsequent tracking fix.

Each of twelve input contracts used seeds 17 and 29, four batches of 32 fresh
factory-generation attempts, and 32 actions per admitted training episode.
Evaluation at batches 0, 2 and 4 used 16 held-out generation seeds per
initialization seed. All candidates and all checkpoints admitted the same 16
cases within each seed, with identical initial request and geometry hashes.
The two cohorts contain 32 distinct generation seeds in total. Repeated
checkpoint evaluations are paired observations of these cohorts.

Training used PPO, a separate full-current critic, GAE, CUDA FP32 mini/microbatch
64, and 24 persistent CPU collectors. All input/action definitions, rewards,
behavior probabilities, native validity checks, temporary valid worsening and
retained-best witnesses follow the frozen contract. Actor architecture is width
32/depth 2; absent input branches remain absent. This does not control parameter
count across contracts. No hyperparameters or budgets were selected after
inspecting held-out results.

Each seed-17 job admitted 123/128 training attempts (3,936 actions); each seed-29
job admitted 127/128 (4,064 actions). Generation rejections consumed attempts
without replacement. The comparison budget was equal within each seed, and
candidate means weight the two seeds equally. Individual jobs took 269.47–295.23
seconds. Collection/update totals cover training phases; the overall elapsed time
also includes evaluation, checkpoints and orchestration.

Every training episode received a fresh generation draw rather than resetting a
fixed factory. Draw identities were unique within each job and disjoint from its
held-out seeds. Candidates shared the draw schedule within an initialization
seed. Fresh draws can produce repeated requests or rates: each seed-17 job had
75 unique admitted requests/rates and 118 unique initial geometries; seed 29 had
79 and 117. These are diversity measurements, not a claim that all 3,000 admitted
training factories were unique.

The server had an Intel Core Ultra 9 285K, RTX 5090 (32,607 MiB reported device
memory), approximately 62 GiB RAM, and Python 3.12.13 in the experiment environment.
Aggregate RSS counts shared pages per process; its 39.75 GiB peak stayed below
the declared 48 GiB ceiling. No owned training processes remained afterward.

## All candidates and both seeds

Area reduction is `100 × (starting area − retained-best area) / starting area`,
then averaged across the shared evaluation cohort. Larger is better. Delta is
paired change in this metric from the same candidate's untrained checkpoint,
expressed in percentage points; it is not relative percent improvement.

The mean endpoint for `full-current` exceeds `action-only` by 3.34 points.
Adding 16 action-history entries to full-current adds 0.69 points, but four
entries do not help at this budget. Adding outcomes to the 16-entry history
reduces the mean by 1.17 points. Three best-score components exceed two by
1.34 points; encoding the full best layout does not improve on full-current.
These are observed endpoint differences, not established causal advantages.

The matched restore removal comparisons decrease the endpoint by 4.33 points
for full-current and 5.66 points for action/outcome history. Both no-restore
variants are especially weak on seed 29. `history-no-restore` seed 29 is the
only final candidate/seed regression against its own untrained checkpoint:
−0.81 points, with four improved cases, two ties and ten worsened cases.
All twelve two-seed mean deltas are positive.

Learning curves are not monotonic. For example, `geometry-current` seed 17 moves
21.29% → 18.31% → 22.60%, while `history-no-restore` seed 29 moves
16.19% → 14.53% → 15.38%. `action-only` seed 17 also finishes below its batch-2
result. Retaining the best witness within an episode does not guarantee that
later policy checkpoints improve held-out episodes.

`current-history-16` leads seed 17; `current-best-3` leads seed 29. Initialization
seed also changes factory draws, so between-seed variation includes both effects.
The 0.19-point gap between the leading means is small relative to this variation.
Keep both seed columns and the untrained comparison when interpreting the screen.
No longer training, additional seed sweep or production-policy selection was
performed as part of this completed shallow comparison.

## Tracking, verification and the cursor correction

W&B stores all 24 runs under the protocol group in the
[project](https://wandb.ai/acrylicshrimp-personal/endfield-aic-layout-gen).
Per-run links are listed in the compact provenance manifest. Final shared-cohort
metrics are available against actions, batches and elapsed seconds. Generated
PNG/SVG views under `artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/ppo-input-contract-screen/`
include `paired-view/input-contract-outcomes.png` (all contracts, untrained/final
and paired deltas) and `view/outcomes.png` (checkpoint curves).

The observer exited with code 1 when bounded terminal cleanup interrupted W&B
SDK teardown with a connection reset. Training was already complete. Explicit
terminal export finished the pending shared-cohort points. The final standalone
record check passed all frozen job/phase schedules, full admitted horizons,
fresh draw identities, cumulative action counts, shared cohorts and **120
checkpoint file hashes**. It compared **240 W&B history records and 6,072 scalar
values** exactly against durable records, with no missing or duplicate history
ordinals. This is a record/accounting audit; it does not claim a full-screen
native/model replay. Native replay and exact CUDA continuation were covered by
the earlier [readiness checks](2026-09-10.05-ppo-training-harness.md).

Terminal export also exposed a stale SDK resume cursor: `Run.step` could initially
report zero even when acknowledged remote history already existed. W&B discarded
those old-step submissions, and the complete scalar check confirmed no loss.
The tracker now starts at the maximum of the SDK cursor and remote
`lastHistoryStep + 1`, preserving the existing error when remote history is ahead
of durable local records. A failed cursor lookup closes the opened SDK run.

Five regression tests cover stale-zero resumption, remote-ahead rejection,
lookup failure cleanup and completed-cache boundaries. All eleven monitor tests
pass on macOS and the Linux server.
An actual SDK probe against an existing eight-record readiness run observed SDK
step 0 and remote last step 7: the corrected exporter chose cursor 8, made zero
log calls and left remote history exactly unchanged. The probe used a separate
copy of the corrected tracker; the completed screen's frozen runtime was kept
intact. An independent review also identified a completed-export cache that
skipped the remote-ahead check. Local tests reproduced the issue for zero and
four local records against five remote records; the cache now checks the bound
first and skips only an exact match. Independent follow-up review of all five
regressions passed with no remaining findings in that scope. The full 28-test
harness suite, final workspace check and all 538 Rust tests pass (two existing
ignores). The correction is committed as `3d1c47e`.

## User-selected baseline and elapsed-time interpretation

After reviewing the completed screen, the user selected `current-history-16`
as the baseline for subsequent training. This is a practical model choice;
it does not strengthen the screen's statistical claims. The frozen twelve-way
comparison configuration remains a record of the completed experiment.

The selected candidate took 285.20 seconds for seed 17 and 284.30 seconds for
seed 29, including four training batches and evaluation at three checkpoints.
The two-seed mean breaks down into 105.95 seconds of training collection,
71.26 seconds of PPO updates and 107.54 seconds of evaluation and other work.
Training collection includes freshly generating factories and acting in them.
The 115-minute screen duration covers all 24 sequential candidate/seed jobs.

Collection plus update averaged 44.30 seconds per batch and 22.57 training
actions per second. Holding the observed factory distribution, H32 horizon and
execution setup constant gives a rough linear estimate of 74 minutes per
100,000 training actions, before evaluation and other overhead. Larger factories,
longer trajectories or changing action-set sizes can change that rate. Four
batches establish short-budget behavior, not the time required for convergence.
