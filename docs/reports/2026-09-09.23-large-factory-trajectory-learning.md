# Learning the large compression trajectory with original-root rehearsal

## Result

The two adapted policies do **not** reproduce report 22's area-improving relocation
on the contraction-exhausted 116-facility factory. Both retain its starting score
`[58254,9925,697]`. Their first-action teacher ranks improve substantially but remain
below insertion actions. The bounded policy consequently alternates insertion and
contraction without improving its incumbent. This curriculum is insufficient for
the intended large-state behavior; it is not a successful large compression model.

Small-root teacher learning succeeds, including a newly readmitted reroute. Actual
episode retention depends on the adaptation schedule. One seed regresses on both
small originals and the large uncompressed original; the other improves those groups
but loses some prior watered-grass performance. Keep the parent and both checkpoints;
do not promote either adapted policy to production or unopened evaluation.

## Learning protocol and ranks

Both adaptations start from the same frozen parent with fresh Adam, 512 updates,
learning rate 0.0003 and gradient clip 0.5. Seeds 17 and 29 shuffle the seven training
roots; root visits are balanced and the large root cycles through its four states.
Each seed therefore gets **73 large-state updates, only 19 on the initiating pose**.
The complete ordinary action vocabulary remains in the target-mass loss denominator.
Unmeasured actions are not assigned invalidity labels.

The two seeds vary adaptation order from one shared pretrained parent. They are not
independent end-to-end model training replications. All losses and gradients remain
finite. Mean first/last-32 losses are 5.156/0.547 and 5.485/0.587; maximum pre-clipping
gradient norms are 1551.58 and 1653.31. Observation arrays occupy 78,665,960 bytes,
within the declared 256 MiB cap. Checkpoints retain optimizer state, Torch RNG,
completed update count and frozen protocol hash.

Both models reach rehearsal teacher top-one membership on 6/6 roots, compared with
the parent's 5/6 under the new labels. Mean target mass changes from 0.7745 to 0.9810
and 0.9936. The large-state ranks show the remaining problem:

| Large trajectory lesson | Parent target rank | Seed 17 | Seed 29 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Initiating group pose | 8292 | 15 | 22 |
| First recorded contraction | 55 | 26 | 28 |
| Second recorded contraction | 437 | 2 | 2 |
| Final contraction | 523 | 1 | 1 |

Initiating-pose mass rises from approximately 1.15e-9 to 0.01934 and 0.00553. Both
models still rank an insertion first. Some alternative contraction orders may be
valid; rank against the recorded sequence alone does not prove that an alternate
continuation is bad. Here no model even enters the target trajectory from its source.

## Equal-budget actual factory outcomes

Evaluate the parent, both learned policies and geometry on twelve previous originals
plus the prepared and uncompressed large starts: **112 episodes / 896 selections**.
Use two new banks, 1600000 and 1610000, temperature 0.05, eight actions, 30 seconds,
5000 route expansions per selection and the existing history mask. Every episode
starts a fresh runtime. All 112 finish all eight selections within budget.
Training plus evaluation takes **268.447 seconds**, excluding the separately charged
admission and independent audits. No episode is retried or omitted.

Mean area reductions from each group's own input are:

| Copper liquid development | 4 | 18.75% | 18.75% | 18.75% | 25.00% |
| Watered grass development | 4 | 53.57% | 33.71% | 50.00% | 52.05% |

On rehearsal originals, seed 17 has 2 full-score wins / 4 ties / 6 losses against
the parent; seed 29 has 8 wins / 4 ties / no losses. Both lose all four watered-grass
comparisons against geometry. Teacher top-one accuracy alone misses these episode
regressions.

All methods retain `[58254,9925,697]` on the prepared large start. On the uncompressed
original, the parent reaches `[84500,11149,697]` through five contractions. Seed 29
reaches `[83824,11106,697]` through seven contractions; geometry reaches
`[83486,11111,697]` through eight and wins by area despite five more transport tiles.
Seed 17 keeps the original `[86190,11189,697]` incumbent.

Seed 17 nevertheless executes **16 valid translating poses** on that large original,
eight per bank. F94 moves south one cell repeatedly, growing transport by two tiles
per action while area remains unchanged. These are native-validated, actual relative
facility movements and reroutes, not translation-frame artifacts or executor rejects.
All are worsening explorations, so the initial incumbent remains protected. Seed 29
also makes one valid worsening translation per bank on dense Heavy Xiranite without
improving its best score. Valid relocation capability is distinct from useful packing.

executor status/work, every candidate's native physical validity and original graph,

guard root balance, the intermediate-state cycle, deterministic schedule seeds and
exclusion of development lessons. No Rust, production operator or score contract

Retain both optimizer/RNG checkpoints, all selected episode traces/bests, the three
