## Interpretation and next difficulty

Additional learning was flat in this one execution. PPO improvement is not assumed monotonic,
and a plateau at this budget does not establish capacity limits or convergence. The current
fixture already has aligned facilities; repeated boundary-tail trimming is a narrow behavior.
The user's proposed inefficient but valid starting factory offers a separate difficulty axis:
longer transport paths and wider facility spacing can increase potential improvement, but can
also make sparse valid-return discovery harder. No new fixture or reward change was introduced
into this duration comparison. Its eventual performance must be reported separately.
