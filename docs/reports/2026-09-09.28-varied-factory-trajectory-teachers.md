# Varied complete-factory relocation teachers

All five admitted training factories have a measured, native-valid relocation path
that improves area after ordinary contractions are exhausted. This is teacher
collection, not a learned-policy result. It supplies varied intermediate states
for a controlled architecture comparison after reports 26/27 found no productive
relocation transfer.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.28-varied-factory-trajectory-teachers.md) freezes
six training requests at one medical/food unit per 28000, 11000 and 4500 ms, and six
future evaluation requests at 26000, 9000 and 4200 ms. Future evaluation has not been
constructed or opened. These are geometry/rate partitions within known families;
they are not disjoint recipe families. Previously opened bottling and transfer
roots remain outside training.

## Measured continuation paths

Freeze 256 first-action candidates per prepared root before outcomes: 192 diverse
pose actions and sixteen each of reroute, port, component and bridge release.
Each candidate gets eight selections, 30 seconds and 5000 route expansions per
selection. A valid first result, including a worse current layout, continues with
ordinary contractions. A contraction may improve current geometry while still
trailing the protected incumbent; collection continues in that case.

All 1280 trials complete in 268.878 seconds, with 1389 selected actions. There are
688 valid first actions and 592 executor rejections. Every valid trial reaches
ordinary contraction exhaustion; none reaches a time/horizon bound or continuation
failure. Eighty trajectories improve area, including 45 whose first action worsens
the score. All unsuccessful trials remain in the denominator.

remains protected throughout, and the final score wins by area despite six more
turns than preparation. Immediate-positive-only labeling would miss this path.

## Verification and decision

The independent collection audit replays all 1389 selected actions, checks full
original obligations and native physical rules, ordinary candidate membership,
stratum populations/quotas, status/work, current and incumbent scores and hashes,
relative facility movement, first-contraction continuation order, termination,
teacher minima and retained witnesses. Together with preparation, **1701 transitions
pass**. The recorded candidate lists and membership are verified; the JavaScript
audit does not independently reproduce Python's random-number generator.

Two native-validated before/after views are inspected. Food 23 visibly moves the
rightmost facility inward and reduces width from 57 to 53. Food 105 reduces height
from 227 to 223 while retaining all 105 facilities and long connected routes. Both
remain sparse; these are useful paths, not compact visual-quality success.

Verification passes: 49 native-backed Python tests, workspace check, 538 Rust tests
with two existing ignores, Python compilation and JavaScript syntax checks, actual
admission/import/preparation/collection/audit/view CLI runs, and tracked-file checks.
No Rust, native runtime, policy architecture, operator or game-data change occurs.
The artifact field `episode_seconds` in preparation rows and collection trials is
the configured episode limit; actual elapsed time is `summary.elapsed_seconds`
(and root/study `seconds`). Do not interpret that limit as measured runtime.

Keep all preparation traces, all 1280 trial traces, five prepared witnesses and
five teacher bests under ignored artifacts. Keep failed admission evidence. Remove
only confirmed duplicate progress and process output plus regenerable accepted
constructor HTML. The compact provenance manifest hashes retained inputs and
outputs, including the all-transition audit records.
