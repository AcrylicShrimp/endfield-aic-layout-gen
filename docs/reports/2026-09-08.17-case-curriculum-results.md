## Progression and exposed problem

All three policies advance through A level six and B level three, whose actual setup
prefixes are empty. The first checkpoints at which both original-root cases have two
consecutive passing eight-edit development assessments are:

| Seed | First simultaneous root mastery | Assessment A/B successes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 17 | 172,032 | 58/64, 63/64 |
| 29 | 163,840 | 62/64, 64/64 |
| 43 | 180,224 | 62/64, 64/64 |

A's earlier level-three block is crossed without changing model capacity. Continued
updates subsequently reduce root success for some policies. This supports investigating
retention and checkpoint selection before increasing model size; it does not identify
a unique cause of the regression. Different setup, rehearsal and training exposure
also prevent attributing the progress to one isolated intervention.

A bounded next experiment will select the **first** checkpoint satisfying joint root
mastery using development assessment only, then evaluate it on untouched sampling
seed banks. It must not choose whichever recorded root outcome looks best, retry
later checkpoints after seeing test failures, or relabel this failed final-checkpoint
experiment as passed. The next stage remains gated pending that result or another
explicitly evaluated intervention. No fundamental impossibility has been established.
