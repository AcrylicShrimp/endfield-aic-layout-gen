actor with waves of sixteen independent episodes; every assessment command is
independently replayed in the reference engine. Native acceptance, PPO rules and
policy architecture are unchanged.

Whole-run speedups are 3.05x and 3.93x. They are smaller than isolated actor speedups
because environment, serialization and independent acceptance work remains. These
are single short timing replications, not a statistical performance guarantee.
Peak CUDA allocations remain below 466 MiB. Later curriculum states can cost more.
No development or held-out policy outcome opens, and no model-quality comparison is
claimed from this cost pilot.

carry the actual sampled commands and frozen policy hash. Audits check every command's
domain membership, applied transition, reward, score and counters, reconstruct all
controller/exposure decisions and match checkpoint policy hashes. Numerical output
parity is tested separately; tiny sampling differences do not invalidate an actual
mechanically correct trajectory. Legacy assessment records require their frozen source.
