# Full-score original-root action learning

## Result

Teaching the best collected full-score action over the complete action domain learns
useful facility relocation on the original food/medical factories. It improves final
area performance over the preceding policy in this opened-family screen. One
untrained-family watered-cycle root also gains an improving relocation after seven
contractions. The equal-budget best-versus-measured pairwise arm does not reproduce
that result and remains below its parent.

This is a useful learning signal for broader data and fresh-size evaluation, not
production readiness. Copper gains no additional improvement, and neither Heavy
Xiranite start changes its best score. The requested original 1% target remains unmet.

## Comparison

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.13-full-score-action-learning.md) fixes both arms
before training. Reuse the independently replayed 597-action
[contrast collection](2026-09-09.12-full-factory-action-contrasts.md), unchanged native
acceptance, schema-three ordinary actions and the corrected depth-two/width-32
[trajectory checkpoint](2026-09-09.10-full-factory-trajectory-learning.md).

For each root, retain all completed improving actions tied for the minimum collected
lexicographic area/transport/turn score. Four training originals have one best pose
each; both 48-facility originals have four tied contractions. Five development roots
have teachers but receive no updates. The dense heavy root has no improving teacher
and remains in every evaluation denominator. Collected minima are not global optima.

Each arm gets 256 updates from the same parent weights with fresh Adam 0.0003,
gradient clip 0.5 and seed 17. Both use the same seeded, shuffled round-robin schedule
over the six training roots. Best-mass imitation minimizes negative log probability
mass of the tied best actions over the full ordinary domain. Best-pair ranking uses
one of those actions against a completed strictly worse collected action on the
same root. Its worse example can itself be a successful edit. Untried actions are
not directly supervised in that pairwise arm; they are not invalidity labels in
either arm. Route-work rejection still describes bounded execution utility.

The experiment completes in 145.933 seconds on CPU, including 512 total updates and
96 evaluation episodes/768 selections. Every episode receives eight actions, 30
seconds and 5000 route expansions per selection, with the same history mask, twelve
originals and two parent sampling banks. No episode is shortened. Cached observation
arrays use 20,284,376 bytes within the 128 MiB cap. Runtime kernels and model
parameters remain unchanged during each frozen evaluation.

Both final checkpoints retain their optimizer, Torch RNG and exact schedule protocol
hash. Parent weights are preserved. The two duplicate final progress checkpoints
were removed only after complete recursive equality checks.

## Teacher learning versus factory outcomes

Best-mass imitation raises training teacher top-one membership from 2/6 to 6/6;
best-pair reaches only 1/6. Mean probability mass on the tied training targets moves
from 0.2501 to 0.9189 for imitation and 0.1499 for pairwise learning. All methods
remain 0/5 for the development roots' original-state teacher top-one membership.
The later cycle relocation is therefore an episode result, not a reproduced
original-state development teacher.

Mean first/last-32-update loss is 6.641/0.150 for imitation and 1.229/0.150 for
pairwise learning. Maximum pre-clipping gradient norms are 229.26 and 233.39;
all values are finite. Similar final scalar losses do not imply similar behavior.

At T=1, best-mass means are 14.84% food/medical, 15.63% copper and 41.07% grass,
versus parent 3.74%, 15.63% and 32.14%. Best-pair means are 2.87%, 15.63% and
32.14%. Heavy remains unchanged. Parent, geometry and binary controls are reused
from their recorded runs with source/checkpoint hashes and matching planned budgets;

Using complete lexicographic final scores, imitation has 12 wins/12 ties/no losses
against its T=1 parent and 10 wins/14 ties/no losses against its T=0.05 parent.
Against geometry it has 6 wins/5 ties/13 losses at T=1 and 10 wins/6 ties/8 losses
at T=0.05. The latter comprise eight training-family wins, two lower-rate grass
wins, four copper losses, two higher-rate grass losses and two training losses.
Pairwise learning has no win against its parent or geometry at either temperature.
The source artifacts retain complete paired and action-kind tables.

These are one training seed and two action-sampling seeds, with adaptive opened
development families. Two copper rates share geometry. Repeated identical moves
across sampling seeds are not independent learning replications. The result does
not yet establish broad relocation generalization or superior packing on new sizes.

## Replayed relocation capability and geometry

Imitation selects 22 poses at each temperature, with eight improving poses at T=1
and ten at T=0.05. Its T=1 improvements comprise eight poses and 93 contractions;
T=0.05 has ten poses and 122 contractions. Pairwise learning gets one improving
training pose at T=1 and none at T=0.05. No standalone reroute improves in these arms.
The improving poses themselves reconnect their affected physical networks.

An independent JS audit replays all eight actions of **every episode containing
an improving pose/reroute**: 19 episodes and 152 actions. It checks ordinary action
membership, execution status/work, original-graph validity at each candidate,
current/incumbent full scores, actual facility movement and final retained geometry.
All transitions match, including rejected or exploratory steps surrounding the
successful moves. Separately, every one of the 96 retained bests passes original
coverage and independent area/transport/terminal-aware-turn measurement. Full action
replay is not claimed for the remaining 77 episodes.

Three complete before/after views for best-mass T=0.05, seed 1310000, were native
validated and inspected:

- Medical, 16 facilities: `[3960,345,60]` at 55x72 becomes `[2832,412,68]` at 48x59.
  The southern facility moves inward and reconnects; subsequent contractions narrow
  the layout. Wiring grows while area improves, as required by score priority.
- Food, 18 facilities: `[5610,419,76]` at 66x85 becomes `[4260,407,78]` at 60x71.
  The lower facility relocates into a gap, with the original obligations preserved.
- Lower-rate watered grass, development: seven contractions reach `[147,35,4]`;
  action eight moves a facility and reconnects the cycle, reaching `[126,29,4]`.
  From the original `[294,49,4]`, dimensions change from 14x21 to 7x18. Both banks
  reproduce this sequence, which beats the previous geometry result on this root.

Food and medical still contain large gaps, long trunks and sparse facility banks.
Grass still has a long internal loop. These figures support valid compression,
not polished final-factory visual quality.

Dense Heavy Xiranite remains `[2132,374,148]`, 41x52 and **30.5347% empty XY**;
the sparse original remains `[12900,2401,268]`. All valid incumbents remain protected.
The work follows transactional improvement on complete factories under the
[constructive planner design](../designs/2026-09-02.00-constructive-layout-planner.md);
it introduces no production fallback or feasibility proof.

## Verification and next decision

All 36 full-factory Python tests pass, including full-score priority despite longer
wiring, exact teacher ties, family/missing-evidence exclusions and target-mass
gradient behavior. Workspace check and 536 Rust tests pass with two existing
ignores. The independent audit checks 512 update memberships, 96 bests and the
152-action capability replay. Worktree/index artifact checks pass before commit.
Generated traces, witnesses and three PNG/SVG comparisons remain ignored, identified
by the compact provenance manifest and per-episode audit hashes.

The best-mass objective now supplies an actual learned relocation benefit on complete
originals. Retain it as a candidate for fresh geometries, more varied full-score
teachers and larger factory sizes, with independent training replication before
stronger claims. Do not deepen the losing pairwise arm or freeze the model design
from this narrow screen. Larger 65–128-facility coverage, unopened diverse evaluation,
bounded production CPU CLI integration and the original Heavy Xiranite 1%/visual
acceptance remain incomplete. No fundamental blocker has been established.
