## Scope and interpretation

The [stage contract](../designs/2026-09-09.38-input-contract-candidate-screen.md)
compares twelve input/action contracts under a fixed learning recipe. Each
candidate starts from two matched random parameter initializations and receives
32 optimizer updates. One update uses four new 32-action trajectories under frozen
weights: 128 episodes and 4096 training actions per candidate/seed. Evaluation
uses frozen checkpoints at updates 0, 16 and 32, eight intact resets and two
sampling seed banks. The four 48-facility controls were used in earlier studies;
they are held out of this training, not untouched prospective data.

The primary comparison is each checkpoint against its own initialization on the
same evaluation cases and sampling seeds. Positive area delta means an increased
percentage of reset area removed. Full-score wins/ties/losses compare area, then
physical transport tiles, then turns; they can therefore differ from area-only
counts. Equal sampling seeds do not force identical action trajectories after
input or policy distributions differ.

Keep initial-policy area reduction separate from learned additional reduction.
Useful factory edits can occur before any weight update: the policy selects
implemented operations whose physical execution and validation are provided by
the native engine. Relative rank among four final episode incumbents supplies
positive and negative learning signals when their scores differ. Its group mean
is zero by construction. Signal availability alone does not establish learned
improvement, sustained progress or convergence.

All candidates allocate 50,977 parameters with shared current/best encoder
weights. Disabled observation branches have no effective input contribution;
equal allocated parameters do not equalize active capacity. The two no-restore
arms change the allowed action set as well as the observation comparison. Their
validated terminal best record is still retained externally. Current-best-2 is
an input-contract control, not an exact copy of an earlier model architecture.

the intact resets. Use fresh screen and view output paths when repeating a run.
The smoke switch deliberately changes exposure and cases and must be omitted
from the learning screen.

  --training artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-on-policy-learning \
  --config tools/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-input-contract-study.json \
  --output artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-input-contract-screen

  --screen artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-input-contract-screen
