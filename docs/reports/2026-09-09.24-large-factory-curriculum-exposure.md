# Learned relocation and compression on the complete large factory

## Result

Both adapted policies now execute the known **three-facility relocation, rerouting
and three contractions** on the complete contraction-exhausted 116-facility factory.
All four executions, two adaptation schedules times two sampling banks, reproduce
the four-action teacher prefix and retain `[57456,9868,697]` at 216x266. Relative to
the 219x266 input `[58254,9925,697]`, this is **1.370% less area and 57 fewer transport
tiles**, with unchanged turns. Parent and geometry controls do not improve this start.

On the uncompressed original, both models use eight contractions to reach the same
247x338 area as geometry with **81–85 fewer transport tiles**. This fixes the prior
slice's large-state behavior within the same inference limits. It is learning on
opened training-family examples, not unseen-factory generalization. Smaller-factory
retention is mixed, so neither checkpoint is a general production replacement.
