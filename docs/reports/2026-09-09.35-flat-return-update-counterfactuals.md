# Entropy scale, inherited Adam state and actual policy movement

## Finding

The two flat-return warm updates from report 34 reproduce exactly, including every
saved policy and Adam tensor. Single-step controls establish that reducing the
entropy coefficient alone does not reliably control movement here. At the first
update, dividing that coefficient by 100 retains most of the distribution shift.
At group 38, even a zero current gradient leaves substantial movement from
inherited Adam state. Reducing learning rate by ten reduces movement approximately
tenfold at both transitions. These are causal mechanical results for two fixed
updates; they do not establish better final factory quality.

## Registered comparison

Design 35 and the JSON configuration preceded execution. All branches begin from
identical pre-update weights and optimizer snapshots for their group. Eight
audited episodes provide 256 reconstructed actions; every original sampling
distribution, observation and memory mask matches exactly. Both groups have
exactly zero task-reward gradients. Recomputed gradients therefore contain only
the original entropy contribution.

## Interpretation and next experiment

Group 0 begins with empty Adam state. Its reset branch equals the baseline, and
zero current gradients eliminate all movement. The entropy-scale controls confirm
the earlier saved-moment explanation: Adam normalization largely cancels these
gradient-scale reductions. Changing the learning rate directly changes the
parameter step; the policy response is approximately proportional at these tested
scales, not guaranteed linear in general.

Group 38 has inherited first and second moments. Its zero-current-gradient branch
still moves by 21.7758% TV. This is not equivalent to skipping: Adam advances its
step, decays both moments and applies a nonzero parameter displacement. Skipping
preserves both policy and optimizer exactly. Do not subtract the TV values to
assign a percentage of blame to entropy: the optimizer and policy are nonlinear,
and inherited moments contain the history of both task and entropy gradients.
Clearing Adam while retaining entropy actually increases the measured movement
to 51.2323%; clearing state is not supported as a standalone stability fix.

The next fresh on-policy comparison should cross unchanged versus one-tenth
learning rate with ordinary versus skipped flat-return updates, retaining the
original entropy coefficient, action horizon, group size, sampling rules and
lexicographic reward. Start all arms from the same frozen warm policy and empty
Adam state, use equal complete reset cycles and register evaluation before running.
Collect fresh experience independently for every diverged policy. This isolates
two supported control variables without asserting that small movement is itself
learning. Measure original/prepared reward coverage, fixed-input movement,
physical pose/reroute improvements and initial/terminal factory scores.

This decision does not establish adequate experience volume, a sufficient horizon,
good credit assignment or shared-policy representability. Those remain potential
limits. The original Heavy Xiranite target, sustained learned improvement and
bounded production CLI remain unfinished. The parallel collector's timed matrix
is still pending; its throughput result can inform the new experiment's resources.
