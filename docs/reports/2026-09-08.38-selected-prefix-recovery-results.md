## Exposure and the horizon comparison

Audited metadata shows 196608 training commands per case for both widths, or 12288
sixteen-command episodes. Width-two battery gap-zero rotations zero and two each
received 7104 episodes starting from the original root. The corresponding width-one
counts are 7488 and 7008. All cases reached curriculum level nine, the original root.
These failures are not explained by never training the original case. This does not
prove that exposure was sufficient, diverse or well optimized.

The 16/128 comparison has a material conditioning difference. In
`crates/aic-pilot-native/src/observation.rs`, the first global feature is
`(horizon - steps) / horizon`; the reference observation agrees. At step one it is
`15/16` in a sixteen-command episode and `127/128` in a 128-command episode. These
actors trained with horizon 16. Therefore the longer evaluation does not simply
continue the same first sixteen sampled commands. Even with paired random seeds,
different logits may change actions before step sixteen.

The lower width-two root count at horizon 128 does not violate incumbent retention:
the paired runs are different trajectories. It is also not proof that the time
feature causes the weakness. A future causal comparison must hold the physical
state and sampling fixed while explicitly varying time conditioning, or compare
declared restart schedules with separately accounted budgets. Do not relabel these
results as a pure test of additional search time or silently alter the active study.

No new training or data expansion follows automatically. Complete the remaining
declared streams and inspect their full curves. If the weakness persists, time
conditioning and recovery training distribution are bounded diagnostic candidates;
larger dataset/model size and from-scratch generation remain unproven interventions.
