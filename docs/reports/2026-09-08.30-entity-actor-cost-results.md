# Batched entity inference reduces the measured actor cost

All four width/case cells pass frozen-observation output parity. Policy and BN hashes
remain unchanged. CPU thread adjustment alone does not resolve serial evaluation cost;
batching sixteen observations gives the material improvement. The probe includes input
preparation, completed forward/host transfer and sampling, but excludes native environment
work, capture, parity checks and independent audit. It is not an end-to-end speedup claim.

| Width / facility count | CPU 16 threads, single | CPU batch 16 | CUDA batch 16 | CUDA actor speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 / 2 | 0.2314 s | 0.0621 s | 0.0452 s | 5.12x |
| 1 / 4 | 0.3337 s | 0.0806 s | 0.0495 s | 6.74x |
| 2 / 2 | 0.3243 s | 0.0882 s | 0.0459 s | 7.07x |
| 2 / 4 | 0.4562 s | 0.1138 s | 0.0500 s | 9.12x |

Each timing is the median of three measured repeats over 512 identical observations,
with one warmup and alternating order. Maximum absolute real-candidate logit/value
error across all tested CPU-thread/batch and CUDA-batch combinations is 7.511e-6,
within the declared ordinary tolerance. This does not require identical subsequent
sampled trajectories after floating changes. Only training cases are captured.
