# Learning from original compression trajectories

## Outcome

Actual original-root compression paths now feed a family-aware learning comparison.
Eighty recorded improving steps replay from ten complete factories. The policy
trains on the 48 food/medical steps only; the 32 copper/watered-cycle steps remain
development labels. Additional original and dense Heavy Xiranite starts contribute
no lessons. Independent audit replays all teachers, checks their original episode
context and validates every retained best from 96 evaluation episodes.

The shallow learned policy improves sampled food/medical results over its untrained
initialization, with mixed results on the two new development families. It remains
well below the existing geometry selector. No method reduces Heavy Xiranite area.
The dense original target remains `[2132,374,148]`, 41x52 and **30.5347% empty XY**;
the requested 1% result and production CLI release remain unachieved.

## Protocol

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.10-full-factory-trajectory-learning.md) and
`tools/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-trajectory-learning.json` declare all source
artifacts, families, additional starts, update count and bounds. The preceding
[admission screen](2026-09-09.09-full-factory-family-admission.md) supplies the
ten original requests and validated trajectories. Failed construction requests
cannot become lessons, and related families cannot cross training/development.
These are opened development families, not a sealed benchmark. The two copper
rates share geometry and are not independent topology samples.

The same schema-three depth-two width-32 model is initialized with seed 17 and
updated 256 times using cross entropy, Adam at 0.0003 and gradient clipping 0.5.
All 28 parameter tensors change. The final declared update supplies the learned
rollouts; development results do not select a checkpoint. Both initial and final
weights are retained, with final optimizer/RNG state and the exact lesson/update log.

Each method runs two predetermined sampling seeds, 1310000 and 1320000, on twelve
original starts. Methods are untrained, learned, uniform and existing geometry
selection. Every episode receives eight selections, 30 seconds, 5000 route expansions
per selected edit, and the same history mask. Scores retain area, physical transport
tiles, then terminal-aware turns. There is no learned feasibility pruning. All 96
episodes finish their eight selections, totaling 768 ordinary physical edits.

The full corrected screen takes 168.230 seconds on this host. Observation array
cache usage peaks at 119,310,504 bytes under its declared 128 MiB cap; that is not
a process RSS measurement. Lesson admission, observations and training are charged
separately from the equal evaluation episode limits. No broad CPU deadline guarantee
or stable performance estimate follows from this one-seed screen.

## Teacher imitation and original-root evaluation

Greedy exact-teacher accuracy increases from 0/48 to 36/48 on training lessons and
from 0/32 to 26/32 on development lessons. Mean probability of the recorded teacher
choice increases from 0.000421 to 0.563604 and from 0.005585 to 0.605870 respectively.
These are labels on real intermediate compression states, not final-factory quality.

Mean area reduction from each original start, averaged within each family:

| Family | Episodes per method | Untrained | Learned | Geometry |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Food/medical, training family | 12 | 1.21% | 3.74% | 10.52% |
| Copper liquid, development | 4 | 15.00% | 15.63% | 25.00% |
| Watered grass, development | 4 | 27.17% | 32.14% | 52.05% |
| Heavy Xiranite, development | 4 | 0% | 0% | 0% |

Uniform sampling has the same area means as the untrained model for these seeds;
their complete action outcomes are not all identical. Food/medical area improves
in 9/12 learned episodes versus 4/12 untrained and 12/12 geometry episodes. All four
copper and all four grass episodes improve area under every method.

Paired complete lexicographic score comparisons for learned versus untrained are
8 wins / 1 tie / 3 losses for food/medical, 2 / 0 / 2 for copper, 2 / 0 / 2 for grass,
and 0 / 3 / 1 for Heavy Xiranite. Learned loses all twenty new-family comparisons
against geometry and ties the four heavy comparisons. These results support a
limited selection-learning effect, not reliable family generalization or parity
with the current baseline.

The sparse original Heavy Xiranite start has area 12,900. Untrained and uniform
each find `[12900,2384,264]` in one sampling bank, reducing transport and turns from
`[12900,2401,268]`. Learned retains the original score there. Every method retains
`[2132,374,148]` on the dense incumbent. Neither sparse wiring improvement nor
teacher imitation satisfies the original target's area or visual requirements.

## Remaining selection problem

The learned policy selects 115 contractions, 61 insertions, nine port changes,
three reroutes, two component edits and two poses across its 192 choices. Its
native results are 74 improvements, 110 accepted explorations, three equal results
and five executor rejections. Validity improves relative to the untrained policy's
94 executor rejections, but many valid selections expand or undo compression.

Geometry selects 160 contractions on the new roots, all improving, and 32 rejected
poses on the two heavy starts. Training on contraction examples changes the model's
preferences; it has not learned to spend the budget as effectively as this simple
baseline. All eighty admitted teacher actions are contractions. This study therefore
does not supply new-family pose, port or pipe-rerouting demonstrations, even though
their complete production semantics remain present in the input and validator.

Three full native-validated learned before/after views are retained for the 48-facility
medical root, internal-pipe copper root and watered cycle. Each shows the better of
the two declared learned sampling seeds, explicitly labeled as a display selection.
The medical best is `[18496,2114,262]`, copper `[520,64,15]`, and watered grass
`[203,43,4]`. Long gaps and trunks remain; these are weaker compressions than the
geometry teacher. They do not establish compact or visually polished factories.

## Context correction

The first completed comparison reopened each stored intermediate witness as a new
episode. Native admission correctly validated it, but the observation's original
score baseline reset to the current score. Consequently the training ratios were
all one while real trajectories retained their original baseline.

The corrected lesson schema stores original episode initial/best metrics and the
float32 context actually observed during replay. Reopening a witness now restores
that context, checks current metrics, and requires exact context equality. The
independent JS audit reconstructs those ratios again. A regression test covers
an 80/100 area ratio becoming 0.8 rather than resetting to one.

The corrected full run reproduces all 96 best metrics from the superseded run,
although its learned parameters/probabilities differ. The correction is necessary
for trajectory fidelity; it does not explain away the weak selection results.
Only corrected-context checkpoints and records support this report's main claims.
The superseded screen source remains local to identify the defect.
