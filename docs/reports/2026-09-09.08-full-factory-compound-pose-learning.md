# Full-factory compound poses and learned inverse transfer

Status: implementation, complete learning screen, independent audit and scoped
replay verified. Compound edits are model-connected, but broad learned area benefit
and learned compound-inverse execution are not established. Stages four/five and the
original <=1% empty-XY target remain open. The preceding goal turn made progress.

## Implemented action and observation contract

Implementation `c63b14f` follows the
[declared contract](../designs/2026-09-09.06-full-factory-compound-poses.md).
Executor RPC/description schema 3 replaces separate translation and rotation with
`pose(poses: [{member,x,y,rotation}])`. All members describe their final geometry
in one transaction. Up to sixteen facilities and four distinct translation vectors
use the existing dedicated route repair and complete native acceptance. Old actions,
descriptions and schema-two weights are explicitly rejected; historical reports must
use their implementation commits. Complete-witness native acceptance stays schema 1.

This removes separate model/executor paths for translating groups and rotating a
single facility. Catalog geometry, group destinations and orientations share one
contract. The schema-three model encodes each member's source graph state, final
spatial sample and ten normalized pose features *before* pooling. Tests distinguish
member order invariance from source/destination reassignment and verify gradients
through every member, destination and pose feature. Rates and the entire physical
network scene remain visible; identity strings address relationships only.

Compound candidates add rigid facility-body/port transforms of physical connected
pairs, SCCs/terminal closures and aligned connected runs of two to four facilities.
They do not rigidly rotate all internal routes or logistics components. Current
junction ownership determines carried geometry and the router repairs the remainder.
The finite default domain uses 128 groups, 256 origins per group/angle, eight admitted
poses per stream and 512 movement preflights shared round-robin. Current origin,
local shifts, empty-rectangle corners/insets and coprime-stride destinations are
geometry proposals, not ranked successor scores. Report every static rejection and
truncation. Selected actions use pipe-first routing, at most 5000 shared expansions
and two new crossings. Enumeration does not run A* or complete successor validation.

Compare with the constructive planner design's iterative improvement contract:
all explored children are complete validated factories, exported bests preserve
area/physical-transport/turn priority, and production construction remains unchanged.
No global exact solver, whole-factory facility-only fallback, external-anchor
relaxation, port-repair fallback or game-dimension limit is introduced.

## Decision after this slice

The missing model-connected pose contract is resolved, and deterministic observation
cost now fits the declared cell allowance. Do not keep describing either as an absent
capability. The finite default still omits the exact historically successful block
destination, and the new inverse lessons do not establish original-root compression
or learned compound selection. Next work should join actual validated compression
trajectories with curriculum coverage, and construct new recipe-family pipe/cycle
data with family-level splits and recorded construction attrition. Selected failures
and rank ties can inform feasibility/utility learning hypotheses; do not silently
prune on predictions or repeat the same duration/seed gate as a substitute for new
capability or data. Production CPU CLI integration and the complete release suite
remain required. No fundamental contradiction or goal-level impossibility is proved.
