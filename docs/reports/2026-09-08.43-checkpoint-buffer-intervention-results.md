# Checkpoint weight and buffer intervention results

Status: complete training-only diagnostic under the declared design 32. No new
training or held-out outcomes. Frozen width-one seed-43 checkpoints at 1179648
and 1212416 commands bracket the selected regression (32 training updates).

Replacing normalization buffers alone does not explain this regression. Keeping
old weights with new buffers preserves nearly all successes, whereas keeping new
weights with old buffers makes performance substantially worse. This implicates
weight changes or their interaction with normalization, without identifying an
individual faulty update or proving the cause of every later regression.
