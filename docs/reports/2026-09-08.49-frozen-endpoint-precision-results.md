## Consequence

The earlier three-pair experiment showed reduced update movement and better worst
current-level assessments at lower rate. This diagnosis confirms a remaining
root-level tradeoff in its failing pair, so a permanent learning-rate reduction
is not an established repair. Do not repeat the same selection bank until it passes
or promote the lower rate on pooled counts alone. Next work should examine the
retained affected-case trajectories and training gradients/objectives to explain
why current-level stability and original-root performance separate, before another
full capacity matrix. No fundamental impossibility is established. Stages 4/5 and
the original 59-facility acceptance remain incomplete.
