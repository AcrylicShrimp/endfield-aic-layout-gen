## Next decision and remaining goal

The one-step screen still provides only secondary-objective gains. Before adapting
the policy around these alone, test short ordinary continuations from the 119 valid
non-insertion/non-restore candidates. A valid move or reroute may expose a new strip
even when its immediate area is unchanged. Because the source has zero contractions,
any newly offered contraction can be attributed to that first edit. Keep this as a
separate bounded trajectory study with explicit accounting and full graph guards.

Retain preparation trace/input, contrast outcomes and selections, valid incumbents,
selected per-stratum candidates and the three inspected views under ignored artifacts.
There are no obsolete intermediate candidate files or progress-row duplicates in
this slice. The gzip preparation copy remains an explicit input to its retained view.
No model or optimizer checkpoint is created, overwritten or deleted.
