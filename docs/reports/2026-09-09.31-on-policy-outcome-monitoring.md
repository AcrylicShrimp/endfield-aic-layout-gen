# Observe outcomes during on-policy factory learning

## Status and implementation

The user requested outcome monitoring while report 30's training was already
running. The read-only monitor is implemented, its three focused tests pass, and
its actual PNG/SVG output has been inspected. It stopped successfully when training
published a partial report at the imitation-warm arm guard. The final snapshot has
115 completed groups, 460 episodes in those groups and three unapplied episodes.
Independent physical replay passes all 463 episodes and 14816 actions, including
the three unapplied episodes. Frozen policy evaluation completed 175 episodes and
passes independent replay of all 5600 actions; report 30 records its final
initial/terminal comparisons, which are mixed for both arms. The
observations below are training measurements, not frozen policy comparisons.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.31-on-policy-outcome-monitoring.md) separates
both initializations and all ten fixed reset factories. Each point contains the
mean and observed minimum/maximum of four completed episodes, with the horizontal
coordinate equal to the number of updates **before** sampling. Group zero therefore
describes the initial policy. The layout resets for each episode; the curve does
not describe one factory repeatedly improved across training updates.

Each update uses only four 32-action episodes. Random initialization completed
64 updates (8192 training actions), and imitation-warm completed 51 (6528 training
actions). With ten reset cases, each plotted case has only 20–28 episodes per arm.
These curves therefore describe a small experience budget, not an established
learning ceiling. See the [experience-budget interpretation](2026-09-09.30-on-policy-factory-return-learning.md#experience-budget-and-limits-of-interpretation)
for the distinction between sample count, wall time and episode horizon.

Only complete groups enter the curves. The monitor checks episode membership,
case, attempts and terminal scores, and rejects mismatched or duplicate records.
Its balanced summary includes a cycle only after all ten reset cases have one
completed group. Original and contraction-exhausted starts retain separate means.
The observed four-episode range is not a confidence interval, and different seeds
across visits prevent a paired-seed interpretation of first/latest differences.

The watch command reads progress/final/partial reports and atomically replaces
`snapshot.json`, `curves.png` and `curves.svg` under ignored artifacts. It retries
incomplete writes and stops when a final or partial training report appears. It
performs no inference, environment actions, teacher queries or future admission.
It records source identities and refresh duration. The final refresh at 115
completed groups took 0.594 seconds; this is one render measurement, not a total
overhead or process-memory measurement. Its later introduction is explicitly part
of the training measurement conditions.

## Recorded training observations

The random arm has six complete ten-case cycles before its 64-update endpoint.
Its mean area reductions across those cycles are 1.741%, 1.852%, 1.206%, 0.995%,
1.734% and 1.939%. This fluctuating sequence alone does not establish an improving
or impossible RL process. Four remaining update groups are not a seventh balanced
cycle.

The five complete imitation-warm cycles have the following area reductions
relative to each episode's fixed reset. Each row equally weights the ten cases.

| 2 | 10.126% | 19.886% | 0.367% |
| 3 | 8.105% | 13.448% | 2.762% |
| 4 | 9.830% | 17.677% | 1.984% |
| 5 | 11.233% | 20.712% | 1.754% |

The warm arm's remaining completed group and three unapplied episodes do not make
a sixth balanced cycle. Its 51-update terminal policy is identified under the
[budget-termination amendment](../designs/2026-09-09.32-budget-ended-on-policy-evaluation.md);
the originally planned 64 warm updates did not complete.

Case-specific movement is mixed. Prepared Food 23 has mean area reductions of
0%, 0% and 10.088% at pre-update counts 0, 10 and 20, then 8.772% at count 30.
Original Food 48 changes from 19.444% at count 11 to 2.593% at count 21, then
16.111% at count 31. These visits use different policies and changing seeds.
Neither a one-visit decline nor a rebound is
evidence of monotonic convergence or statistically established policy benefit.

Recorded pose-action improvements are also retained, separately from contractions
and reroutes. These are execution status counts. The completed independent replay
audit establishes actual facility translation/rotation relative to canonical frame
changes and validates the complete factories; report 30 records its event counts.
Positive episode returns
alone do not establish an individual action's causal contribution.

The different starting weights make random-versus-warm levels unsuitable as a
measure of the effect of RL. Each terminal policy must still be compared with its own
initial policy and geometry under the frozen evaluation conditions. Heavy
Xiranite's at-most-1% empty-XY/visual target remains unfulfilled.

indexing, incomplete-group exclusion and complete-cycle aggregation; and exact
