This compares learning configurations: PPO clipping, a critic, GAE, advantage
standardization and multiple optimization epochs change together. It does not
isolate clipping. The actor still has partial state information. The critic's
richer pre-action current/best geometry, commodity graph, remaining budget and
allowed-candidate summaries are explicit. They do not change actor inputs or
native factory proposals. Actor initialization precedes critic initialization
and exactly matches the earlier untrained checkpoints.

The separate critic predicts remaining fractional area reduction. Valid temporary
worsening may continue, with the best witness retained. Reward is zero until a
new best area is achieved; restoring an old record yields no recovery bonus.
GAE uses gamma 1, lambda 0.95 and zero terminal bootstrap per finite episode.
Frozen value targets are old value plus unnormalized GAE. Advantages are
standardized once over the complete current rollout batch.

PPO epsilon is 0.2, four shuffled epochs and minibatches of 64 selected actions.
Both separate Adam optimizers use learning rate 0.0003 and gradient-norm limit
0.5; actor entropy coefficient remains 0.001. The critic minimizes half mean
squared error. Accumulate each minibatch with fixed weights and apply one
optimizer step afterward. A dedicated saved RNG controls shuffling.

Collection and optimization use CPU, with one Torch thread per process. The
coordinator processes variable-shaped observations sequentially inside each
minibatch to bound live autograd memory. It also executes every update again for
online reconstruction. This study measures that implementation's wall time; it
does not measure GPU throughput or establish an efficient PPO training pipeline.

Recompute exactly the original masked, temperature-adjusted and kind-balanced
action distribution. Before optimization, every stored behavior log probability,
distribution hash and critic value must match a fresh forward pass. All initial
probability ratios are one. Measured ratios may leave [0.8, 1.2]: clipping limits
the surrogate objective, not the resulting probability ratio. After each epoch,
measure whole-batch mean `ratio - 1 - log(ratio)` and stop further epochs if this
sampled KL estimate exceeds 0.02.

## Confirmation results

Each evaluation uses 16 fixed factories with 32 edit selections per factory.
Area reduction is the arithmetic mean of each factory's fractional reduction
from its own starting layout, including facilities and transport geometry.
The untrained policy also gets 32 selections and retains its best witness.

| Seed | Completed batches | Untrained reduction | Final reduction | Gain over untrained | Final wins/ties/losses |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 17 | 32 | 14.79% | 53.35% | 38.56 pp | 16/0/0 |
| 29 | 16 | 14.39% | 55.17% | 40.77 pp | 16/0/0 |

Seed 17's intermediate reductions were 21.22%, 46.13% and 51.92% at
batches 8, 16 and 24. Seed 29 reached 29.20% at batch 8. Both seeds satisfy
the user's confirmation criterion of visible learning improvement. Different
seed-specific factory banks and unequal training budgets preclude interpreting
the final seed ordering as a controlled comparison of learning speed.

The run committed 48 batches and 48,288 training selections: 1,509 admitted
factory attempts out of 1,536, with 27 constructor rejections. It performed
3,052 minibatch steps per optimizer; every batch completed four epochs without
the KL stop triggering. Recorded collection took 1,414.87 seconds and
optimization plus its duplicate reconstruction took 4,359.21 seconds. The whole
confirmation took about 108.7 minutes including evaluation and other checks.
The next group began collecting briefly after the final evaluation; its
uncommitted partial output is retained and excluded from all training totals.

The descriptive supplement confirmed the corresponding direct-study request,
admission and initial geometry for every committed factory attempt. On the
last training batch, behavior-critic explained variance against realized
remaining return was 0.9835 for seed 17 and 0.9455 for seed 29; mean squared
errors were 0.000534 and 0.001766. These changing-distribution training metrics
are not an independent critic generalization measurement.

Most measured final reward came from contraction: 51.68 of 53.35 percentage
points for seed 17 and 51.50 of 55.17 for seed 29. Their final evaluations
selected contraction 435/512 and 390/512 times. Across training, 1,423 and
1,071 temporary-worsening actions preceded a later record; 591 and 492 had
positive standardized GAE. Those counts establish that such trajectories and
credit exist, not that detours caused the improvement. A fixed contraction
heuristic was not evaluated in this study.

The stop supervisor verified the final evaluation and checkpoint identities
before requesting shutdown. All workers exited. The original runner reports
`incomplete` because it was interrupted; the separate confirmation reports
`complete-user-shortened` in its curve and records exact 32/16 limits. Existing
online validation and final checkpoint hashes passed finalization. The final
curve was rendered and visually inspected.
