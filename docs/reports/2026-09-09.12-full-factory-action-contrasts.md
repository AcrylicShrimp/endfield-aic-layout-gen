# Original-root action contrasts and pairwise learning

## Result

The new collector finds real facility-movement and rerouting improvements on complete
original factories. Binary improving-versus-non-improving ranking learns some of those
pair preferences but regresses final area performance. Keep the preceding policy and
validated incumbents as references; this checkpoint is experimental evidence, not a
promoted improver. No Heavy Xiranite area improvement or final 1% result is found.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.12-full-factory-action-contrasts.md) fixes twelve
original starts, family splits, a bounded action shortlist and 256 updates before
training. All collection actions use the ordinary schema-three domain and existing
pipe-first executor. No runtime model, action vocabulary, acceptance rule, production
command or original request changes. Transactions retain complete native-validated
incumbents with area/transport/turn score priority, consistent with the constructive
planner policy and its [design](../designs/2026-09-02.00-constructive-layout-planner.md).

## Collection and label meaning

All twelve shortlists complete in 14.521 seconds: 597 selections, including 152
strict native improvements. An independent audit re-executes **every selected action**,
checks offered-action membership and full scores, validates candidates against their
original graph, and compares every stored improving witness to replayed geometry.

| Split | Selections | Contraction positives | Pose positives | Port positives | Reroute positives |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Food/medical training | 312 | 24 | 21 | 7 | 1 |
| Opened development | 285 | 16 | 77 | 6 | 0 |

Nineteen training poses and 73 development poses reduce area. The remaining poses,
all ports and the single reroute first improve transport count. All positive
contractions reduce area. Development pose positives include 43 copper actions,
33 watered-cycle actions and one sparse-heavy action. The dense 374-tile heavy
incumbent has no improving action in its 44-selection shortlist.

The binary label describes the outcome of this **bounded execution**. There are 116
route-work rejections (95 training, 21 development) among completed non-improvements;
these remain negatives for the 5000-expansion execution utility. They do not certify
illegal geometry or impossibility with more route work. Wall-clock-late, native
budget-exhausted and untried actions are excluded from ranking; this collection has
no wall-clock-late/native-budget-exhausted outcomes. Rejected proposals remain in
the collection denominator. No failed route becomes a successful training label.

Full-score best collected single actions already provide a more specific teacher:
four of six training originals prefer a pose, while both 48-facility originals prefer
a contraction. The 16-facility medical pose changes `[3960,345,60]` to
`[3245,419,68]`, accepting more wiring for lower area. A lower-rate watered-cycle
pose changes `[294,49,4]` to `[168,44,10]`. These are teacher capabilities, not actions
successfully reproduced by the new learned policy.

## Training and original-factory outcomes

Start from the corrected [trajectory checkpoint](2026-09-09.10-full-factory-trajectory-learning.md).
Use the unchanged depth-two, width-32 policy on CPU, fresh Adam at 0.0003, gradient
clip 0.5 and seed 17. Balance the four positive action kinds at 64 updates each;
within each kind sample a root, positive and completed negative on that same root.
This repeats the single training reroute positive 64 times. The explicit schedule
uses 49 distinct positives and 162 distinct negatives, all from training roots.
Development observations receive no gradient updates. Cached arrays occupy
20,284,376 bytes within the declared 128 MiB cap.

Mean loss falls from 1.745 in the first 32 updates to 0.584 in the last 32. The
largest pre-clipping gradient norm is 148.89; all recorded values are finite.
Among all measured same-root positive/negative pairs, training pose ordering improves
from 456/861 to 535/861 and reroute from 17/46 to 46/46. Development pose ordering
improves from 491/1885 to 725/1885. Training contraction ordering declines from
1019/1036 to 995/1036. These pair counts are correlated and do not measure independent
generalization trials or episode quality.

Evaluate both T=1 and T=0.05 on the same twelve originals, two parent sampling banks,
eight actions, 30 seconds and 5000 route expansions per action. The run, including
training, takes 83.585 seconds and completes 48 episodes/384 selections with no
shortened episodes. All bests pass independent original-graph coverage and metric
checks. Parent controls below are explicitly reused from trajectory learning and
[frozen decoding](2026-09-09.11-full-factory-decoding.md).

| Family | Parent T=1 | Ranked T=1 | Parent T=0.05 | Ranked T=0.05 | Geometry |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Food/medical, training | 3.74% | 0.17% | 7.20% | 0.89% | 10.52% |
| Copper liquid, development | 15.63% | 4.69% | 18.75% | 3.13% | 25.00% |
| Watered grass, development | 32.14% | 21.43% | 50.00% | 42.86% | 52.05% |
| Heavy Xiranite, development | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% |

Values are mean original-area reductions. Each temperature has twelve food/medical
episodes and four for each other family. Against its matched parent, ranked T=1
has 3 wins/6 ties/15 losses; ranked T=0.05 has 2 wins/8 ties/14 losses, using complete
lexicographic scores. Neither arm beats geometry in any comparison. This is a
single-training-seed adaptive development experiment, not unopened evaluation.

T=1 selects 109 ports, 31 component edits, 28 contractions, 16 insertions, four bridge
releases, three reroutes and one rejected pose. T=0.05 selects 112 ports, 50
contractions, ten insertions, ten reroutes, eight component edits and two bridge
releases; it never selects a pose. Executor rejections are 38/20 respectively,
versus 5/0 in their parent arms. Improving partial pair accuracy has not taught
useful original-root relocation selection.

Binary labels impose no ordering between two successful actions, including an area
reduction versus a wiring-only improvement. The kind-balanced schedule also heavily
repeats rare wiring examples. These are plausible contributors to the measured
shift toward ports; this experiment does not isolate their individual causal effect.
The next useful comparison should train full-score preferences, including among
successful actions, or imitate the best collected full-score choices. Preserve the
parent trajectory checkpoint as the shared start and compare equal update budgets.
Do not extend this regressing binary objective merely by running longer.

## Geometry, verification and outstanding scope

Three complete native-validated before/after PNG/SVG views were inspected: medical
and watered-cycle best collected poses, plus ranked medical T=0.05 at seed 1310000.
Medical's teacher moves its southern facility inward and reconnects it, reducing
height from 72 to 59 while lengthening several belts; large gaps remain. Grass
reduces height from 21 to 12 while preserving its internal cycle and pipe. The ranked
medical best leaves 55x72 and the facilities intact, reducing transport 345 to 336.
Its image demonstrates wiring improvement without packing improvement.

All heavy evaluation bests remain `[12900,2401,268]` for the sparse original and
`[2132,374,148]` for the dense incumbent. The latter remains 41x52 and **30.5347%
empty XY**, far from the requested 1%. Larger 65–128-facility data, unopened diverse
evaluation, bounded production CPU CLI integration and the final original target
remain incomplete. This result supplies an actionable objective/data correction,
not a fundamental impossibility finding.

All 33 full-factory Python tests pass, including three new tests for same-root
training-only sampling, missing-evidence exclusion, family leakage rejection and
gradient preference direction. Workspace check and 536 Rust tests pass with two
existing ignores. Collection replay passes 597 actions; ranking audit checks all
256 update memberships and all 48 bests. Full evaluation-action replay is not claimed.
Artifact checks pass before commit. The retained learned checkpoint contains Adam,
Torch RNG and the exact pair schedule's protocol hash; an identical final progress
copy was removed. Generated data remain ignored with a compact provenance manifest.
