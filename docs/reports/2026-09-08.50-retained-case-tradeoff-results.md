Status: retrospective analysis complete for all six continuations and the frozen
first-pair precision evaluation. No additional sampling, learning or gate changes.
This narrows the next investigation: aggregate assessment gains conceal regressions
already present at the short training horizon on fully exposed original roots.

starts and 32 easier rehearsal starts. Fourteen cases receive 96 original-root
starts each. Battery gap4 turns 0/2 remain at level eight and receive none in this
frozen interval; all other cases are at level nine. Each branch therefore has
1344 root-start episodes. The two largest precision regressions, glass gap0 turn0
and battery gap0 turn0, both receive all 96 original-root starts. Absence of root
exposure is not their explanation in this interval.

At update 32, total short success improves from 914 to 956 (+42), while battery
gap0 turn3 alone improves 13 to 61 (+48). The other fifteen cases together lose
six successes. This decomposition does not replace the original metric; it explains
why aggregate improvement was insufficient evidence of case-wise retention.

The lower-rate glass gap0 turn0 short curve at updates 8/16/24/32 is
63/47/42/46, versus ordinary-rate 64/64/64/61. Battery gap0 turn0 is
64/58/51/51 versus 62/63/61/63. Both weaknesses therefore precede the long-horizon
precision evaluation and appear from the original root at sixteen commands.
A long-horizon mismatch alone cannot account for these measured regressions.

## Completion counters and incumbent semantics

Across all long precision cases, ordinary/lower rate has 101/207 failed translated
improvements. Of these, 99/197 never complete a changed valid layout. Every failed
episode issues at least one placement command. These counters do not prove that
every command changed a position, that every failure has the same sequence, or that
any selected placement was impossible to reconnect. The existing selected repair
witness remains an existence result for that one saved prefix.

An unfinished final draft must not be counted as failed factory output. In the first
pair, 1678 of 1802 positive-return training episodes (ordinary) and 1778 of 1979
(lower) finish with unfinished drafts after already preserving an improved valid
incumbent. Across the other four branches, the corresponding positive/unfinished
counts are 2026/1860, 1779/1650, 1802/1641 and 1997/1797 in recorded run order.
Continuing private edits after success is allowed by the iterative objective.
These counts do not justify stopping at the first completion, rewarding validity
alone, or penalizing all intermediate invalid states.
