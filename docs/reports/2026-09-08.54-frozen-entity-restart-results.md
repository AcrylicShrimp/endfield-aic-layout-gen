## Interpretation and next work

The inference procedure materially changes reliability and geometry quality.
This comparison changes both the remaining-horizon input and state resets, so
it does not identify either as the sole cause. Earlier clock clamping was a
separate intervention and also did not establish a general repair. No training,
BN updates, witness setup, free repair or early-success stopping occurred here.

The remaining retention question concerns earlier scheduled checkpoints under
the actual intended inference procedure: the original capacity selector evaluated
one 128-command episode, whereas stage 3 deployed four 32-command attempts.
The final-checkpoint matrix does not evaluate every earlier checkpoint under
restarts. A bounded next selection study should declare the complete six-stream,
nine-checkpoint grid and exact quality/reliability ranking before sampling, then
freeze any selections before fresh root confirmation and development/held-out
outcomes. Supplemental success is not sufficient to skip that full selection
and confirmation. Do not repeat only the nearly passing final cell or narrow
away the failing geometries. The old failed criteria remain historical results.

Stages 4/5, shared-network/pipe/cycle coverage and the original 59-facility
at-most-1%-empty-area and visual acceptance remain incomplete. No new training
matrix is supported by this inference comparison alone.
