## Method and limits

Follow the [capacity protocol](../designs/2026-09-08.13-relocation-capacity-pilot.md).
Width 1 was selected using only pilot development evidence. Seeds 17 and 29 each
train for 393216 edits; seed 43 reuses its original pilot run and selected policy.
All three runs use source `3bf5f8d` and the same effective learning configuration.
Training times are 139.910, 140.680 and 141.315 seconds respectively. Two consecutive
root development passes select a checkpoint; no outcome-dependent checkpoint search
or alternative seed is allowed. All confirmations precede opening either outcome bank.

Confirmation takes 0.985 seconds for 3072 native edits; outcome evaluation plus independent
representative validation takes 8.424 seconds for 24576 native edits. Total selection
execution is 9.506 seconds. These are batch experiment costs, not a production latency
promise. Horizon is 16 for confirmation and 128 for outcomes.

Seed 29's final model was less reliable during development. The result establishes
capability with explicit policy retention, not monotonic training stability. Outcomes
contain failures as well as successes; do not claim guaranteed improvement or infer
new-layout generalization from new sampling seeds.
