# Valid pose teachers on the complete 116-facility original

## Result

The ordinary executor can relocate facilities and reconnect the **complete large
factory**. Of 384 independent pose trials, 164 validate, 143 include actual facility
translation, and 18 improve the full lexicographic score. Fifteen improvements
include translation and three are pure rotations. None reduces area: gains are in
physical transport tiles and turns. This is measured teacher capability, not a
learned-policy success or a compact-layout result.

All 384 actions are independently reexecuted with full original-graph validation,
ordinary-vocabulary membership, route-work/status checks, complete metrics and exact
current-witness hashes. Every selected retained witness also matches. The large
root is not intrinsically beyond the existing local movement executor: the previous
frozen policy was choosing unsuccessful poses from a domain that includes valid,
useful ones.

## Declared screen

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.20-large-factory-pose-teachers.md) and external
study configuration select 192 poses from each of two states: the medical 116
original and report 19's eight-contraction geometry best. These are two states of
**one original**, not two independent factories. Trained-family and opened-development
metadata are retained. No unopened family, held-out split or model weights are used
to train in this slice.

Each state offers the ordinary full vocabulary. Within five disjoint strata,
shuffle facility-member groups deterministically, visit groups round-robin, and
prefer shorter travel then movement toward the facility center within each group.
Selection does not route candidates or change the production vocabulary. All five
quotas fill without borrowing from another stratum. Seeds are 1400000/1400001.

Every trial starts from its own immutable state, permits one ordinary action,
5000 route expansions and 30 seconds, and submits its result to the native full
factory acceptance session. The whole 900-second study finishes in **105.949 seconds**,
with no late trial. No contraction continuation, partial factory or extra teacher
operator is included in these 384 trials.

## Why these are not yet preferred-action learning targets

The original still offers 108 one-step contractions, and the second state offers
100. After the pose screen is complete, declare and evaluate two separate controls:
the first offered contraction from each immutable initial state, with full original
validation. These controls do not alter the pose denominator or append work to poses.

| State | Best measured pose | First contraction | Better under configured priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original | `[86190,11181,695]` | `[85852,11181,697]` | Contraction |
| After 8 contractions | `[83486,11103,695]` | `[83148,11092,697]` | Contraction |

Both controls use zero route expansions and beat every measured pose because area
comes first. Training the policy to prefer these pose positives over all ordinary
actions would contradict the configured objective. The labels establish improvement
over the initial state, not the best action among the complete vocabulary.

Next construct a validated state where ordinary contractions are exhausted, recording
and charging that preparation explicitly. Screen poses on that state and include
full-score action contrasts before adapting the learned model. This uses the new
large-factory relocation evidence without teaching the model to ignore cheaper,
higher-priority area improvements. Do not increase routing limits merely because
the previous policy's selected long moves failed.
