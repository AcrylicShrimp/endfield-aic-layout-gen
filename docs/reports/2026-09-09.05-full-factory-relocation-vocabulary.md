# Full-factory distant and group relocation

Status: implementation, complete comparison, independent audit and scoped replay
passed. Stages four and five remain open; no global impossibility finding.

The group vocabulary increases mean sampled area reduction from 1.3489% for raw
local moves to 3.2249%, but its before/after-training scores are identical in all
50 pairs. This is evidence of a more useful executor domain, not learned compression.
Static filtering reduces futile selections, but every final policy still wastes
more than half its actions. A shared untrained/final/uniform target result reaches
`[2132,385,146]`, still `41x52` and **30.0188% empty XY**. The 1% and visual gates fail.

## Contract and implementation

Implementation commit: `1cab494`. The [compact provenance manifest](2026-09-09.05-full-factory-relocation-vocabulary-provenance.json)
identifies the retained protocols, checkpoints, audits, witness and visual.

The [declared contract](../designs/2026-09-09.03-full-factory-relocation-vocabulary.md)
replaces model-connected single-facility `move` with `relocate(members, dx, dy)`.
Members are current placement indices, resolved to stable instance identities when
executing an edit or recording a semantic action key. Executor RPC/description
schema 2 and `full-factory-spatial-relational-v2` reject older contracts explicitly.
The native complete-witness schema and original-request validator are unchanged.
Historical schema-one experiments require their historical Git versions.

The policy now pools every member's facility embedding and every member's destination
spatial sample. Destination coordinates, normalized full-range displacement, group
size, body area/bounds and source geometry enter the action features. This removes
the single-facility operand assumption; no old-weight adapter or old movement branch
remains. Other operations share the same model and executor in all four arms.

Groups include singletons, required owners of non-detachable typed terminal slots,
closed producer/consumer pairs and physical production SCCs. Directed reachability
is computed separately within each commodity network; disconnected islands do not
become connected simply because their item IDs match. Fixed external-terminal
closures and groups exceeding sixteen members are omitted with scoped diagnostics.
These are restrictions of this local neighborhood, not game infeasibility claims.

Static preflight checks destination bodies and carried internal geometry against
stationary bodies/anchors. Prefix occupancy tables avoid rescanning every body cell
for each candidate. Ordinary route cells can be ripped up after selection. Candidate
enumeration performs no A* or full validation. It scans a deterministic bounded
spread of translations inside the current extent, including cardinal one/two-cell
moves, with no objective-based candidate ranking. Every selected edit still invokes
the dedicated pipe-first router and native original-request acceptance.

The 512-group, 256-scan, 32-candidate defaults, sixteen-member limit and 200000-cell
observation guard are prototype work bounds. They are not game dimensions or proofs
that an omitted move is impossible. Outward rerouting and external-terminal
re-anchoring remain absent. A successful endpoint relocation can jump over obstacles;
there is no physical robot movement path to validate between the old and new bodies.

Compared with the constructive planner design, every edit starts with a complete
validated factory and repairs routes privately. Only complete valid score improvements
replace the retained best. The constructor, global exact research solver and score
order remain separate and unchanged.

## Comparison protocol

Four fresh depth-zero width-32 policies share seed 17 and the same initialization:
raw local singles, statically filtered local singles, filtered distant singles, and
filtered distant singles plus connected groups. Each receives 1024 selected actions:
32 updates of four eight-action episodes, rotating battery-38, citron-48,
iron-nine-108 and closure-refined. Adam .0003, entropy .01, rank objective and gradient
clip .5 are common. Sampling is flat over candidates and history masking is disabled.
Changing the candidate vocabulary changes the resulting operation probability mass;
this is part of this declared comparison, not an isolated learned-prior experiment.

Each untrained/final policy and one uniform control per vocabulary uses all 23
retained roots plus the 392-tile and latest 386-tile strong starts. Banks 1230000 and
1240000 receive four eight-action attempts per input. A common 30-second cell guard
includes resets, observation, selection and recording; each successive episode gets
only the remaining time. The overall screen guard is 1800 seconds. The full matrix
contains 600 evaluation cells and 2912 total episodes: 4096 training and 19200
evaluation selections. Original graph coverage, score priority and best retention
apply to every case, including failures.

This is a one-training-seed screen on historically opened, related geometries.
It is not sealed new-family generalization, convergence evidence or production
readiness. Comparing final versus untrained separates sampled learning benefit from
some executor benefits; comparing the uniform controls examines vocabulary changes
without learned selection. Equal selection budgets do not imply equal CPU costs.

An initial run was interrupted during the first arm to finish diagnostic stage,
severity and entity fields. Its partial report and progress checkpoint remain under
`artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-relocation-v2`. The complete comparison
restarts all arms from the same seed in `full-factory-relocation-v2-complete`; no
outcome-based seed, allocation, vocabulary or hyperparameter change was made.

## Outcomes and cost

All 600 cells and 2912 episodes completed in 469.53 seconds, with all 23296
selections accounted for. No native-invalid candidate was submitted in this matrix;
executor failures remain charged. All retained outputs independently preserve the
original graphs and metrics.

Each row below has 50 input/bank cells. W/T/L compares final versus untrained scores.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

The corresponding uniform controls improve 29/31/28/27 cells, with mean area
reductions 1.3689%/1.8251%/2.1346%/3.2249%. Groups-final versus local-final has
21 score wins, 14 ties and 15 losses; area comparison is 11/36/3. Thus the wider
vocabulary does not dominate every request. Filtered local has the lowest measured
waste, while groups has the largest average area reduction. These are exploratory
tradeoffs, not a selected release model.

After excluding the four exact training geometries, the 42 remaining paired cells
have W/T/L 0/42/0, 3/39/0, 1/41/0 and 0/42/0 respectively. They remain related,
historically opened families. No final evaluation best improvement had an explicit
pipe operation operand; full-factory validity includes pipes/cycles, but robust
learned pipe improvement is not demonstrated.

The 28545-parameter policies did update. Across five inspected root observations,
final-versus-initial total variation ranges from 0.36% to 2.20%; group-policy drift
is only 0.36%–0.64%. Parameter L2 changes range from .290 to .390, with finite
recorded gradients and non-tied rank groups in 20/20/24/20 of the 32 updates. The
short screen neither establishes convergence nor proves that more training cannot
help. It does establish that the reported group advantage already exists without
training, so it must not be presented as a learned success.

Final raw-local selection attempts 657 relocations and changes geometry in 97.
Filtered-local changes 141/203. Distant-final changes 173/630 distant singles and
114/149 local singles. Groups-final changes 79/181 group moves, 148/505 distant
singles and 117/150 local singles; ten group moves improve the retained score.
For filtered relocation candidates, every observed selected failure is `local-no-path`
or `local-work-budget`; the static body/anchor failure categories disappear. This
says nothing about the remaining rotate/port/component failures, and static admission
is still not a routing or whole-factory feasibility test.

Candidate-generation CPU per 1600 final evaluation selections is .24 / 11.17 /
11.77 / 24.01 seconds. Its per-selection p95 is .0007 / .0402 / .0418 / .0882
seconds; groups' maximum is .1502 seconds. Final episode p95 is .388 / .705 /
.768 / 1.286 seconds, and the largest cell duration is 8.257 seconds including all
four attempts and recording. All are below the declared 30-second cell guard.
The per-cell futile-action p95 is 84.375% / 81.25% / 84.375% / 75%, well above the
provisional 20% release gate. Filtering is charged computation, not free masking.

## Mandatory target and next capability

Distant-untrained, distant-final and distant-uniform all reach `[2132,385,146]` in
bank 1240000, attempt 1, starting from the prior 386-tile incumbent. A belt-port
edit supplies the one-tile/two-turn improvement; it is not a distant/group relocation
success or a paired learned advantage. The retained final-policy witness has 1133
facility cells, 267 belt cells, 118 pipe cells, 26 belt/pipe overlaps, 1492 occupied
XY cells and 640 empty cells. The largest empty rectangle remains 7x10 at `(27,26)`.
The lower transport score slightly increases the empty fraction, as expected from
lexicographic scoring. Do not add filler wiring to reverse that metric.

The native-verified side-by-side PNG/SVG in
`artifacts/factory-edit-pilot/full-factory-relocation-heavy-view` was inspected:
all 59 facility labels remain present and the large internal holes remain visible.
It does not pass visual acceptance. The stable witness and its original-request,
matched-control and independent-metric records are retained under
`full-factory-relocation-heavy-best`. All earlier incumbents remain intact.

A separately declared post-screen probe selects 32 evenly spaced actions from the
172 offered destinations whose complete group-body bounds fit that empty rectangle.
Each starts from the same 385-tile witness, with 5000 route expansions and a shared
60-second diagnostic allowance. This is outside the learning comparison. It finishes
in .611 seconds: five valid relocations, 26 `local-no-path` failures and one work
exhaustion. The five moves are three-cell facilities, each independently validated;
all retain area 2132 and increase transport to 395–412 tiles. Neither the original
state nor these five candidates exposes a strip insertion/contraction. Their
exploratory witnesses are retained separately and do not replace the best.

This falsifies the narrow explanation that the grammar cannot propose or execute
moves into the empty region. The next task is to learn or construct a sequence that
releases boundary space while keeping its connecting routes valid. Use these verified
strong-root transitions to build full-factory curriculum/trajectory tasks, and test
coupled placement/port repair where retained port choices block the required moves.
External-terminal re-anchoring and outward routing are additional explicit missing
operators; this screen does not establish that they are indispensable. Do not claim
impossibility from the 27 failed probe choices, or launch another unchanged
selection/horizon sweep. Broader new-family pipe/cycle data and production CPU CLI
integration remain required by the active goal.
