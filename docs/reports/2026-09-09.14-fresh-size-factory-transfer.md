# Fresh-size admission and frozen factory-policy transfer

## Result

The fresh-size screen exposes two separate limits. Five of six original factory
requests stop in construction before any learned edit, including every 85- and
116-facility request. The one admitted 26-facility medical original evaluates
successfully, but the report-13 learned policy ties its parent and loses to geometry.
No learned relocation transfer or 65–128-facility policy result is established here.

The [contract](../designs/2026-09-09.14-fresh-size-factory-transfer.md) and source
study predeclare all six requests. Existing food/medical plans receive fresh target
rates of 1/25, 1/6 and 1/4 units per second. Wiring measures 26, 85 and 116 facilities
for each plan. The `enriched-consumables` training-family identity is preserved;
the new requests have a separate fresh-size development role. This is evaluation
on new rates/geometries from a trained family, not unseen-family or sealed testing.

## Admission and construction bottleneck

All six attempts use the unchanged serial downstream-closed production constructor,
60 seconds per request, 256 growth steps, 256 placement attempts, 4096 route searches,
and a 128-facility admission guard. Source plans, original obligations, catalogs,
binaries and harness hashes are frozen. No outcome-driven retry, global placement,
exact fallback or partial-factory substitution is used. The guards are experimental
resources, not game limits. Admission completes in 77.857 seconds.

The accepted root has one connected 26-facility graph, cyclic SCCs of sizes 3, 3
and 2, eight branching facilities and nine merging facilities. This screen has
no required internal pipes. Import independently validates geometry and original
graph coverage before creating its native edit request. All failed requests stay
in the six-request denominator and never enter policy evaluation.

Each failed final growth transaction records exactly 4096 A* searches and
`production-route-budget-exhausted`. None reaches the 60-second deadline. The
reported per-connection route limit is 64; the routing workspace guard is one
million cells. Those values are operational bounds, not proofs of impossibility.

For all four large failures, the aggregate counters contain 64 new-port attempts
and 4032 existing-network attempts. They record 3866–3956 shared-branch route
failures. This points to attachment/routing work around an existing commodity
network as the next diagnostic subject; it does not isolate the physical cause.
The retained summaries resolve failure edge IDs to exact recipes, items, rates
and attempted facility groups for a bounded follow-up.

The 26-facility food failure is different: 3383 completed lane bundles and 63
validated network-attachment candidates still produce 3196 boundary dead ends,
including 1962 duplicate/opposing-arm and 1234 overlapping-branch-component
rejections. Its attempted transaction is a three-facility plant-cycle group.
The smaller failure shows why this cannot be called an intrinsic facility-count
threshold. Preserve these two failure patterns instead of just raising runtime.

No mutation to the constructor or its semantics is made in this slice. A useful
next construction experiment should reconstruct the earliest failed medical-85
frontier, inspect its port/attachment candidates and physical obstruction, and
compare a specific bounded routing or placement intervention. More training on
the one accepted root cannot resolve missing complete large-factory inputs.

## Frozen policy evaluation

Freeze [report 13](2026-09-09.13-full-score-action-learning.md)'s best-mass checkpoint
and the preceding trajectory parent, with no training, teacher collection or
calibration on this request. Evaluate both at T=0.05 plus the ordinary geometry
baseline, each with banks 1310000/1320000, eight selections, 30 seconds and 5000
route expansions per selection. The full action domain, history mask and native
lexicographic incumbent protection are unchanged. Model tensors and source hashes
remain identical across the run.

All six episodes complete in 5.627 seconds, totaling 48 selected edits without
shortened episodes. Both banks give identical results within each method:

| Method | Best area / transport / turns | Dimensions | Area reduction | Selections per episode |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Parent | 8280 / 876 / 125 | 92x90 | 7.07% | seven contractions, one insertion |
| Best-mass learned | 8280 / 876 / 125 | 92x90 | 7.07% | seven contractions, one insertion |
| Geometry | 8190 / 871 / 125 | 91x90 | 8.08% | eight contractions |

The initial score is `[8910,883,125]`, 99x90. Learned ties its parent twice and loses
to geometry twice under the complete score. No pose or reroute is selected; there
is no executor rejection or deadline loss. The prior six-root training improvement
has not supplied additional benefit on this fresh same-family request. Two sampling
seeds on one accepted root do not establish a generalization rate.

The independent audit verifies admission/evaluation membership, unchanged checkpoint
hashes, every retained best against the original graph, and the complete independent
area/transport/terminal-aware-turn metric. All six pass. No capability episode
requires pose/reroute replay, and full replay of the 48 strip edits is not claimed.
The native-validated learned before/after PNG/SVG was inspected: it removes seven
columns while preserving a sparse arrangement, long external tails, branching
banks and large gaps. There is no new relocation or polished packing result.

## Verification, retention and remaining goal

The full-factory Python suite passes 38 tests, including two new checks that exclude
partial factories, require matching facility counts and preserve the distinction
between trained-family membership and fresh evaluation role. Workspace check and
536 Rust tests pass with two existing ignores. The six source requests, importer,
frozen comparison, summary and independent audit were exercised through their CLIs.
Artifact worktree/index checks pass before commit.

Retain every attempt, wiring graph, source plan and constructor report under ignored
artifacts, including the failed partial states needed for the next diagnostic.
Remove six regenerable construction HTML files and six raw wiring stdout copies
only after confirming JSON equality with the retained wiring files. Cleanup records
their hashes and removes 20,171,082 bytes. No model checkpoint is created or replaced.
The concise provenance manifest points to retained reports, inputs, audit and view.

The learned improver has not been evaluated on an accepted 65–128-facility original.
Construction of such inputs and wider full-score relocation data are now concrete
follow-ups. Unopened diverse evaluation, required game semantics, bounded production
CPU CLI and the original Heavy Xiranite 1%/visual acceptance remain incomplete.
Heavy Xiranite is not rerun here; its preserved dense incumbent remains
`[2132,374,148]`, 41x52 and 30.5347% empty XY. These bounded failures establish
neither global infeasibility nor a fundamental barrier to the full goal.
