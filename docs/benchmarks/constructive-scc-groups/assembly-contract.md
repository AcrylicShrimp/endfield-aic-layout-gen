# Variable-membership source-bank assembly

Input: one allocation-aligned graph, current catalogs, independently checked SCC region
portfolios and a complete incumbent. Each bank has mandatory seed members and optional
extensions. The union of possible owners is disjoint across banks; every variant is
input-closed and contains its seed. No source cloning, dropped recipe rates or implicit
external substitutions are allowed.

Pack actual body/belt/pipe cells, keeping independent transport layers. Keep enough unused
ports for every exact remaining item margin on any side, not only east rays. Intermediate
row-bitset checks are necessary screens; final assembled candidates receive closed-pocket
checks, independent geometry and original-graph coverage validation. Translate the final
assembly to a one-cell origin margin so north/west local ports are not mistaken for
negative global coordinates. That margin is not counted as occupied area.

Retain a bounded beam stratified by covered facility count, with envelope diversity inside
each stratum. Partial area does not make a smaller, less-complete candidate superior to a
larger, more-complete one. No fixed N% cutoff or exact/global optimality claim. Report all
search bounds and the actual members/shape/translation chosen for each bank. There is no
mandatory shared-consumer join or exactly-two-cluster phase.
At every packing depth, screen up to 16 times the requested beam size for closed-pocket
fit and retain alternatives after a rejection. Record checked/rejected counts and the
bounded search scope. Do not describe exhaustion of this shortlist as infeasibility.

Before singleton frontier closure, introduce ready dependent SCCs atomically using a
validated superset variant of their owning source bank. The changed bank may be reseated
transactionally among the other, unchanged routed banks. This phase has no inter-bank
routed terminals yet; it must not detach a region after outside connections exist. A
candidate preserves old members, adds exactly the ready atom and passes all validations.
If no supplied template/placement succeeds, retain the valid partial state and report the
specific exhausted atomic expansion. Do not silently filter out small stop states merely
because they require dependent-cycle growth. Template coverage is a measured research
limitation, not a proof about other legal geometries.

Then invoke the existing immediately routed singleton closure and final external-output
stage. Revalidate complete results against the aligned graph and compare them with the
complete incumbent by full area, physical transport cells and turns. A partial seed or
failed completion cannot replace the incumbent. Preserve separate outcome fields for new
construction completion and incumbent retention. Only complete original-graph audits may
support a full-factory success claim.

Use explicit CLI beam/candidate budgets, JSON reports and per-process log files. Record
generation and closure wall times independently. Inspect actual rendered failures as well
as successful output. This is an isolated JavaScript research stage, not a Rust cutover.

The route audit is narrower: replay one recorded one-input, one-lane failed placement
without moving any facility or existing wire. Compare simple/perimeter templates with
ordinary A* in the measured extent plus four cells. Validate every found candidate and
screen all remaining interfaces. A path alone is not a successful growth transaction.
Other input/lane patterns are explicitly unsupported by this replay, not infeasible.
