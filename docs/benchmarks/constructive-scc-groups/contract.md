# SCC-seeded stop/grow regions

Input: current normalized graph, runtime catalogs, independently validated retained
source regions. Preserve the separately validated complete incumbent; this region-stage
CLI does not construct or select a complete factory. Discover actual strongly connected components
on the current internal requirements, not historical cycle labels. Source SCCs have no
incoming internal or external obligations. Never call an already extended four-facility
region a minimal SCC when allocation changes have reduced its actual cycle to two.

First reconcile internal source-bank allocation with the independently validated concrete
routes. Decompose existing positive commodity flow into source/sink contributions; reject
external terminals, bridge-channel ambiguity, unmatched demand or residual circulation.
Emit explicit replacement edges, preserving every per-source/per-target item margin via
the shared checked graph-delta contract. Recompute SCCs only on the emitted graph. A
compatible whole-bank supply reallocation may legitimately change SCC membership; record
the new dependencies instead of retaining the historical five-member SCC label.

Extract a private source SCC from retained concrete geometry by retaining exactly its
required internal commodity flows. A capacity-bounded rational max-flow projection may
reduce existing segment and terminal rates but never increase them or introduce a new
route. Each included facility retains its identity, recipe, utilization and all required
input margins. Removed downstream consumption becomes exact pending source output.
Shared sources are neither duplicated nor silently reallocated. Bridged retained
networks are outside the first projection family: do not merge their directional channels
into one ordinary cell. Independently validate every selected projected region.

For each source SCC, retain the stop candidate and bounded one/two-atomic extensions.
An atom is one whole current SCC (including an acyclic singleton), not necessarily one
facility. A dependent cycle must be introduced together. The first experiment restricts
extensions to the same retained parent region; it does not claim unrestricted growth.
An extension must have all inputs inside that region, route its inputs immediately and
preserve pending outputs. Consider runtime allowed rotations and short direct links;
retained-geometry projection provides a separate measured candidate, not an optimum.
Record productive/occupied fill, actual area, transport cells, cuts and membership.
`facility_fill_ratio` is facility cells divided by the complete used bounding area;
`fill_ratio` includes the union of facilities and both transport layers in its numerator.
Do not reward a long detour merely because it raises occupied fill. Neither ratio is a
replacement for comparing complete factories by area, transport cells and turns.
Do not silently force a shared-consumer join between distinct source regions.
Check exact unused pending-output capacity and closed-pocket consumer fit for every
selected region, including all earlier owners. A detached region's normalized origin is
not a factory wall: negative local connection cells outside its nonnegative occupied
geometry remain accessible by later translation. Recheck access after actual packing.

Region portfolios may have different covered facility sets: every alternative must
contain its mandatory seed, be input-closed and have ownership disjoint from every
other bank's alternatives. Omitted facilities remain in the original graph's frontier.
Packing must use actual occupied cells and explicit pending port access, with no
requirement to create exactly two clusters. Assembled partial states still independently
validate, then normal transactional closure must cover the entire original graph.
This assembly/closure integration is the next stage, not implemented by the region runner.
No N% threshold selects a final partition in this stage; it retains the measured stop/grow
portfolio for that later comparison. `scope` makes both unimplemented stages explicit.

The report records total wall time and generation, projection, interface, rendering and
validator-task times. Validator-task time sums concurrent tasks and cannot be added to
the other fields to reconstruct elapsed time. Detailed reports use files; stdout is one
short completion record. Output directories must be fresh.

Area, physical transport cells and turns retain their priority for complete-factory
selection. Local fill and limited beam diversity are heuristic candidate signals, not
global optimality or impossibility claims. Preserve the complete incumbent on loss or
failure. This is research construction; no Rust migration or production contract change.
