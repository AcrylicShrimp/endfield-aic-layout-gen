# Independent-layer compaction experiment

The order diagnostic exactly reproduces the existing compactor's facility equality
groups. Removing only incidental cross-layer coincidences splits its largest 19-member
X group into 9/7/2/1 members; the Y group containing F37/F48 and the F19 process chain
also splits. This is evidence of unnecessary coupling, not evidence of a smaller valid
factory yet.

Input/output are the existing complete-detail and certificate contracts. Add an explicit
`layerPolicy` experiment: `coupled` is the historical control; `independent` indexes
transport events by layer and applies retained row order separately to each layer.
Facility body cells belong to both layers and share one rigid facility pose. Facility
terminals remain tied to that pose on their own layer; logistics components and shared
same-layer channels remain tied on their layer. External sides, rates, topology, directed
unit geometry and width/height scoring retain their contracts. No new rotations, port
assignments, same-layer crossings or components are introduced.

The new model may break incidental belt/pipe coincidences and reverse their geometric
order because the layers are independent. It must not break any body, same-layer
separation, terminal or component constraint. Solve and materialize candidates privately;
independent geometry and original-graph validation remain mandatory before promotion.
Compare both policies from the same incumbent with both axis orders and identical tie
exploration budgets. Report actual full area/cells/turns, validation failures and timings.
This is a bounded improvement experiment, not a new full generator or global optimizer.
