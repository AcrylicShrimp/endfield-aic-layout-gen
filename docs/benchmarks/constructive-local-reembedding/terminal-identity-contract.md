# Identity-based coincident terminal detachment

Input remains a complete witness and schema-3 simultaneous facility translations.
Chain schema 2 explicitly represents an isolated same-cell source/sink pair as a
zero-segment directed chain. It requires exactly two uniquely identified facility
terminals on one transport/network, equal positive rates, opposite flow directions,
no incident segment, and no component at that cell. More complex shared junctions
retain explicit shared-ownership failures; no arbitrary flow allocation is inferred.

Facility terminal identity, not its former grid coordinate, determines its final
position. Two owners may translate differently. Their zero-segment connection then
becomes an ordinary rate-preserving routed chain. Equal translations may still carry
the one-cell connection. Final body/terminal/component collisions are checked before
acceptance; old isolated cells must not survive as ghost geometry after separation.
All original demands, commodity membership and external boundary identities remain.

Port assignment eligibility and output positions use terminal IDs, including the two
ends of detachable zero-length chains. The engine assigns no temporary game coordinate
and accepts no worse intermediate factory. Explicit split branches and complex shared
anchors require a different synthesis contract and are not detached by this slice.

Remove obsolete mandatory group closure for these supported pairs and update candidate
ranking, motion screening, route generation and diagnostic replay to use endpoint-aware
coordinates. Historical chain IDs are not stable across this cutover: new reports declare
chain schema 2; old replay reports require their recorded implementation. Witness/game
schemas and production CLI remain unchanged.

Verification: isolated same-shift, separate shifts, immutable input, exact rates,
negative ambiguous/shared branches, port reassignment, no ghost cell, body/anchor
collision, full actual-catalog factory validation and original-graph negative controls.
Compare prior incumbent, singleton/group/coalescence portfolios and an explicit F36
reproduction derived from the input labels. Inspect every published native result.
Area is the first objective; no global optimum/infeasibility or arbitrary recipe claim.

also supports the inverse of isolated detachment: one direct equal-rate chain may
become one shared cell with exactly its two original facility terminals. All other
anchor/body/component collisions remain checked. This changes executor eligibility,
not witness schema or native acceptance. See the
