## Goal and Scope

Compute exact physical before/after cost and capacity-use metadata for each accepted direct, network and repair witness, then expose those same values through versioned outer action features and reports. Guidance and congestion terms remain separate from physical units.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] Costs use committed geometry and catalog components: steps/turns, unique new occupied cells, component IDs/kinds/rotations and footprints, reuse, and exact rational capacity utilization. Network/repair cost is net before-to-after delta including removals.
- [ ] Candidate metadata, feature rows, reports and training records use the same physical vector and charged work, including negative repair deltas; old shapes are explicitly versioned or rejected.
- [ ] A completed native replay supplies the retained original source and trusted child for cost calculation without duplicate internal replay or a full before-state snapshot. The cost cursor retains data-dependent changed-route, route-turn, touched-site bridge-channel/owner and occupied-user positions for direct, network and repair member costs. Repair command outcomes remain available for `Step` assembly. External/deserialized action application retains boundary checks.
- [ ] For a native before/after pair and a complete changed-connection list, every changed junction or bridge site lies on a changed route's before/after cells. Prove this offline with installed, removed and replaced component cases; eliminate the separate whole-junction/bridge owner-map scan and its cursor when the invariant holds. Do not add a routine per-candidate invariant audit. The public cost method's documented input contract remains a natively replayed successor and named changed obligations.

## Proposed Changes

Complete the WIP `physical_cost_between` and candidate metadata cutover in `aic-pilot-native`, and the WIP outer feature/checkpoint/report schema update in `aic-learning`. Use native before/after sessions at the publication boundary, deduplicate shared cells/components once, and preserve exact `Rate` arithmetic until conversion to finite feature values. Version incompatible rows/checkpoints together. One `PhysicalCostCursor` shares the existing `site_cost`/`utilization_at` rules across direct/network/repair. Its phases collect changed-route before/after cells and turns, then inspect only those touched sites with a nested channel/owner or occupancy-user cursor. Native junction and bridge updates can alter a component only at a changed route cell; the old whole-map owner scan adds no site for a trusted native delta and is removed after an offline component-change fixture proves this invariant. Repair member replay will keep its original draft and validated child through Plan .10; calculate its exact delta from those two retained states, with no pre-replay `PhysicalCostSnapshot` capture or second full-state copy on that new path. Reserve each touched-site/component growth and retain both source and child through completion. The fair first-turn scheduler advances this cursor during publication. Plan .10 removes the obsolete internal snapshot path when it cuts over the current production one-shot repair caller. No routine duplicate cost/replay pass is added.
