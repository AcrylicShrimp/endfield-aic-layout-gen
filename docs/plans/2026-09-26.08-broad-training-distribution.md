# Plan: Broad hierarchical training distribution and scale calibration

## Current Status

- State: Ready
- Last Updated: 2026-09-27

## Goal and Scope

Prepare the actual training and fixed held-out input paths to expose the eventual model to the intended production graph sizes and routing patterns. Keep small panels as fast diagnostics. This step prepares and validates configurations and local sampled inputs; it does not run GPU training or claim that the untrained model solves large factories.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] State the intended production size envelope from the production sampling policy and project use cases, including facility, terminal and connection counts, physical area, belt/pipe mix and branching/crossing/repair patterns. Do not treat the 256-facility sampler ceiling as proof that 256-facility physical layouts are feasible. Record which upper ranges remain unproven on local hardware.
- [ ] The training curriculum draws a documented mixture of small, medium and intended large production graphs using the existing generator. The initial final-distribution admission floor is 32 distinct training graph identities per size band per generation and 8 disjoint fixed held-out identities per band; within each band, at least 8 training and 4 held-out identities must be belt-only, and the same minima must be pipe-containing, with at least 8/4 branching identities where production graphs admit branching. These floors follow the production sampler's roughly balanced belt/pipe observations in the local 1–3, 4–64 and 65–256 probes; they create more than one independent graph for every required transport and branch slice. They are admission floors, not caps, and a larger empirically needed sample must be used rather than relaxing a failed floor to fit a small run. Every required size band has exposure independent of small-band promotion success; realized per-generation counts and rejection reasons are recorded. A successful tiny level cannot silently become the entire training distribution. For each feasible band, explicit quotas cover belt-only, pipe-containing and logical branching graphs in both training and fixed held-out partitions; missing quotas fail panel publication. Select quota-compliant cases from a bounded candidate pool before the generator discards it, preserving uniqueness and partition split; report which quota exhausted the pool. Physical crossing, catalog-component and repair exposure cannot be inferred from an unplaced logical graph: collection reports actual legal opportunities and accepted witnesses by band, and any required category missing from model training or held-out evaluation blocks readiness. Distinct fixed held-out identities never enter training or promotion.
- [ ] Preserve size diversity *inside* the broad bands. Audit exact facility counts in the fixed subranges `1`, `2`, `3`, `4–16`, `17–32`, `33–64`, `65–128`, `129–192`, `193–239`, and `240–256`, as well as terminal count, connection count and physical area. For each subrange in the declared production envelope, the initial panel floor is eight distinct training identities per generation and two disjoint held-out identities; each subrange also needs at least two training and one held-out belt-only identities and the same number of pipe-containing identities. These are floors, not an instruction to downsample other sizes or stop after finding small examples. If the claimed production envelope reaches 256 facilities, the near-maximum 240–256 tail must appear; if physical feasibility cannot be established, report that portion as blocked and do not quietly redefine it as a smaller target. Declare any genuinely inapplicable subrange from production use cases and physical evidence before sampling, with user approval for a narrower intended product envelope.
- [ ] Make the declared non-facility scale envelope operational too. Before sampling, version the production-use-case upper requirement for terminals, logical connections, occupied-cell count, and the bounding-box area of placed facility/route/component cells in each applicable band; define the upper-tail threshold as at least 75% of each declared required maximum, rounded up. Each required terminal and connection tail has at least eight distinct training and two disjoint held-out graph identities per generation. Each physical-area tail has at least eight distinct training and two disjoint held-out *placed* case identities; unplaced graphs cannot satisfy it. For each such tail, both models must receive usable targets from at least five distinct sources and at least 5% of selected optimizer mass. Audit lower and middle ranges as well, and predeclare representative joint extremes when a production use case requires a large facility count together with large connection load or physical spread; apply the same panel and optimizer floors to those declared joint cases. Do not require every cross-product cell merely because two marginal labels exist. A tail that cannot yield legal placed states or usable targets blocks readiness and names the failed metric. Do not set the declared maximum from whichever local sample happened to be easy.
- [ ] Actual optimizer exposure is stratified too. Per-generation outer and lower target records and selected optimizer examples have declared minimum counts for each required size × pattern stratum. The initial admission floor for each required band × belt/pipe/branch stratum is 8 distinct source graph identities with usable targets **in each model's selected examples** and at least 5% of that model's selected-example mass in the generation; each size band must independently contribute at least 20% of the selected mass in each model so small cases cannot dominate the optimizer; no one source graph may contribute more than 20% of any required stratum's selected mass, including replay. For a required physical crossing, component or repair stratum in a production-applicable band, use at least 5 distinct source graph identities and 5% selected mass in both models; five is the minimum compatible with the 20% per-source ceiling. Declare applicability from production use cases before drawing cases, not after seeing which targets were easy to collect. These conservative floors prevent one lucky large case or repeated replay from masquerading as broad training; measure effective diversity and raise the floors if correlated examples still dominate. Deterministic selection reserves real usable examples for every declared stratum before filling remaining capacity; overlapping labels may share an example but unique selected example identities and each fulfilled quota are reported. Replay stays inside its reserved stratum and cannot consume another stratum's minimum. A large case with placement roots but no route-choice target does not count as lower exposure. If any stratum lacks usable lower or outer targets, the generation records a named blocked diagnostic and cannot pass distribution readiness by panel count alone.
- [ ] In both models’ *selected optimizer examples*, each declared size subrange contributes at least five distinct source graph identities and 5% of that model’s generation mass. Within every applicable subrange, both belt-only and pipe-containing examples must occur from at least two distinct source identities each; otherwise broad-band totals can hide a transport mode concentrated at just one size. Report exact counts and effective diversity by subrange × transport and branching label. Label physical opportunities and accepted witnesses on separate axes: transport (belt or pipe), geometry (straight sharing or multiple interacting crossings), installed catalog component kind (splitter, converger or bridge), and intervention (bounded repair). Report bridge transport separately so belt and pipe bridges cannot substitute for one another. Declare which combinations the production use cases require before collection. For each required kind, verify usable lower and outer targets and selected examples against the five-identity/5%-mass physical-stratum floor above. A positive logical branching label or a tiny forced fixture does not satisfy a physical-pattern exposure requirement. If a production-required kind has no legal local witness or usable target, readiness remains blocked with that exact kind named.
- [ ] Attach scale and physical-pattern labels to each *individual* lower and outer optimizer example from its actual input tensors, candidate/action features and supervised target. A source graph or later completed layout may provide provenance but cannot confer a physical-pattern or area-tail hit on an earlier example that does not contain it. Count a component or crossing hit only when the example’s input state or supervised candidate/target includes that legal opportunity or accepted witness; count physical-area exposure only when the example’s input spatial state itself reaches the declared occupied-cell or bounding-box threshold. Recompute and check labels after replay and selection, immediately before each update. Include a case with a bridge and large area reached late: its earlier small-state examples must contribute zero bridge/area hits.
- [ ] Audit the tensors and targets that actually reach each model update, not only prepared panels or search attempts. Report realized facility/terminal/connection and physical-pattern histograms for collected states, usable lower/outer targets and selected optimizer examples separately, across multiple bounded generations. A failed large-case search, a filtered target, or small-band replay cannot be counted as large-band learning exposure. Require the intended large and pattern strata to persist through the last selection stage, or block readiness with the missing stratum named.
- [ ] Each case has one immutable band key, an effective versioned search configuration and explicit per-case native `Limits` bound to that key before collection. Its placement bounds, model raster dimensions, input limits, candidate work caps, request deadline, peak-memory limit, placement page, Gumbel shortlist, outer simulations per move, search horizon, episode command budget and, when enabled, beam width and beam execution are validated for that band's intended physical scale. Record whether each finite cap actually binds in local upper-band cases, whether increasing it exposes previously missing legal targets, and whether the resulting optimizer examples still meet the size × pattern quotas; a tiny default is not justified by merely being finite. The panel case, collection report, episode identity, training-record lineage and resume state retain both effective identities. Training reopens the case with those same `Limits` and reconstructs action features, area/quality normalization, lower guidance inputs and directed-edge offsets using that case's bounds and candidate config; a single global training config may define shared weight shapes but cannot replace per-case geometry or budget. Model inference and updates support variable spatial sizes by band grouping or padding with a valid mask, without cropping. Limits remain finite and measured; increase, derive or expose them where bounded local probes show truncation. Do not silently downsample, crop, reduce graph obligations or substitute easier layouts to make a band pass.
- [ ] Bounded local preparation generates both partitions at each specified band and reports actual facility/terminal/connection histograms, transport mix, endpoint-degree/branch patterns, known component opportunities, duplicate identities, rejection counts, input bytes and estimated routing-work/memory needs. Include representative large prepared states in a CPU-only input/allowance smoke, and compare default with generous allowance settings. Record where the full large-layout search cannot be completed locally without calling it a passed gate.
- [ ] Measure aggregate process memory through collection, retained root/target records, publication, concurrent singleton inference and network pages, and both lower and outer optimizer updates. Include variable-size raster activations and the current `batch_states` selection/retention path. Per-request peak allowance alone is not an aggregate process bound. Calibrate finite inference and training batch/memory settings against representative medium and intended large inputs without cropping or excluding required strata. A small-panel 41 MB request peak and one successful CPU update are not evidence of upper-band safety. If a required band cannot complete a bounded local optimizer update, readiness is blocked with its exact memory or work stop.
- [ ] Exact configurations, seed, source/catalog identity, compact distribution results and a `ready` or blocked verdict are committed under `docs/`. Small route/crossing panels remain isolated regression fixtures and are not substituted for the production-distribution gate. No server access or long training occurs without renewed user authorization.

- [ ] Configure a finite candidate-generation attempt budget separately from requested output count for each size band and partition. The current `max(count, 16) × 128` rule yielded only 8 upper-band validation candidates for an 8-case request in one local seed, all pipe-containing, and other seeds selected only 1–2 belt-only cases. Calibrate upper held-out effort from accepted-pool and quota-hit rates across multiple predetermined seeds. Do not increase requested output count merely to obtain more attempts, select a lucky seed as the sole proof, relax the 4/4 transport floor, or substitute smaller graphs. Preserve the old default for callers without quotas. Exhaustion reports the exact missing stratum, attempts, accepted identities, and observed pool mix; a quota-compliant fixed held-out set must be reproducible within a stated finite effort cap.

## Proposed Changes

Extend the existing curriculum configuration and panel preparation, without a second generator, to describe a stratified per-generation mixture over production size bands. Extend `ProblemGenerator::sample_facility_range` with a bounded, deterministic *joint* quota-aware selection over its existing generated candidate pool before the current balancing step discards candidates. The selector receives size subrange, terminal tail, connection tail, transport and logical-degree quotas together; overlapping labels may satisfy multiple floors, and exhaustion names the unsatisfied quota and accepted-pool counts. Preserve the partition identity split, uniqueness, attempt bound and existing default selector for callers without quotas. Declare nonzero transport and logical-degree quotas where the production generator can supply them; if the candidate pool cannot meet a quota, fail with that category and realized pool counts rather than substituting a smaller graph. Keep promotion as a skill metric while ensuring that configured exposure to later bands does not depend on reaching a small-band completion threshold. Record the actual sampled band, graph signature and pattern stratum for each episode. Prepare a fixed held-out panel with matching declared coverage and distinct identities; keep the held-out set out of the training update path. Treat physical-area tails and actual crossing/component/repair exposure as separate post-placement collection and quota-aware selection requirements in both partitions, because the unplaced generator cannot guarantee a spatial opportunity. Retain enough bounded placed candidates to select a compliant set; the final train and held-out selections must satisfy the logical size, load and topology quotas together with physical-area and component quotas, then recheck the same distinct identities and partition split. If legal placed cases or usable targets are too scarce, report the missing physical quota and block readiness rather than substituting a smaller geometry.

Derive or explicitly configure placement bounds and resource allowances per size band using small bounded CPU probes and conservative measured headroom. Bind the band key to an effective `hierarchical_search::Config` and native `Limits` for each case inside the existing collection path; keep one shared lower/outer weight set, with variable spatial extents handled explicitly in inference and training updates. Version the panel, report and resume identity for this binding. Replace the current single-domain equality checks with validation that each case's effective domain matches its collection record and the shared model's weight/channel definitions. In `hierarchical_generation`, reconstruct every session with the case's limits and derive action features, normalized quality, macro inputs, lower targets and edge offsets from the same case's effective bounds and candidate config. Verify eager and compiled inference at multiple band extents or explicitly disable compilation through a recorded per-band setting when parity cannot be established. If a large band exceeds local search or memory limits, report the unresolved scale requirement and leave readiness blocked rather than shrinking the production target. Keep diagnostic small runs cheap by retaining their separate configuration. Add a CLI-visible distribution audit for already prepared panels, with summary counts only, and use it as a required local admission check for the final readiness plan. This step does not change the game topology contract or claim model quality.

Treat process-wide memory as a separate measured limit from each request's allowance. The configured singleton batch size can hold several requests at once; account their simultaneous inputs, retained sessions, model outputs and publication buffers in the upper-band smoke. Also measure retained collection records and peak activation/optimizer memory during actual lower and outer updates with upper-band inputs. Set finite batch sizes or use bounded accumulation that preserves every declared size × pattern optimizer quota without cropping. Retain the measured process peaks and any stop reason in the readiness report. Derive within-band subrange and physical-kind labels from each exact input/target pair submitted to an optimizer, and fail readiness when a sampled tail disappears during placement, search, target filtering or replay selection.

Carry immutable graph-scale provenance and example-local observed pattern labels into root records and lower route-choice targets; recompute each selected example’s labels from the same state, action and target used by its model update. Replace the current contiguous capped `outer_epoch_examples` rotation with deterministic quota-aware selection over usable records for every declared size × pattern stratum; reserve the required identities first, then fill spare capacity, and apply replay only within the selected stratum's allocation. Apply the same rule to lower route-choice batches. Recheck the quotas against the exact selected examples passed to each optimizer, reporting collected target counts, selected unique identities, per-stratum counts and overlapping labels separately. A stratum without route-choice targets must fail the lower-exposure gate, not receive fabricated labels or be counted because it appeared in the prepared panel.

The quota-enabled sampler receives an explicit maximum-attempts value per band and partition, separate from output count. It uses the existing production generation loop and retains the current count-derived default for callers that do not request quotas. Select the quota-compliant subset from the accepted unique candidate pool. Calibrate upper held-out effort on predetermined seeds and report attempts, accepted identities and belt/pipe/branch mix when a quota fails; do not expand the candidate pool by raising the requested output count or silently changing seeds. This closes the specific upper validation-partition shortage observed in the local quota probe.
