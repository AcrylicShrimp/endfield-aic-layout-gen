## Proposed Changes

Add `operations/declarative_quality_study.py`, a fixed experiment JSON and focused
aggregation/ranking tests. Implement the exact eight-profile, 256-update development
and 480-update confirmation allocation in the design (736 total). Generate two
new 32-factory panels with fixed namespaces distinct from previous panels; accept
by generation/native validity, never observed editing performance. Bind panel files
and freeze confirmation access until the selection receipt is written. Use normal
run/evaluate/report functionality, packed models and workers. Share original-parent
development evaluation once and do not relabel historical panel reports.

Branch continuation and the paired second stochastic replicate use plan 07.
Replicate-two sampler/shuffle seeds are fixed in the experiment JSON before running.
Synchronize actual new fresh files into the shared experiment bank after each
sequential arm; compare hashes on existing files and preserve inherited plans
privately. Record global slot identity independently of the action/shuffle RNG.
Keep the normal checkpoint cadence; retain required parent/endpoints and actual
input evidence. Add only compact quality-allocation aggregates, not full-rollout
archives or a duplicate recovery journal.

The selected two profiles are the two highest mean development endpoint quality
among nonbaseline arms, including negative outcomes. Baseline always confirms.
Persist selection before confirmation evaluation; do not replace candidates after
seeing confirmation. Rank final mean confirmation quality across the two paired
training replicates. Include whole-geometry stratified bootstrap intervals and
per-stratum tradeoffs; do not label correlated single-parent repeats independent
seeds. If numerical outcomes differ but uncertainty is large, preserve the observed
ranking and report uncertainty. Only numerical equality uses the declared tie
rule. Never invent a five-point hurdle or assert baseline superiority.

Bound total time to 14,400 seconds including setup/evaluation/finalization,
736 new updates and 12 GiB added artifacts, plus unchanged parent resource/native
limits. Stop and retain partial checkpoints at hard caps, missing sources or
worker failure. Use a conservative ledger of new owned files/inputs and shared
inodes for total added storage, rather than multiplying old hardlinked parent
files by the number of branches. No automatic extension or main resume.
