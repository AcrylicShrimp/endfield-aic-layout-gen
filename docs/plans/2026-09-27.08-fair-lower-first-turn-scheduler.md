## Goal and Scope

Give direct, network and repair lower tasks equal, fixed first opportunities over all 16 cumulative work categories and active time after repair scope preparation. A temporary stop retains the exact stage and gives the partner its opportunity; the original full request cap remains the hard limit. This step governs scheduling and page windows, not the internal legality rules of prerequisite cursors.

## Completion Criteria

- [ ] After shared repair scope, snapshot one fixed half-remainder ceiling for each of 16 categories and active time; direct and repair first turns use the same quotas without re-halving. Network member stages follow their owner task quota.
- [ ] Guidance input, batched inference, route/repair preparation, search, native replay, cost and publication are inside the temporary windows. A soft stop preserves exact progress and hard stops remain typed unknown.
- [ ] PageWindow suspends active time while another task prepares or waits for shared inference. Uncancellable inference batches require measured upper-scale soft-time admission for every participating task; batch partition or temporary yield preserves prepared inputs.
- [ ] Under the checked-in 6-action cap and default hard request caps, a local direct/repair fixture shows both first opportunities, including a successful later repair, without repeated work or false no-action verdict.
- [ ] On varied, physically valid local cases, measure the direct/network/repair first-turn scheduling cost and work across all 16 categories with the actual page credit; hard stops remain typed. The broad distribution plan separately checks generated and placed size/pattern coverage, both models' selected examples and default hard caps, and the final local readiness plan combines that evidence with the complete CLI workflow. This scheduler cannot claim those later gates passed.

## Proposed Changes

Complete the WIP `PageWindow` and `LowerPager` stage ownership around the reviewed publication progress from Plan .10. At task selection, hold its pending page and owned reservations; suspend its active clock before moving to another task. Before a shared inference batch, resume participating windows and check each against a measured conservative batch time bound; partition or yield if one cannot fit. For every page, advance one bounded stage under the same temporary work/time ceilings; on soft stop retain its cursor. After both first turns, remove only temporary ceilings. The ordinary hard `RequestAllowance` remains unchanged. Calibration at representative local upper size is a completion gate, not a hard cap on smaller legal tasks.
