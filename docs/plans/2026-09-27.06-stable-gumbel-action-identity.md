## Goal and Scope

Bind Gumbel root shortlist membership, sampled noise, forced visits, recorded action lineage and training targets to immutable physical action IDs while placement and lower pages append and sort actions. Later actions can enter a new root budget after reroot, while the current root eligibility set stays fixed. This step owns the consumer-side identity contract through record admission and training examples; Plan .05 still owns how lower pages produce and schedule witnesses. A reviewed test supplier must exercise the complete consumer contract here, and Plan .05 must rerun it after its producer cutover.

## Completion Criteria

- [x] The root selection freezes unique physical IDs, initial noise/rank, eligible IDs, initial prior mixture and shortlist before sequential halving. Every forced visit resolves the selected ID against the current edge set after append/sort, never an old vector index; ambiguous duplicate IDs cannot choose another witness.
- [x] Initial route, network and repair witnesses and late appended witnesses of each kind survive through `RootRecord` with their exact action bodies and physical IDs. A late witness has `gumbel_eligible = false` and exactly zero current-root policy target, remains in the outer softmax denominator and still contributes its applicable lower training example. Rerooting may give that witness a new initial rank/eligibility in the next root budget.
- [x] An already exposed route's `RouteUpdate` preserves its physical ID, frozen prior/noise, visits and Q while its final additional provenance and action feature reach both the record and lower examples. Renormalization and reordering after append cannot change the frozen shortlist, forced-visit owner, selected witness or initial-eligible target mass.
- [x] A versioned root record reports shortlist cap, pending lower pages, initial eligible count and exact eligible/late action lineage. Full stored action witnesses and required per-action eligibility flags identify the physical IDs; compact shortlist evidence records initial rank and noise with each shortlisted physical ID. Admission validates selected ID, uniqueness and Gumbel target classification once at the report load boundary. Old ambiguous search reports are rejected by an explicit schema transition; PUCT records under the new schema remain readable.

## Proposed Changes

Use the existing WIP physical-ID root selection in `hierarchical_search.rs`. At root entry, derive the initial unique-ID/eligibility set and freeze the prior mixture, rank-indexed or action-ID noise assignment and shortlist. Keep compact `(physical ID, initial rank, noise bits)` evidence for shortlisted actions. Resolve each forced visit and final selected action against current edges by ID after append/reorder; preserve all existing edge visits/Q and frozen priors when scoring newly published actions. The append boundary receives real route/network/repair actions and route provenance updates from a controlled lower-supplier fixture; it must preserve the full witness and reject or semantically merge an ambiguous duplicate before selection. Retain existing `gumbel_eligible` and `policy_target` per action in `RootRecord`; their marked full action bodies are the persisted eligible physical-ID set, without a second dense copy of those IDs. Add selected-ID and compact shortlist evidence to the record, then bump the search report schema. The loader validates root identity/eligibility/zero-late-target invariants once when admitting a versioned report; the trainer does not re-audit every example or epoch.

For lower examples, inspect both `Candidate::provenance` and `additional_provenance` after RouteUpdate. Validate every model version and head against the collection checkpoint and model configuration; retain one row per distinct `(physical action, guidance head)`. A route discovered by multiple heads contributes its existing visit weight once in total, divided equally among its distinct heads, so provenance merging cannot multiply that physical action's preference mass. If every candidate in a group has zero visits, retain the group and all guided head rows but give it no **policy** supervision: use zero policy weights and omit that group from the policy-loss average. The existing per-row Q regression and episode-outcome terms remain as they were for the previously admitted single zero-visit row. This makes absence of visit evidence explicit instead of training an invented uniform preference. The root record still includes late actions with zero **outer** target and includes them in the outer softmax denominator. The complete dense ID/prior maps remain a bounded parity path only; Plan .03's streamed catalog must replace them before any upper-scale claim. No runtime distribution audit, extra per-visit output validation or smaller action cap is introduced.
