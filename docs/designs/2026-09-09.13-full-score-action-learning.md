## Comparison contract

Run two independent 256-update arms from the same parent weights, with fresh Adam
0.0003, gradient clip 0.5 and seed 17 on CPU, unchanged depth two and width 32:

- **Best mass imitation:** minimize negative log probability mass of all tied best
  collected actions over the complete ordinary action domain. This teaches a
  demonstrated choice; nonteacher actions are not certified invalid or impossible.
- **Best versus measured:** choose one tied best and one completed strictly worse
  collected action on the same root, then apply pairwise logistic loss. This arm
  explicitly orders successful area and wiring edits, and does not directly
  supervise unmeasured actions.

Use the same seeded shuffled round-robin training-root schedule in both arms.
Precompute the exact target IDs and pair choices before starting updates. Do not
rebalance by positive kind or select the single wiring reroute for equal exposure.
Cache original observation arrays within 128 MiB. Bound the whole study to 900
seconds; keep partial reports and latest optimizer/RNG checkpoints if interrupted.

Evaluate each final arm at T=1 and T=0.05 on the unchanged twelve roots, two parent
sampling banks, eight selections, 30-second episodes and 5000 route expansions per
action. Keep the history mask and incumbent score priority. This produces 96 new
episodes. Reuse parent, geometry and binary-ranking controls with explicit hashes
and equal episode-budget checks. Report full-score paired outcomes, family area
reductions, selected action kinds, actual improving poses/reroutes, teacher top-one
membership and probability mass, deadline completion, and measured runtime.

Independently validate all retained bests against the original graph and full metrics.
Replay learned improving pose/reroute trajectories before claiming that capability;
best-only validation does not prove which action produced it. Inspect full-layout
views of any material relocation result. Persist concise findings and provenance;
generated traces, witnesses and final optimizer/RNG checkpoints remain ignored.

## Interpretation

Compare equal-budget results without promoting a checkpoint based only on teacher
accuracy or loss. This adaptive opened-family screen is not sealed evaluation.
Improvement in training examples alone does not complete larger-factory, unopened
family, bounded production CLI or the original Heavy Xiranite 1%/visual gates.
Regressing or uninformative arms should change the next hypothesis rather than
receive an automatic longer run.
