# Executor-selected transactional route regions

## Status and scope

Implementation authorized on 2026-09-10. The learner selects the facilities and
their target poses. The executor selects a bounded set of routes to repair and
its fixed external contacts. This replaces the native pilot's one-pass repair;
it does not add policy output dimensions, change the reward, or replace the
constructive initial planner. The first experiment is exactly 32 fresh PPO
batches compared with batches 0/8/16/24/32 of the retained compact one-hour run.

## Input and output contract

Input: a complete validated witness, runtime catalogs, a selected edit and one
shared route-work allowance. The edit contains the selected target geometry and
any explicit port/component/reroute operation. A versioned repair-limits DTO
bounds attempts, additional chains, alternative ports and crossings per route.
Defaults are eight attempts, all chains in the admitted witness as the possible
initial repair set, eight additional chains, four port trials and eight crossings
per route. A caller may explicitly set a smaller chain cap; reaching it reports
unfinished search. At most two blocking chains are added per retry. All attempts
share the caller's original route-work allowance. The original 64-chain default
was removed in the 2026-09-27 route-stop revision because it could discard a
supported edit before the shared route-work allowance was used. The original
four-attempt default was raised to eight after a retained 59-facility panel
found one additional candidate at the same route-work allowance; repeated
retry states are skipped. The explicit maximum is 32 attempts.

Output: an `Execution` containing either one complete candidate or no witness,
the aggregate charged route expansions, route reports and a versioned region
report. The report identifies the initial/expanded chain sets, fixed contacts,
attempt ordering and port changes, blockers, work spent, and termination reason.
Full-factory validation and the existing episode acceptance policy remain the
only commit boundary. The pilot may accept valid exploratory worsening; the
production improver's best-layout acceptance remains unchanged.

## Region and ownership

The initial region includes routes incident to moved endpoints, routes intersected
by target bodies/carried geometry, and explicitly selected routes. A region is a
set of directed chains, not a rectangular crop. Whole chains retain their rates,
commodity, transport and original fixed endpoint obligations. Contacts at retained
facilities, external terminals and retained junctions/components remain fixed.
Only selected facility terminals may receive automatic compatible port alternatives.
An explicit port action keeps its requested assignment.

Outside chains retain their geometry. A newly installed catalog-valid bridge on
an outside carrier is reported as a touched contact and preserves that carrier's
direction, commodity and capacity. The first slice does not relocate arbitrary
junctions or outside facilities, resynthesize logical networks, or move external
terminals. Such a required change produces a structured local failure.

## Private repair and expansion

1. Prepare target geometry in private and remove the selected chains from routing
   occupancy. Preserve fixed contacts and their directional arms.
2. Route pipes first. Within a layer use a deterministic difficulty order, with
   stable IDs as the final tie-break. Apply a per-route crossing allowance; the
   former two-new-bridges-for-the-entire-edit restriction is removed.
   The current difficulty key prioritizes the previously failed chain, then
   decreasing endpoint Manhattan distance, within each transport layer.
3. On failure, use encountered retained-route obstacles to choose bounded
   additional chains. Retry the expanded set from the untouched original state,
   with the failed chain prioritized within its transport layer. Automatically
   selected extra chains do not move their outside endpoints.
4. Try bounded compatible port alternatives on selected facilities and alternative
   route order where applicable. Each retry starts from the same original state;
   partial routes/components never leak across failed attempts.
5. Stop on a complete candidate or a work/attempt/region/port bound. Charge every
   path search, including failed attempts, to the original work allowance.

The current measured extent remains this pilot edit's routing neighborhood so
external-boundary semantics are preserved. It is not a game limit or production
construction canvas. Existing explicit strip insertion remains available.

## Diagnostics and failure modes

Use stable `region-*` codes at stage `local-region-repair`, with severity, message
and relevant chains/contacts. Distinguish exhausted work, exhausted attempts,
additional-chain bound, fixed obstacle/contact, invalid edit, and incomplete
repair. Existing precise movement/path diagnostics are retained within attempts.
No failure means global factory infeasibility. The report must allow callers to
distinguish success on the initial region from success after expansion/port/order
repair, and to observe unchanged current/best state after a rejection.
