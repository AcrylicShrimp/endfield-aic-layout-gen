## One question

Under a fixed computation budget, can a learned policy complete placement-and-routing edits
that reduce the area of held-out small, complete factories more than its untrained counterpart?

Completion is necessary but not sufficient. Keeping the initial factory always gives a valid
output; a policy that never improves it has not answered this question positively. Performance
against random and simple search controls determines whether any learned improvement is useful.
This pilot cannot establish arbitrary-factory generalization, the full Heavy Xiranite goal,
or optimality. The existing product score order and final product requirements remain intact.

## Minimal first comparison card

The following is a concrete candidate for discussion, not a frozen run configuration. Its
purpose is to identify the first useful problem in the combination. Change it when needed;
record the change and the claim the replacement comparison can support.

| Question/hypothesis | Observe on fixed roots/checkpoints | Possible next question if weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The action interface permits learnable complete edits. | New validated draft completions, command rejections, pending obligations, and unfinished episode tails. An unchanged root does not count as a new completion. | Are edits too many decisions long, or are the observations/choices too difficult? Scripted success distinguishes expressibility from discovery but not these causes. |
| Best-area reward supplies useful learning signal. | Fraction of episodes/updates with task reward, area-improving completions, and fixed-root quality curves. | Compare a different edit granularity or justified credit mechanism; do not infer model capacity from zero rewards alone. |

| Improvement transfers beyond training layouts. | Same-protocol training-root versus held-out-family quality, with all seed results and admission strata. | Investigate distribution/representation limits rather than reporting training fit as generalization. |

These observations do not uniquely diagnose a cause. They determine the next controlled question;
the pilot does not need to certify a final architecture or reach a product-release threshold.

per-command expansion/validation guards explicit and compare actual charged work and time.

For this candidate, pipe/mixed-transport and cyclic-production coverage, large factories,
additional encoders, demonstrations, and reward variants are deferred. They are future
experiments, not features that must all be implemented before this pilot. Branches/merges and
independent belt crossings remain representable; record their actual presence by data group.

### Remove interface-only choices from this candidate

Propose seven model commands: placements, terminal unbinding, terminal binding, transport erasure,
anchor-explicit path extension, explicit connection, and discard. Fold `START_ROUTE` into the
extension arguments and remove `FINISH_EDIT`. The engine automatically checks completion after
an applied geometry/binding edit, charges that work to the edit, and records any new valid
working base/best result. It never chooses paths or repairs the geometry.

This removes a separate cursor-selection command/state and a learned completion declaration.
Each extension now selects its anchor again; fewer command types do not guarantee an easier
parameter-selection problem. It does not remove geometry/attachment decisions or solve delayed
credit. Automatic checking may cost more;
measure it before adding optimized completion gates. Failed completion leaves the draft editable,
while an immediately invalid mechanical command changes no geometry. A valid worse draft can
become the next working base; best-result retention is independent. This reduced interface and
automatic rollback-boundary update are themselves hypotheses that may be replaced.

## Recommended starting configuration

These are agent recommendations for one falsifiable comparison, not claims that the combination
is optimal or commitments to the final system. Specify settings for one comparison and keep
them stable while interpreting that comparison. A deliberate change is a recorded intervention
or new comparison, not prohibited redesign. Do not combine changing architectures, rewards,
action domains, and datasets under one unchanged learning-curve label.

| Part | Proposed default | Why it belongs in this first comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task | Small complete factories with original production obligations preserved. | Includes actual placement and routing while keeping failures inspectable. |
| Observation | Whole draft geometry plus explicit facility, port, terminal, network, and pending-obligation information; include working-base state, incumbent score, and remaining budgets. | Avoids making local-crop ambiguity another initial variable. |
| Encoder | A small CNN for grid geometry plus a small relational/object encoder for the explicit entities and connections. | Supplies both spatial and connectivity information; depth/width and fusion remain to be specified. No initial CNN/GAT/Transformer sweep. |
| Output | Seven conditional typed commands from the parent design, including anchor-explicit path segments and attachment targets. No separate route-start or finish action. | Preserves model ownership of geometry while reducing interface-only decisions. |
| Environment | Derive logistics components, maintain the private draft, and automatically check completion after applied edits. | Removes redundant component/completion decisions without automatic path selection. All checking work is charged. |
| Flow | Observe immutable demand/supply and normalized capacities; the engine certifies rates on the exact selected geometry. No model-emitted flow values. | Keeps production requirements exact while avoiding another continuous action parameter. Unresolved draft loads are marked unknown, not zero. |
| Learning baseline | PPO with a value baseline, trained from fresh weights on on-policy interactions. | Provides one established policy-gradient baseline to assess this environment; it is not evidence of sparse-reward suitability. |
| Task reward | Relative best-valid area improvement, with an undiscounted fixed-horizon objective. | A clearly scoped relationship between reward and returned area. Lower-priority routing goals are measured but not trained by this reward. |
| Horizon | One fixed command horizon for training and the primary evaluation. | Avoids confounding a learning comparison with changed rollout length. Exact T depends on a declared useful-edit envelope and environment calibration, not an arbitrary short wall-clock cap. |
| Demonstrations | Authored sequences for environment checks only; no imitation, expert pretraining, or inherited trajectories in this first RL comparison. | Separates the mechanical check from evidence that the policy discovers improvements. |

Reference for the proposed optimizer: [Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347).
That paper does not establish the right architecture, allocation, or success probability here.
If PPO is selected for the first pilot, specify the relevant value settings, conditional action
log-probability/mask handling, and update diagnostics for that scope. PPO, its input encoder,
reward, horizon, and even this task formulation remain replaceable. No dependency choice is made here.

## Objective and primary measurements

Let `A0` be the initial complete area and `Bt` the best validated area after command t. Proposed
task reward is `(B(t-1) - Bt) / A0`, with discount factor one and a true finite horizon. The return
is `(A0 - BT) / A0`. Only successfully completed, independently validated edits update best.
Do not reward incomplete drafts, component count, removed tiles, short edit scripts, or merely
passing automatic validation. Invalid/no-op commands consume budget without erasing the incumbent.

This area-only learning objective is a pilot limitation. Export selection still compares the
complete lexicographic area/transport/turn tuple; routing tradeoffs and visual quality remain in
the report. An area-learning result is not satisfaction of every product objective. No penalty
is added for a valid temporary worsening. The main risk is long stretches of zero task reward;
report this directly instead of claiming that changed weights imply useful learning.

Primary comparison: final trained checkpoint versus its paired untrained initialization on the
same held-out roots and predeclared rollout seeds, using final relative area reduction. Plot fixed
checkpoint results on a frozen development set; the final checkpoint is specified in advance,
not selected from evaluation outcomes. Compare independently under matched work and matched
elapsed-time envelopes, recording any difference in effective command exposure.

With resources available, include a predeclared cumulative training-budget ladder in each
independent seed's single uninterrupted study. Keep architecture, reward, episode horizon,
sampling distribution, and controls unchanged across checkpoints. Record optimizer/RNG/data-stream
state needed for continuation. Fix checkpoint allocations and the final endpoint before training;
do not reset a learner at each checkpoint and call the results a cumulative learning curve.
Evaluate all checkpoints on the fixed development set; reserve the primary held-out comparison
for the predeclared final checkpoint. A short early null result is not a conclusion that the
interface cannot learn. Resource/time interruption is recorded as incomplete, not a negative
result at the planned endpoint. A flat finite curve is not a convergence or impossibility proof.

Also report:

- strict complete-factory improvement frequency and exact score tuples;
- successful edit closures, including closures that change nothing or worsen the working base;
- rejected commands, discarded work, and unfinished drafts at termination;
- terminal/capacity/topology failure categories and task-reward frequency;
- command counts, expanded cells, decoded assignments, validation work, inference and mask time;
- seed-level and factory-group-level variation, with the declared aggregation and uncertainty method;
- direct geometry inspection of preselected examples and failures/outliers under a fixed rule.

Controls share the same action semantics: retain-initial, untrained policy, random policy, and
a simple bounded search over edit sequences. Search cannot receive an uncharged repair router
or expert answer; its candidate generation, validation, and branching work are counted. Specify
its exact neighborhood and sequence depth before the comparison, since one-command greedy
search may be unable to finish an edit that the learned policy attempts across many commands.

## Verdicts and limits of interpretation

- `invalid-experiment`: original-request checks, data isolation, work accounting, or replay
  integrity fail. Do not interpret the learning comparison until the measurement issue is fixed.
- `learned-signal`: the predeclared comparison supports a positive held-out trained-versus-untrained
  advantage, with a practically meaningful effect and uncertainty reported. Whether it also beats
  random/search is a separate required comparison; this label alone is not product readiness.
- `no-observed-advantage`: the completed allocation does not show the declared advantage. This
  applies to the recorded setup and budget, not every possible model or learning allocation.
- `inconclusive`: variation, failed admission, interrupted allocation, or insufficient data prevents
  the registered interpretation. Do not force a binary success/failure verdict from a point estimate.

The confidence method, primary aggregation, any useful-effect threshold, and multiplicity treatment
remain to be registered before training or evaluation results exist. Seed/root counts above are
proposals until the run configuration is recorded. A diagnostic pilot need not use a product-release
threshold; it must still report effect size and uncertainty rather than only a favorable point estimate.
Training seeds are independent learning replications; many rollout seeds from one trained model
do not replace them. Do not silently increase the allocation after seeing a disappointing curve.
