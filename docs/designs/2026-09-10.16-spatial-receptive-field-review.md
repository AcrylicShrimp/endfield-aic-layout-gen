# Spatial receptive field review

## Decision

The user's concern is a strong, testable hypothesis: local spatial context may be
too narrow relative to facility footprints and surrounding routing space. Give a
controlled spatial-reach screen priority over the previously proposed width and
attention screen. Eight convolution layers are a reasonable candidate, not a
deduced minimum or an established solution. This review changes the experiment
proposal only; no architecture or training behavior is changed.

## Confirmed representation and sampling

At source `4adef57`, one raster position represents one game grid cell. A 3 by 3
facility paints nine positions in the facility-occupancy channel, not one entity
pixel. `WorldEncoder` uses two stride-one, dilation-one 3 by 3 convolutions without
downsampling. The configurable policy `depth` controls graph message layers only.

The external normalized facility catalog contains six 3 by 3 records, thirteen
5 by 5 records, eight 6 by 4 records and one 6 by 5 record. These are catalog
records, not frequencies in training or a claim about all possible game entities.

Spatial feature reads are asymmetric:

- A facility graph node samples the CNN at placement `(x,y)`, its body origin.
- A pose action samples a feature at the target group envelope origin.
- Each moved member additionally samples the CNN at its target body center.
- Port, terminal and transport-cell graph nodes provide other spatial samples;
  messages can propagate their information through the graph.

Consequently an origin sample on a 5 by 5 facility covers only part of that body
through its local convolution path. A center sample on a 3 by 3 body covers the
body plus a one-cell border with a 5 by 5 window; on a 5 by 5 body it covers no
outside border. Target samples read the current world at the proposed destination,
not a pre-rendered candidate factory. Group-member pooling combines several reads
but does not guarantee a detailed representation of their shared surroundings.

Sources: `crates/aic-pilot-native/src/full_factory/observation.rs` (`world`,
`candidates`), `full_factory_input_contracts.py` (`WorldEncoder`, policy `forward`),
`ppo_training_world.py`, and `data/game/normalized/facilities.json`.

## What the receptive-field arithmetic does and does not imply

For stride-one 3 by 3 convolutions with dilation sequence d, the local convolution
path has receptive-field side length `1 + 2 * sum(d)`. Thus two ordinary layers
give 5, and eight give 17. This follows the standard receptive-field recurrence.
[Araujo et al., Distill 2019](https://distill.pub/2019/computing-receptive-fields/).

This is not the entire policy's information boundary. The policy also receives
facility dimensions, poses, coordinates, graph messages, global means and history.
GroupNorm couples positions through spatial statistics. Candidate generation and
native validation already handle parts of legal geometry; the CNN need not infer
every footprint or collision rule from pixels. It is incorrect to conclude that
the current model cannot understand any facility larger than its local window.

Conversely, global means and normalization statistics are not a replacement for
spatially resolved information about nearby walls, ports and unrelated commodity
routes. A graph path communicates connectivity, not automatically every spatial
conflict near a proposed destination. This makes the concern particularly relevant
to predicting rerouting difficulty and useful compaction opportunities.

A nominal 17 by 17 field also does not mean all its positions influence decisions
equally. Effective receptive fields can occupy a smaller, centrally concentrated
part of theoretical support. This is a reason to measure the learned model, not
to assume that exactly eight layers solve long-range reasoning. [Luo et al.,
NeurIPS 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04128).

## Revised first experiment

Hold graph depth at two, width at 32, the existing sampling locations, history,
action domain, PPO configuration, reward and region executor fixed. Initially
change only the actor's spatial encoder; use identical critic architecture and
initial weights, then train each critic normally. This isolates actor spatial
encoding from simultaneous changes in value estimation.

| Actor spatial encoder | Dilation sequence | Local field | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current two convolutions | 1,1 | 5 by 5 | Reference |
| Four convolutions | 1,1,1,1 | 9 by 9 | More local processing |
| Four convolutions | 1,1,2,4 | 17 by 17 | Wider context at the same convolution parameter count as the prior row |
| Eight convolutions | Eight ones | 17 by 17 | More processing with the same theoretical reach as the dilated candidate |

Dilated convolutions expand contextual reach without reducing feature-map
resolution. [Yu and Koltun, ICLR 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.07122).
The proposed sequence retains two dense local layers before increasing dilation.
Its combined theoretical support is dense, but its computation paths and learned
weighting differ from eight ordinary layers. Equal kernel multiply counts do not
guarantee equal latency. Keep residual/normalization choices identical between
the two four-layer variants and declare them before measuring quality. Identity
residuals are suitable candidates for the added blocks; do not simultaneously
change normalization statistics as an unreported treatment.

Do not use downsampling alone as the first solution: one-cell corridors and
directional arms matter. A later multiscale branch can preserve a full-resolution
path while adding coarse context. Likewise center/footprint/perimeter pooling
deserves a separate sampling experiment; changing it simultaneously would obscure
whether wider convolutional reach helped.

Before PPO, verify single-state/batched parity and profile the actual largest
retained states. Both `WorldEncoder.forward` and the manually unrolled
`ppo_training_world.encode_worlds` currently assume the two-convolution layout;
changing only the Sequential container is insufficient. Define separate
`cnn_layers`/dilations and `graph_layers` in the next model contract, with checkpoint
identity and the same correctness gates as the architecture-screen proposal.

Use fresh 32-batch paired runs on the frozen starts. Compare outcome and change
from each untrained policy, especially by facility size and for single/group moves
and routed cases. Also compare native rejection categories, useful pose gains,
preparation/inference/update time, memory and KL behavior. A pose validity rate is
conditional on the actions selected, not a direct measure of perception quality.

Interpretation: if four dilated layers beat four ordinary layers, wider spatial
context becomes a stronger explanation, though dilation also changes connectivity
within the network. If eight ordinary layers outperform the dilated four, extra
processing/optimization structure may matter. Neither result alone proves a
universal minimum receptive field. Retain the attention and width experiments as
subsequent, separate hypotheses rather than growing all dimensions together.
