# Live factory performance curves

Status: implemented and exercised for the direct-area study. The PPO observer
uses the same matched-evaluation and verified-mirror contract. See the individual
study reports for completed measurements and limitations.

## Contract

Input: a frozen study protocol, compact training progress and hash-bound completed
evaluation reports. The observer is separate from training and cannot alter
weights, factory draws, action selection or optimizer settings. The original
stopped rank continuation has no running observer and is not resumed here.

Output: PNG/SVG performance plots, curve JSON, file-hash manifest and mirror
status. Refresh at least once per minute while training runs; add measured points
only after an evaluation completes. Plot refresh is not a new measurement.
Record measurement epoch, publication epoch and measurement age at publication.
Stop on the study's terminal status or the observer's explicit time bound.

The primary curve is additional mean area reduction in percentage points against
the same untrained actor on the same evaluation factories. Positive means better.
Recompute from native initial/best scores and require matching complete banks,
request/start hashes and admission outcomes. Show separate seeds, elapsed time
since each seed started (including evaluation/audit) and selected training actions.
Compare algorithms at actual measured checkpoints. A rollout batch and a PPO
minibatch optimizer step are different units and must be labeled accordingly.

Keep exact per-case/per-product differences and full lexicographic score
wins/ties/losses in curve JSON. The PPO view also reports probability ratios,
sampled KL and critic explained variance. None of those training diagnostics can
replace measured factory performance. Preserve all evaluation points, including
regressions. Do not smooth, extrapolate or interpret a small two-seed change as
proof of broad generalization. Historical rank checkpoints exist at 0/16/32;
direct-area and PPO checkpoints are scheduled at 0/8/16/24/32.

Write each artifact atomically and publish its manifest last. The bounded local
SSH mirror downloads into a private temporary directory, verifies all hashes and
then replaces local artifacts, publishing its manifest last. A concurrent server
refresh can cause a hash mismatch; record the transfer failure, retain the last
verified local snapshot and retry. Do not claim a multi-file filesystem transaction.

## Verification

A real-native reduced direct-area run produced three measured evaluations and
multiple intermediate refreshes. The observer terminated with the completed
study, the local mirror verified the final snapshot, and its PNG was visually
inspected. The full study keeps the same observer and mirror running. Generated
plots and raw curve JSON remain under ignored artifacts.

Implementation: `plot_direct_area_study.py`, `plot_ppo_area_study.py` and
`mirror_performance_curve.py` in `tools/factory-edit-pilot/`. Verification of the
PPO observer is recorded with its own experiment, not inferred from this smoke.
