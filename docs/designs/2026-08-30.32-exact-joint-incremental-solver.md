# Exact Joint Incremental Solver Cutover

## Status

Accepted and in implementation. This document supersedes the optimizer architecture in `2026-08-30.31-incremental-scc-optimization-plan.md`.

## Goal

This document defines the current exact-joint experiment. In this experiment, each cumulative SCC phase is one exact joint optimization problem. The solver decides facility placement, rotation, directional port selection, belt and pipe routing, logistics components, and used geometry together. A separate placement-candidate producer or heuristic router may not make authoritative decisions within this experiment.

Search-space explosion is expected baseline evidence. The implementation must expose and measure it before applying only solution-preserving reductions.

Neither joint solving nor iterative SCC growth is a permanent project invariant. They are two separable experimental layers:

- while this experiment is active, the solver contract represents placement and routing together in one complete optimization problem;
- while this experiment is active, the harness tests ways to present, grow, reformulate, warm-start, or schedule that complete search without removing legal solutions or weakening objective quality;
- iterative SCC growth is the first experiment because it may improve incumbent discovery and propagation, not because it is known to work;
- if iterative growth does not help, the harness must make it replaceable without rewriting the domain model, witness validator, or HTML/report pipeline.

The joint solver architecture itself may also be replaced after it has been implemented faithfully, observed for a meaningful period, and shown inadequate by measurements. Such a replacement is a new approved experiment, not an implementation detail or silent fallback. Solver-contract details and harness/orchestration strategies are both expected to change frequently during research. Their module APIs must therefore be narrow, explicit, diagnostic-rich, and replaceable rather than coupled through shared mutable implementation state.

## Current Experiment Contracts

- Routing decisions remain inside the solver model.
- Total route cells are the primary objective and route turns are the second objective.
- Prior phase solutions are solver-native hints, never hard coordinate commitments.
- Caller width and height are per-request hard ceilings only.
- Actual blueprint bounds come from selected geometry.
- No shelf placement, constructive placement, greedy port assignment, routing-order portfolio, greedy path search, cropped neighborhood, or data-specific coordinate rule is allowed.
- A resource-limited exact solve may return `unknown`; it may not invoke a heuristic fallback.
- Every emitted witness is independently validated.

## Current Code Assessment

The exact experiment has been separated from the former mixed-responsibility `integrated.rs` facade. The current Pumpkin formulation jointly represents:

- facility origin and rotation choices;
- directional compatible port choices;
- directed grid arcs and route-cell occupancy;
- flow conservation;
- belt and pipe layer separation;
- facility and route collision exclusion;
- one-cell dangling external routes;
- minimum route length.

Model preparation now groups capacity-split logical requirements by item and transport, preserves every split supply and demand as an independent terminal lane, and converts terminal and line rates to checked exact integer flow units. The exact common denominator, line-capacity units, terminal-lane count, and total terminal units are exposed in model metrics. The current Pumpkin formulation does not consume those network-flow units yet; it still solves the explicitly labeled per-requirement path baseline.

The current formulation remains incomplete research code and lacks required production contracts:

- no exact route-turn or used-geometry objective stages;
- no prior full-solution hints;
- no incremental SCC history integration;
- no physical commodity-network flow formulation replacing route-per-wiring-edge paths;
- no complete joint logistics-component formulation;
- no incumbent-versus-best-bound history across lexicographic objective stages.

The former candidate-policy, incumbent-extension, neighborhood, coordinate-portfolio, and sparse-router authorities have been removed. They are not retained as fallbacks while the exact experiment is incomplete.

## Module Boundary

The cutover uses the modern paired-file Rust module layout.

```text
layouts/
  integrated.rs                 public facade and stable re-exports only
  integrated/
    report.rs                   public result, phase, diagnostic, and metric DTOs
    model.rs                    validated solver input derived from wiring and catalogs
    exact.rs                    replaceable joint-solver contract and entry point
    exact/
      formulation.rs            variables and hard constraints
      objective.rs              proof-preserving lexicographic optimization stages
      extract.rs                solver solution to complete witness
      metrics.rs                model-size and search observations
    harness.rs                  replaceable experiment protocol and common observations
    harness/
      iterative_scc.rs          first experimental cumulative-growth strategy
    witness.rs                  independent witness validation
    html.rs                     automatic phase-history visualization
```

`model.rs`, `witness.rs`, and the report DTOs define domain-facing boundaries. `exact.rs` owns one joint solve. `harness.rs` may call that contract repeatedly, but may not reach into formulation internals or substitute placement/routing decisions. This lets either the Pumpkin formulation or the orchestration experiment be replaced independently.

The following existing areas are reusable contracts:

- recipe and facility-instance wiring;
- SCC growth and cumulative graph projection;
- stable facility, edge, route-requirement, and component identities;
- facility definitions, footprints, rotations, and port geometry;
- item transport classification and transport capacity splitting;
- request-bound validation and facility-area lower bounds;
- canonical geometry calculation;
- witness validation rules;
- localization and HTML rendering infrastructure.

The following existing areas are not reusable optimizer authority:

- placement candidate policies;
- deterministic incumbent extension placement;
- fixed/free LNS neighborhoods;
- routing-order portfolios;
- greedy sparse port assignment and path construction;
- candidate scoring and reduction as the main solve loop;
- coordinate-feasibility and sparse-feasibility CLI strategies.

## Exact Baseline Formulation

### Placement

For each facility instance, the model chooses exactly one legal `(rotation, x, y)` placement within the request ceilings. Every footprint cell is linked to that choice. Selected facility footprints may not overlap.

The first faithful baseline may use enumerated legal placement literals because that representation already has direct correctness evidence. Replacing them with coordinate variables is a later exact reformulation only if it preserves the complete legal placement set.

### Ports

For every route endpoint, the model chooses exactly one compatible port linked to the selected facility placement and rotation.

Compatibility includes:

- facility instance;
- input versus output direction;
- belt versus pipe transport;
- rotated port position and outward edge;
- an in-domain outside-adjacent connection cell.

A port is part of the facility footprint. Its route begins at the outside-adjacent cell in its outward direction. Two facing ports separated by one routing cell may legally share that cell only as the two endpoints of the same route.

### Routing

The capacity-split wiring edges define terminal supply and demand; they do not define a fixed physical source-to-target pairing. Edges with the same item and transport kind are normalized into one fungible commodity network. The solver chooses the physical flow allocation for that network. Treating each logical edge as an independently routed path is only the faithful baseline and must be removed during the complete-routing cutover because it can exclude legal shared trunks and alternative producer-to-consumer pairings.

Normalization must not aggregate two capacity-split lanes at one logical node into one oversized terminal. Every split lane remains an independently selectable terminal connection whose rate is at most one line capacity. Requirement IDs identify diagnostic provenance only; they do not pair one supply lane with one demand lane inside the physical solver network.

For each commodity network, convert every terminal rate to exact integer flow units using a checked common denominator. Each directed grid arc has an integer flow variable and a channel-use Boolean. Each grid cell has directional channel incidence derived from those arcs. Constraints enforce:

- exact supply or demand at every selected facility/external terminal;
- zero flow imbalance at ordinary transport cells;
- `channel_used <= flow <= line_capacity * channel_used` on every directed channel after exact integer scaling, so no zero-flow geometry is emitted;
- at most one direction on a plain physical channel;
- one physical channel may carry only one item, although its flow may serve multiple terminals in that item network;
- a complete flow decomposition from all sources to all sinks without fixing logical edge pairings;
- no disconnected active component or circulation;
- facility exclusion;
- belt and pipe layer independence;
- same-layer occupancy and legal crossing/component rules;
- hard request ceilings.

The authoritative witness is a set of physical commodity networks, not a list of independently routed logical wiring edges. Each network records its item, transport kind, selected terminals, placed components, directed channel segments, and exact segment flow. Logical wiring requirement IDs remain diagnostic references on the network, but do not prescribe physical source-to-target paths.

During the hard cutover, the old `IntegratedRoute` witness is removed rather than retained as a compatibility projection. The HTML renderer, score calculation, used-geometry calculation, and independent validator consume the physical network witness directly.

The faithful per-route baseline still enforces the following properties until that cutover is complete:

- the chosen source and target port cells;
- flow conservation at every cell;
- exactly one connected simple source-to-target path;
- no disconnected active component or circulation;
- no active zero-flow route geometry;
- facility exclusion;
- belt and pipe layer independence;
- same-layer occupancy and legal crossing/component rules;
- hard request ceilings.

Connectivity must be structural. It may not rely on the route-length objective to make cycles unattractive because a time-limited feasible incumbent must also be valid.

### Logistics Components

Every splitter, converger, bridge, or other required logistics component is represented by solver variables and constraints before the solver path is declared feature-complete. Components may not be inserted after solving to repair an otherwise invalid route witness.

For every transport layer and cell, the solver may select at most one component topology and one allowed rotation:

- a plain cell has one incoming and one outgoing channel with equal item and flow;
- a splitter has one allowed incoming direction and two or three selected allowed outgoing directions; total outgoing flow equals incoming flow and does not exceed component capacity;
- a converger has two or three selected allowed incoming directions and one allowed outgoing direction; total incoming flow equals outgoing flow and does not exceed component capacity;
- a bridge has two independent perpendicular straight channels; each channel conserves its own item and flow and the channels never merge;
- component direction sets are the catalog directions after the selected rotation;
- a selected component occupies its cell, participates in used geometry, and is emitted directly from solver variables.

Component selection is never derived from a deterministic pre-bundling pass. Branch membership, component position, topology, rotation, and channel flow are all solver decisions. Exact presolve may remove only values proven impossible by the same contract and must report the reduction.

### Used Geometry

Selected facility cells, belt cells, pipe cells, and logistics-component cells determine exact minimum and maximum used coordinates. Translation symmetry is removed only through an exact canonical condition equivalent to translating used geometry to `min_x = 0` and `min_y = 0`.

The request ceilings never become reported footprint dimensions.

## Exact Objective

The objective is lexicographic:

```text
1. total route cells
2. total route turns
3. used bounding-box area
4. maximum used side
5. physical transport tiles
6. logistics-component count
7. moved prior facilities
8. prior-facility Manhattan displacement
9. rotation changes
```

No unchecked weighted approximation may replace this ordering. An implementation may use proof-gated sequential objective stages or another exact encoding with proven safe bounds. If a deadline expires before a primary stage is proven, later stages may not worsen the best primary objective merely to improve a tie-breaker.

## First Harness Experiment: Incremental SCC Growth

Incremental SCC growth is a testable orchestration hypothesis, not a permanent solver invariant. Its hypothesis is that a sequence of smaller complete joint solves can provide useful incumbents and solver-native hints for an enlarged complete joint solve without binding previous coordinates or routes.

For each cumulative phase:

1. Build the complete cumulative wiring graph.
2. Build the full exact joint model for that graph.
3. Translate the previous phase's complete exact solution into hints for every stable placement, rotation, port, route, and component variable that still exists.
4. Solve the complete enlarged problem under the phase resource budget.
5. Retain every complete independently validated solver incumbent exposed by the exact solve.
6. Append the authoritative phase result and model metrics to history.
7. Expand the logical SCC frontier and repeat.

There are no fixed placement neighborhoods and no separately routed placement candidates. The final cumulative phase already contains the full graph; remaining budget may continue exact optimization from its best complete solution.

The experiment must report enough evidence to compare it with alternative harnesses, including a direct full-graph solve. Failure to improve construction time, first-incumbent time, incumbent quality, or bound progress is a valid negative result and a reason to replace the harness. No iterative-phase decision may become a hard restriction on the final full-graph legal solution set.

## Diagnostic Contract

Model construction and solver search are separate report stages. Each phase records, when available:

- cumulative SCC, facility, route, and logistics-network counts;
- placement, endpoint, route-cell, route-arc, connectivity, component, and objective variable counts;
- hard-constraint counts by category;
- construction elapsed time;
- search elapsed time;
- complete incumbent count;
- incumbent lexicographic objective;
- solver best bound for the active objective stage;
- active objective stage and proof status;
- termination reason;
- peak resource observations available from the runtime;
- independently validated witness status.

Model construction failure, search timeout, cancellation, resource exhaustion, infeasibility proof, and unproven `unknown` are distinct outcomes.

The CLI writes HTML automatically for success and for every failure containing at least one completed phase or complete solver incumbent.

## Cutover Slices

### Slice 0: Contract And Module Extraction

- mark the prior optimizer plan superseded;
- move report DTOs and validated model preparation out of `integrated.rs` without behavior changes;
- establish the thin facade and exact-module boundary;
- preserve all existing tests.

### Slice 1: Faithful Exact Baseline

- implement the new exact formulation from domain contracts rather than copying the prototype;
- jointly solve placement, rotation, ports, route cells, and directed arcs;
- add structural simple-path connectivity;
- add model construction metrics;
- independently validate every returned incumbent.

### Slice 1A: Authority Hard Cut

- make the exact experiment the only integrated solve entry point before performance measurement;
- remove candidate placement policies, deterministic shelf warm starts, incumbent extension, neighborhood routing, coordinate portfolios, and sparse routing authority;
- remove obsolete CLI strategy switches and public library exports;
- keep the legacy report projection temporarily until the physical commodity-network report cutover;
- do not interpret this authority cut as making joint solving a permanent architecture.

### Slice 1B: Physical Network Witness Hard Cut

- replace the public route-per-requirement witness with item-specific physical transport networks;
- record selected terminals, occupied cells, directed segments, exact segment rates, logical requirement references, and logistics-component references;
- move witness validation, score calculation, used bounds, JSON output, and HTML rendering to the network contract;
- label networks projected from the temporary per-requirement formulation so they cannot be mistaken for solver-selected shared flow;
- generate diagnostic HTML even when exact search returns no geometry.

### Slice 2: Complete Routing Mechanics

- add exact belt/pipe occupancy rules and all required logistics components;
- prove no disconnected active route geometry can be emitted;
- add chain, branch, capacity-lane, cycle, crossing, and one-cell-gap tests.

### Slice 3: Exact Objective And Hints

- implement proof-preserving lexicographic objective stages;
- add full prior-solution Pumpkin warm starts;
- prove hints do not alter feasibility or objective semantics;
- expose incumbent and best-bound history.

### Slice 4: Iterative SCC Hard Cutover

- introduce the replaceable harness protocol;
- implement `iterative_scc.rs` around one full joint solve per cumulative graph;
- connect automatic HTML history;
- verify that removed heuristic optimizer paths have not re-entered as fallbacks;
- remove the remaining obsolete phase and candidate-score DTO fields.

### Slice 5: Exact Search Baseline

- run the real production graph incrementally;
- record where model construction or search first becomes intractable;
- preserve `unknown` as the truthful result;
- do not add a workaround.

### Slice 6: Quality-Preserving Search Research

- investigate only exact reformulations, propagation, valid redundant constraints, dominance rules, symmetry breaking, incumbent bounds, and solver-native search support;
- compare iterative SCC growth with direct full-graph and later lossless harness experiments through the same joint-solver contract;
- document the proof or equivalence argument for every reduction;
- compare model size, incumbent quality, bound progress, and runtime against the faithful baseline.

### Slice 7: MVP Benchmark

- run the complete Heavy Xiranite request under an explicit caller bound and resource budget;
- publish success or truthful partial history automatically;
- inspect every completed phase in HTML;
- report formulation metrics and remaining exact-search blockers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- No authoritative path calls a placement or routing heuristic.
- Placement changes when routing constraints or route objectives require it, within one solver model.
- Harness and orchestration strategies can be replaced independently during the joint experiment.
- The joint solver architecture may be replaced by a later explicitly approved experiment when measurements justify it; it is not a domain invariant.
- Iterative SCC growth is retained only if measurements support it; it is not treated as a domain truth.
- The final full-graph solve is not constrained by earlier phase placements, ports, routes, or components.
- Port direction, transport kind, and outside-adjacent connection cells are solver constraints.
- Belt and pipe are independent layers.
- Every active route cell belongs to one complete source-to-target path.
- No disconnected circulation or active zero-flow geometry is possible.
- Request ceilings and actual used bounds remain distinct.
- Prior solutions are non-binding hints.
- Every search-space reduction preserves the legal solution set and objective quality.
- Timeout returns a complete validated incumbent when one exists, otherwise structured `unknown`.
- HTML is generated automatically for all reportable outcomes.
