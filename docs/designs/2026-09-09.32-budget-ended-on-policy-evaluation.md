# Evaluate retained policies after the training arm guard

## Trigger and amendment

The report-30 trainer has terminated at its declared 5400-second imitation-warm
arm guard. Random initialization completed 64 updates. Imitation initialization
completed 51, then sampled three of the next group's four episodes without an
optimizer update. The original `partial.json`, checkpoints, trajectories and
frozen source/configuration remain unchanged. The complete-only verification
supervisor stopped before executing any audit.

This amendment is made after observing that resource termination and before
opening any prospective evaluation factory. Do not extend training, restart an
arm, choose a favorable intermediate checkpoint or claim that both arms completed
64 updates. Evaluate the last available completed-update policy in each arm, with
the original initializations as controls. The comparison has unequal update counts
under the same configured time ceiling; report that limit explicitly. The original
64-update study remains incomplete.

## Terminal analysis contract

Input is the unchanged frozen protocol and exactly one original terminal source:
`training-report.json` for completed training or `partial.json` for the recognized
`on-policy.arm-budget` termination. Reject other failures, inconsistent memberships,
nonsequential updates, duplicate or missing episodes and unexpected checkpoints.

Write a separate `training-result.json` with source/protocol hashes, status
`complete` or `budget_exhausted`, planned and actual group/episode/action counts,
completed and unapplied episode membership, and each attempted arm's initial and
terminal checkpoint. Never manufacture a completed trainer report. A completed
arm's terminal is its saved final checkpoint. A budget-ended arm's terminal is its
last completed group checkpoint, or its initialization if it made no update.
Unstarted arms are explicit and cannot satisfy the two-initialization evaluation.

Retain each episode's `episode.json.gz`, `best.json.gz` and `states.json.gz`, as
well as all expected group checkpoints. The terminal-result loader requires these
artifacts even after the audits pass. Snapshot cleanup therefore requires a future
explicit retention-contract change; audit completion alone is insufficient.

The original partial report does not contain an exact elapsed arm time. Record
that value as unavailable and the configured 5400 seconds as a verified lower
bound implied by the recognized guard, not as an exact runtime measurement.
Retain measured group and episode durations with their narrower scopes.

The independent checkpoint audit checks every unique retained model/Adam/RNG
checkpoint, strict architecture/initialization, policy identity and optimizer step
counts. The physical replay audit checks every recorded episode, including the
three unapplied episodes, against the full original production/terminal graph,
native rules, ordinary actions and independent history masks. Unapplied episodes
must use the last completed policy but receive no claimed gradient replay, group
return or optimizer update. Missing later planned episodes remain in the planned
denominator and are not reported as executed.

## Evaluation and verification

All analysis consumers use the explicit terminal-result contract. Evaluation
method names are `random-initial`, `random-terminal`, `imitation-warm-initial`,
`imitation-warm-terminal` and `geometry`. Retain actual completed update counts
and terminal roles in the frozen evaluation protocol. Both terminal checkpoints
and the full training replay audit must be fixed before prospective admission.
Keep the previously declared factories, seeds, action/episode/work limits and
7200-second evaluation guard; perform no further parameter updates.

Test completed and budget-ended accounting, unstarted arms, zero-update endpoints,
unapplied episode handling, and rejection of invalid termination causes, update
gaps, duplicate/missing episodes and missing or unexpected checkpoints. Run the
actual terminal-result/checkpoint/physical audit CLIs, then normal repository
verification. Report any audit failure before admitting future factories.

Finalize reports 30/31 with the measured bounded result and this amendment. The
retained policy comparison is a diagnostic outcome, not completion of the original
Heavy Xiranite 1%/visual target, sustained improvement or production CLI goal.
