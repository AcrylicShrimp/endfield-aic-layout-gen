# Multi-Edge Integrated Routing

## Contract

The integrated layout stage accepts any number of facility-to-facility logical edges. One Pumpkin model chooses all facility placements, rotations, source ports, target ports, and directed routes simultaneously.

For logical edge `k`, the model creates an independent directed grid-flow network and enforces flow conservation from its selected source connection cell to its selected target connection cell. All edge networks reference the same facility placement variables.

## Exclusivity

For every grid cell, at most one of the following may be selected:

- a facility placement candidate covering the cell;
- a route cell belonging to any logical edge.

This is intentionally restrictive. It proves that every returned route is independently realizable without assuming unmodeled splitter, converger, bridge, crossing, or shared-capacity behavior. It also prevents two logical edges from silently reusing one facility port connection cell.

The restriction currently applies across belt and pipe routes as well. A later transport-component contract may permit specific overlaps or shared segments when external game data proves their geometry and capacity rules.

## Objective and Output

The objective minimizes the sum of selected directed arcs across all routes. The output preserves input edge order and reports the selected endpoint ports and ordered cells for each edge.

A cyclic production graph requires no special spatial exception. Its directed logical edges are routed exactly like an acyclic graph; the upstream throughput stage remains responsible for steady-state balance and bootstrap diagnostics.

## Deferred Work

- belt and pipe capacity;
- shared trunks and explicit splitters or convergers;
- legal bridges and crossings;
- component-specific geometry and item filtering.
