# Recipe-sized layout dataset generator

Status: exploratory implementation. The subtree best-match algorithm and reuse
of the path-specific source-plan output were superseded on 2026-09-16 by the
aggregate CP evidence in
`../reports/2026-09-16-recipe-flow-cp-prototype.md`. The dataset contract and
physical-generation direction remain active; the semantic output contract is
being prototyped before it is frozen.

## Goal

Build a high-throughput generator of complete, validated layouts for dataset
supply. The dataset's two primary controlled axes are:

1. facility count, which controls semantic problem scale; and
2. facility packing, which controls whether the spatial arrangement is compact,
   balanced or spacious.

Recipes provide realistic facility types, instance counts and material
connections. Seeded variation supplies different recipes, source-plan cuts,
rotations, sequence pairs, ports and routes inside each requested scale/packing
cell.

The ordered acceptance priorities are:

1. semantic and physical correctness;
2. fidelity to the requested facility count and packing profile;
3. raw generation yield across every requested cell; and
4. integrated generation throughput, including semantic selection, placement,
   routing, validation and publication.

There is no general layout-quality optimization phase. The generator does not
seek one smallest, shortest or most human-looking layout. Once a result is valid
and satisfies its requested cell, diversity is preferable to repeatedly selecting
the same scalar-best arrangement.

Speed remains an architectural constraint from the first slice. Persistent
workers, shared immutable catalogs, compact candidate state, cached semantic
evaluation, incremental routing updates and one-time publication must be designed
in before large corpus generation.

## Dataset contract

### Controlled axes

The public request samples and then fixes:

- one exact target facility count `N` from an inclusive configured range; and
- one packing profile from the configured set.

The first named packing profiles are `compact`, `balanced` and `spacious`. Their
meaning is defined by versioned facility-density bands, not by exposed gap or
canvas parameters.

Facility packing density is:

```text
sum of facility body cells / area of the facility-body bounding rectangle
```

It is measured from facility bodies only, before route extents alter the final
layout bounds. Final union occupancy, route extent, transport cells, turns and
components remain recorded observations. They do not redefine the requested
packing profile.

Density bands must be calibrated from real supported layouts and kept disjoint.
Their numerical limits are a versioned policy decision backed by a controlled
survey, not part of this architectural design. A result outside its requested
band is a `packing-target-missed` failure rather than a successful sample in the
wrong dataset cell.

### Seeded nuisance variation

The following properties vary by seed and are recorded, but are not independent
public control axes in the first version:

- target item and root recipe;
- target rate within a finite credible domain;
- upstream recipe expansion and external-input cuts;
- facility definitions implied by the recipes;
- rotations and sequence-pair topology;
- exact whitespace pattern within the requested density band;
- compatible port assignments;
- negotiated routes and legal components; and
- measured topology, transport mix and geometry.

This keeps the request surface small while retaining the variation needed by a
dataset. Later evidence may justify another explicit axis, but connection density,
long-link preference or arbitrary graph topology are not user-facing controls;
the selected recipes determine them.

### Reproducibility and failures

The same catalogs, policy version, request and seed produce the same semantic
selection and physical result independent of worker scheduling. Reports retain
requested and achieved axes, catalog identity, semantic identity, policy version,
attempt seeds, stage work and structured failures.

A survey slot is never replaced. Semantic count mismatch, packing miss, routing
exhaustion and validation failure remain rows in their originally requested
cells. A production caller may use bounded request-preserving retries, but those
retries do not establish raw yield.

## Recipes own semantic problem generation

A chosen recipe source plan and target rate determine:

- which items are consumed and produced;
- which facility modes execute the recipes;
- the exact required facility instances and utilization;
- directed item/rate obligations between instances;
- transport kinds and shared-network opportunities; and
- explicit external inputs, target output and surplus outputs.

The generator reuses the existing authoritative pipeline:

```text
RecipeSourcePlanRequest
-> contextual production graph and throughput
-> contextual facility requirements
-> facility-instance wiring
```

It does not synthesize a plausible random graph or duplicate recipe arithmetic.

## Boundary-closed partial factories

A partial factory is a connected production problem rooted at a selected output
recipe. It is not an arbitrary subset of facility instances.

For every non-root material-demand path, the source plan chooses one of:

1. expand through a recipe that produces the demanded item; or
2. cut at that path and represent the material as an explicit external input.

The root output remains an explicit target boundary. Recipe surplus remains an
explicit output. Every internal recipe demand is covered by an internal flow, and
every cut demand is covered by an external boundary.

Cycles are atomic expansion units. A cut may occur outside a cyclic dependency,
but it may not split the cycle and leave an unsatisfied internal flow or bootstrap
obligation. The existing source-plan cycle semantics remain authoritative.

## Facility-count best match

### Candidate state

A semantic candidate consists of:

```text
(target output item,
 root recipe,
 target rate from the configured grid,
 path-specific recipe/external-input selections)
```

A multi-output recipe contributes a distinct root candidate for each eligible
target output. Other outputs follow the existing surplus contract.

### Moves

- `expand(path, recipe)` replaces an external boundary with a producer recipe
  occurrence and its new input boundaries.
- `contract(path)` replaces a non-root recipe subtree with an external boundary.
- `switch(path, recipe)` selects another producer for the same demanded item.
- `adjust-rate(previous|next)` moves within the configured target-rate grid or to
  a known facility-count breakpoint when it can be derived without changing
  semantics.

The exact existing semantic pipeline evaluates each retained candidate. A cheap
incremental estimate may order moves, but it cannot admit or rank the final result
in place of the exact calculated facility count.

### Ranking and diversity

Semantic validity is a hard gate. The primary score is:

```text
abs(achieved_facilities - requested_facilities)
```

Search keeps a bounded beam per root recipe. Each root contributes only a fixed
bounded number of best states to the final tier, preventing recipes with more
producer alternatives from dominating through state multiplicity.

Final selection uses the globally smallest count-error tier. Exact ties are
resolved by the request seed, not by recipe ID, discovery order, physical ease or
downstream score. The result records requested count, achieved count, signed and
absolute error, and whether the match is exact.

The target-rate domain is finite, explicit and credible for data generation.
Unbounded rate adjustment is forbidden because it can match a large target by
cloning a shallow stage arbitrarily.

Search terminates on exact match under its policy, bounded frontier exhaustion,
work exhaustion or cancellation. A closest result is best effort, not proof that
no closer source plan exists.

### Efficient evaluation

The normal implementation must not parse catalogs, initialize solvers or copy and
serialize a complete source tree for every neighbor. It should use:

- one shared validated producer/recipe index;
- compact canonical source-plan identities;
- parent-plus-move or another persistent candidate representation;
- an exact-evaluation cache keyed by source plan and target rate;
- cached rate/count breakpoints where valid; and
- verbose report materialization only for the selected plan and compact failure
  evidence.

## Frozen semantic problem

Semantic selection finishes before placement. The chosen rate, facility
instances, wiring, items and external boundaries then become immutable physical
obligations.

Physical attempts may vary rotations, sequence pair, gaps, compatible ports,
routing order, negotiated paths and legal component realization. They may not
change recipes, source-plan cuts, rate, facility instances or wiring after seeing
physical difficulty.

This boundary prevents the generator from producing easy layouts by silently
changing the problem. It also lets the same semantic instance be paired with
several spatial seeds when a dataset explicitly requests multiple layouts for one
problem.

## Packing-controlled placement

A seeded random sequence pair and legal rotations define an initial
nonoverlapping placement. Gap construction targets the requested facility-density
band, but the connection graph is already fixed and participates in feasibility
screening.

Placement search has three responsibilities:

1. preserve nonoverlap and facility rules;
2. stay inside the requested packing-density band; and
3. leave a layout that the negotiated router can realize.

It has no objective to minimize area inside the `compact` band or maximize space
inside the `spacious` band. When several validated candidates satisfy the band,
seeded selection chooses among a bounded reservoir rather than always returning
the lowest wire length or smallest area. Sequence-pair mutations, rotations and
whitespace moves exist to recover yield and produce diversity, not to converge on
one optimum.

A cheap per-layer demand estimate may reject obviously congested placements.
Bounded negotiated routing remains the routability authority. Packing bands must
be calibrated so every supported size has attainable raw yield; an impossible
profile/count cell must be exposed rather than filled with another profile.

## Ports, logical networks and negotiated routing

Random compatible port choice is a seeded initial assignment or candidate order.
It is not immutable. A network may choose another compatible directional port
during negotiation when the current terminal assignment blocks completion.

Facility-instance wiring is the obligation ledger. Compatible flows with equal
item and transport kind may share a capacity-sufficient physical network.
Producer and consumer terminals, direction, exact rate and individual logical
edges remain recoverable when trunks are shared.

Each transport layer has a compact routing-resource graph. Facility bodies and
incompatible fixed components are hard obstacles. During global negotiation,
networks may temporarily overuse otherwise exclusive resources. Present and
historical congestion costs drive bounded rip-up and reroute until resources are
legal or work is exhausted. Completion and overflow dominate path length.

Global paths are guides. Exact legalization selects ports, cells, rotations,
bridges, splitters, convergers and segment capacities. A localized legalization
failure may trigger bounded network or placement repair. Only an independently
validated exact layout is admitted.

The existing bounded A* remains an inner detailed-search kernel. The new work is
the multi-network allocation and reconsideration policy around it.

## High-throughput execution

Normal dataset generation uses persistent workers. Each owns reusable semantic
search scratch space, placement state, congestion arrays, routing queues and
legalization buffers. Workers share immutable catalogs and precomputed recipe,
facility and component indexes.

Within one request:

- cached semantic neighbors reuse exact results;
- placement mutations update affected density and demand regions;
- rip-up updates only affected networks and resource costs;
- compact integer grids replace map-heavy per-cell state where measured;
- validation runs at stable candidate and publication boundaries rather than
  every negotiation iteration; and
- only the accepted sample, compact attempt summaries and required recovery state
  are persisted.

There is no per-candidate catalog load, per-net worker initialization, full-state
snapshot, repeated stable-input hash or publication write. Worker count obeys a
conservative memory guard. Stage-specific work and wall limits are checked between
bounded units and report exhaustion rather than infeasibility.

Throughput measurement includes semantic matching, placement, routing, exact
validation and publication. A fast isolated route kernel does not establish data
supply performance.

## Evaluation

### Correctness

- Every semantic problem derives from validated external recipe data.
- Facility instances and wiring exactly cover the selected rate and boundaries.
- Physical construction preserves the frozen semantic identity.
- Every admitted layout passes independent recipe, network and geometry checks.
- Invalid or substituted samples are never admitted.

### Control fidelity

Measure by requested facility count and packing profile:

- exact facility-count match share and absolute error distribution;
- achieved facility body density and distance to the requested band;
- profile-miss failures;
- semantic recipe/depth/branch/transport composition; and
- duplicate semantic and physical identities.

### Raw yield

Use one physical attempt per fixed request. Report yield and failure stage for
every count/profile cell. Keep semantic search exhaustion, placement miss,
routing exhaustion and validation failure separate. Production retries are a
different metric.

### Throughput and resources

Measure end-to-end layouts per second, p50/p95 latency, semantic candidates,
placement candidates, routing work/iterations, validation/publication time, peak
resident memory and bytes written. Include failures and use stable release-mode
panels.

### Diversity

Within each count/profile cell, record recipe families, graph descriptors,
rotations, sequence-pair identities, aspect ratios, density position within the
band, port assignments, route descriptors and exact duplicate hashes. Diversity
is coverage evidence, not a scalar objective used to rank every candidate.

## Continuity map

| Existing benefit | Reuse or replacement | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe source plans, throughput and facility counts | Reuse as exact semantic evaluator | Same plan produces identical reports and wiring |
| Constructive commodity networks and catalog components | Reuse network semantics | Item/rate/capacity and component validation |
| Shared physical schema and independent validator | Reuse at publication | Every admitted sample validates |
| Bounded native A* | Reuse as inner route search | Fixed-query and integrated comparison |
| Persistent native workers and shared catalogs | Reuse from first batch slice | Integrated throughput and memory |
| Seeded failure-preserving survey | Reuse with count/profile cells | No slot substitution; worker-independent identity |
| Sequence-pair representation | Reuse as varied placement state | Nonoverlap, density fidelity and yield |
| Random graph and route-conditioned edge acceptance | Remove | Wiring freezes before geometry |
| First-come immutable routes | Replace | Negotiated-routing comparison |
| Scalar layout quality optimization | Omit | Seeded valid reservoir preserves diversity |

## Rejected alternatives

- Treating topology, edge count or long-link fraction as primary public controls
  when recipes already determine realistic connections.
- Removing arbitrary facilities to hit a count; partial factories must be
  boundary-closed source plans.
- Unbounded target-rate scaling.
- Selecting an easier semantic problem after physical failure.
- Accepting a packing miss as another profile's successful sample.
- Freezing the first random port assignment.
- Using one random sequence pair without routing feedback.
- Minimizing area, path length or turns across every valid candidate and thereby
  collapsing dataset diversity.
- Reintroducing repeated initialization, full candidate copies or hot-loop I/O.
- Learned graph generation, learned placement or reinforcement learning before a
  deterministic generator establishes the dataset contract.
- A global exact joint solver as the target-scale generator; it remains useful
  for small references and local oracles.

## Implementation sequence

1. Prototype aggregate facility-count and material-balance CP, then add positive
   material-flow decomposition and lazy target-relevance cuts. Expand the result
   directly into facility instances and measure the integrated semantic stage.
2. Define and calibrate versioned `compact`, `balanced` and `spacious` facility
   density bands on a bounded real semantic panel.
3. Compare sequential and negotiated routing on identical fixed placements and
   frozen obligations, including flexible compatible ports.
4. Integrate one random sequence-pair placement per request and admit only the
   requested density band after exact legalization.
5. Add bounded sequence-pair mutation and seeded valid-candidate reservoirs to
   improve cell yield without scalar quality selection.
6. Integrate persistent workers, publication and count/profile surveys; measure
   end-to-end throughput and memory before corpus production.

## First implementation slice

Implement only the research semantic selector and its report boundary. Use real
normalized catalogs and a small exact-count panel. Before promoting an output
schema, it must demonstrate:

- exact facility counts from bounded recipe workloads;
- concrete material allocation for every active recipe input and output;
- explicit external cuts, target output and surplus;
- no zero-flow facilities or active components irrelevant to the target;
- seeded topology variation without one fixed scalar optimum;
- direct facility-instance expansion without path-occurrence rounding drift;
- explicit closest-match evidence when exact matching is unavailable; and
- bounded runtime with catalog, SCC and base-model reuse.

It does not place facilities, select ports, route paths, calibrate packing bands,
start training or publish a corpus. Its frozen output is the semantic input to the
next physical slice rather than a throwaway format.
