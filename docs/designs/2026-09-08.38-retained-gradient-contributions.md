# Retained gradient contribution diagnostic

Status: declared before gradient measurements. Retrospective, training cases only.

## Inputs and scope

Use the immutable update-size comparison identified by report 48 and continuation
seed 1030000, both rate multipliers 1 and 0.1, updates 9 through 16 inclusive.
This interval was selected after report 50 showed the lower-rate glass short
assessment falling from 63/64 at update eight to 47/64 at update sixteen. Include
every one of the sixteen training cases, not only the cases that regressed.
Read incoming checkpoints 8–15, outgoing checkpoints 9–16 and the 64 recorded
episodes per update. No optimizer, sampling or fresh evaluation may run.

Require matching original CUDA runtime, policy/collector source, native binary,
catalog and root identities. Verify all input hashes before reconstructing. Native
replay must reproduce candidate indices, commands, transitions, terminal flags,
incumbents and reward totals. Independently replay each episode with the reference
engine. Reproduce collector batches of sixteen and compare saved sampled log
probabilities and values at the incoming state, maximum absolute tolerance 2e-5;
report actual discrepancies rather than claiming bitwise identity.

## Measurements

Compute GAE/returns exactly as the recorded PPO implementation and normalize
advantages over the full 1024-command rollout. At the incoming checkpoint, compute
three gradients per case from its 64 observations: clipped policy loss, weighted
value loss, and negative weighted entropy. Use all named parameters, including
normalization affine parameters; keep running statistics frozen. Average the sixteen
equally sized case gradients to obtain each pooled component.

Following the user's question about previous-stage rehearsal, additionally split
each case into its 48 current-stage and 16 rehearsal observations. Compute the same
three components for each split, retaining the full-rollout advantage normalization.
Combine gradients and losses with observed 3:1 weights, and report within-case and
pooled current/rehearsal policy alignment. Current-stage does not mean original
root for the two cases still at level eight. This addition precedes any gradient
outcomes and does not alter training, exposure or the declared update interval.

Record per-case component norms, policy alignment with the other fifteen cases'
policy gradient, pooled component alignments, and complete pairwise policy/total
cosine matrices. A zero norm produces a null cosine, not artificial agreement.
For the actual saved parameter displacement, record each component's dot product
with that displacement. Positive means a first-order increase in that loss; negative
means a decrease. This is not an optimizer-step prediction: the saved displacement
includes 64 Adam steps and changing gradients over four epochs.

Also evaluate the same recorded objectives at outgoing weights with incoming
buffers, and at the complete outgoing actor. Record changes from the incoming
objectives. These fixed-data objectives are neither fresh factory outcomes nor
held-out retention measurements. They expose nonlinearity and buffer effects rather
than treating a directional derivative as an exact outcome change.

## Outputs, invariants and failure

Schema-one `gradients.json` contains source/protocol/checkpoint hashes, per-update
reconstruction errors, per-case objectives/gradient summaries, pairwise matrices,
actual displacement norm, replay/setup accounting, elapsed time and final status.
Retain incoming/outgoing hashes through completion and never modify their policy,
optimizer or RNG state. Save partial completed cells on error with stable
`retained_gradients.interrupted`, error severity, `gradient_contributions` stage,
active branch/update reference and explanatory message. Reject dirty source,
existing output, changed inputs or a failed reconstruction. Bound this diagnostic
to sixteen update cells and 900 elapsed seconds, checked between cells.

Test weighted component pooling and directional-derivative signs against an
independent direct scalar objective and finite differences, including zero gradients.
Run local Python/workspace verification and the complete CUDA diagnostic before
reporting findings. Copy/hash-check the result and independently recompute report
aggregates. Gradient conflict is an observation on these saved states, not proof
of the regression's cause or automatic authorization for gradient surgery, a new
reward, more training, or a larger capacity study. Preserve every earlier failed gate.
