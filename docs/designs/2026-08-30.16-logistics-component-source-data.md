# Logistics Component Source Data

## Finding

Splitter, converger, and connector definitions are not facility-building records. They live in four dedicated source tables at the same pinned upstream commit:

- `FactoryGridRouterTable`: belt splitter and converger;
- `FactoryGridConnecterTable`: belt logistics bridge (source ID `connector`);
- `FactoryLiquidRouterTable`: pipe splitter and converger;
- `FactoryLiquidConnectorTable`: pipe logistics bridge (source ID `connector`).

These tables are now part of the repository-managed source snapshot and checksum manifest.

## Geometry

Belt components have a one-by-one horizontal range. The splitter has one input direction and three output directions. The converger has three input directions and one output direction. The connector exposes all four directions as both input and output candidates. Official Korean item localization names the connector `물류 브리지`.

Pipe components also occupy one horizontal grid cell. Their range height and port positions use vertical coordinate 3, which is not an additional horizontal footprint dimension. Pipe splitter, converger, and connector direction topology mirrors the belt variants. Official Korean item localization names the connector `파이프 물류 브리지`.

All components carry the transport round duration of their line type. Pipe records additionally carry volume 1.

## Modeling Boundary

The source records establish footprint, rotation-relative directions, and maximum degree. Equal split behavior and line capacities are material-flow rules. A normalized logistics-component catalog should preserve component kinds and directional patterns; the integrated solver may then activate those patterns instead of permitting arbitrary route sharing at a cell.

Control ports and underground conduits require their own source tables. Bridge behavior is normalized from the connector records and their official localized item identity.
