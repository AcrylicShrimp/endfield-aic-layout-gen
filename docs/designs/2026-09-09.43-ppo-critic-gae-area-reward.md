performance under the same direct best-area reward? This is a comparison of
learning configurations, not an isolation of PPO clipping from a value baseline,
GAE, advantage normalization or repeated minibatch optimization.

Keep the stage-42 fresh product/rate/setup distribution, untrained actor seeds

Use r_t = (best_area_before - best_area_after) / initial_area. Gamma is one,

## Actor and critic

The actor is the unchanged InputContractPolicy with action-only observations.
Initialize it before the critic so its weights exactly match the earlier seeds.
The critic is a separate network and optimizer; it cannot alter actor logits,
mask construction, rollout actions or factory proposals. It predicts remaining
undiscounted fractional area improvement from a pre-action observation.

Give the critic the existing full current/best-layout observation, remaining
budget, score context and a summary of currently allowed candidate descriptors.
The critic's extra state information is explicit; it is not silently added to
the actor. Use the existing world encoder on current/best geometry and commodity
graphs, sharing its weights within the critic. The value head is initially zero.
Do not provide future rewards, outcomes, post-action geometry or evaluation data.
Actor partial observability and critic approximation remain limitations.

## PPO and GAE contract

Store immutable pre-action actor/critic observations, ordered candidate kinds,
allowed mask, sampled index, normalized behavior log probability and old value.
Bind each trajectory to the actor and critic checkpoint hashes and actual input
hashes. Recompute exactly the sampled distribution, including temperature,
kind-count adjustment and the original mask; raw logits are not probabilities.
The ratio is exp(new_log_probability - old_log_probability).

Use PPO-Clip epsilon 0.2: minimize the negative mean of
min(ratio * advantage, clip(ratio, 0.8, 1.2) * advantage), with the unchanged
0.001 entropy coefficient. Clipping is part of the surrogate objective; it is
not a hard guarantee that all observed ratios remain in [0.8, 1.2].

Compute per-episode deltas r_t + V_(t+1) - V_t and backwards GAE with lambda
0.95. Terminal bootstrap is exactly zero; never connect two episodes. Freeze
old values, advantages and value targets throughout all epochs. Standardize
advantages once over the complete rollout batch, with an explicit small-variance
floor; never replace rewards by cross-factory ranks. Fit the critic to
old_value + unnormalized_GAE with half mean-squared error.

Use four shuffled epochs and minibatches of 64 selected actions. Separate Adam
optimizers have learning rate 0.0003 and independent gradient-norm ceilings 0.5.
Hold parameters fixed while accumulating a minibatch loss, then step once.
Shuffling uses a dedicated checkpointed RNG. No replay of older-policy batches.
Measure the whole-batch nonnegative sampled KL estimate mean(ratio - 1 - log
ratio) after each epoch. Stop further epochs if it exceeds 0.02; record the actual
stop and completed minibatch steps. This limits additional work, not factory
validity or the number of fresh rollout batches.

## Method references

[PPO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347) defines the clipped surrogate and
multiple epochs of minibatch updates. [GAE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438)
describes the value-based advantage estimator and its bias/variance tradeoff.
These methods do not guarantee improvement on this factory distribution.
