# Short episodes and mastery-gated curriculum

Status: complete; see the [results](../reports/2026-09-08.15-mastery-curriculum-results.md). Follow-up to the failure analysis; no behavior
cloning, reward shaping, critic initialization change or production planner change.

## Frozen experiment

Four conditions cross training horizon 8 versus 128 with fixed versus mastery-based
advancement. Each uses the existing eight concurrent native environments, batched GPU
inference, A/B alternation, seeds 17/29/43, 65,536 sampled training edits, 1,024 samples
per PPO update, four epochs and 64-example minibatches. Short collection concatenates
16 complete eight-environment rounds per update; long collection uses one round.
Every episode terminates naturally at its configured training horizon. No success
termination, action filtering or reward changes are introduced.

Shorter episodes deliberately provide sixteen times as many resets at equal sampled
exposure: 8,192 versus 512 episodes per run. Preparation actions and wall time are
recorded separately. This is not an equal-reset comparison. Both reset count and
sample count are first-class reported outputs. Model initialization uses the same
original-root 128-step observation and common seed in all four conditions.

Fixed advancement remains 8,192 edits at remaining-one, then 8,192 at remaining-two,
then 8,192 at remaining-three, followed by original-root training. Mastery advancement
starts at remaining-one and requires at least 52 successful episodes out of 64 on
*each* case in two consecutive assessments before moving to remaining-two, then three,
then root. Failure clears the consecutive-pass count. There is no automatic advancement
when the budget expires. The current level and pass streak are checkpointed.

Assessments occur after each 8,192 sampled edits. They use 64 independent local CPU
sampling generators with seeds 200000–200063, eight edits per episode, and the same
prefixes for both training-horizon conditions. They are curriculum feedback, not
held-out outcome evidence, and never enter PPO. Fixed conditions record the same
assessment at their just-trained level; once training uses roots, assess remaining-one
as a retention diagnostic. No assessment at checkpoint zero changes the scheduler.
Record current level, per-case successful count, threshold/streak and next level.
Save a policy checkpoint at every assessment, including those without root evaluation;
independent audit replays every assessment with the Python reference environment.

Frozen outcome evaluation always uses original roots, CPU inference, 128 edits and
32 sampling seeds 100000–100031 per case at 0,8192,16384,24576,32768,49152,65536.
These results never choose curriculum advancement. Assessments cannot consume training
RNGs or mutate model parameters. The original valid base/best and reward denominator
are preserved in all prefix starts. Training, assessment and root-evaluation horizons
are explicit separate configurations and native engines.

## Outputs and failures

Outputs include declared protocol and source/native/input hashes, per-episode case,
level, setup count, return, retained score and counters; update sample/optimizer counts;
per-assessment seeds, successes and scheduler transition; original-root evaluations;
model/optimizer/all RNG checkpoints and per-stream native states. Partial collection
and accumulated complete rounds survive interruption; no automatic resume is claimed.
Every selected training champion stores its actual training horizon and setup prefix,
so independent replay uses exactly that horizon while root traces use 128.

Reject incompatible schemas, non-divisible budgets, inconsistent model feature meaning,
out-of-domain prefixes, malformed mastery counters and existing output paths. Failures
have stable code, severity, stage, entity and message. Complete runs must pass independent
source, action/reward replay, stage transition, sample/reset and checkpoint audits.
