## Question and common sample budget

Compare the previously registered twelve observation/action contracts after
short PPO learning with a separate critic and GAE. Each candidate/seed receives
four batches of 32 freshly generated factory attempts, with 32 selected actions
per admitted episode: at most 4,096 training actions. This equals the old shallow
screen's maximum samples per candidate/seed (32 groups × four episodes × 32
selections), now grouped for parallel fresh-generation PPO collection. It is
not an equal-optimizer-step comparison with that older learning algorithm.

Use initialization seeds 17 and 29. Across 24 jobs, the planned maxima are 3,072
training factory attempts and 98,304 training selections. Evaluate at batches
0, 2 and 4 on 16 held-out generation seeds per initialization seed, regenerated
at each checkpoint: 1,152 evaluation attempts and at most 36,864 evaluation
selections. Admissions are measured outcomes; rejections consume their attempt
without replacement. Do not report these planned maxima as observed work.

## Training, hardware and bounds

Use the checked-in `ppo-input-contract-screen.json`. Keep gamma 1, GAE lambda
0.95, PPO clip 0.2, four optimizer epochs, mini/microbatch 64, target KL 0.02,
learning rate 0.0003, entropy coefficient 0.001 and gradient norm cap 0.5. Keep
actual recorded behavior probabilities, direct retained-best area reward and
temporary native-valid worsening. The initial constructor and native validator
consume the unchanged external generation configuration and catalogs.

Use 24 persistent CPU collectors with one Torch thread each and one CUDA FP32
learner. Run jobs sequentially in the frozen shuffled order (`order_seed=20260910`);
workers collect one immutable behavior checkpoint per batch. The representative
H32 rich-input run measured 37.032 GiB aggregate RSS. This screen allows 48 GiB
aggregate RSS to provide headroom on the approximately 62 GiB server, with an
8 GiB disk reserve. Aggregate RSS includes shared pages counted per process.

The cumulative wall ceiling is 10,800 seconds, with startup 120 seconds, task
360 seconds, phase collection 900 seconds, progress 400 seconds and cleanup
20 seconds. These are operational ceilings, not promises of successful completion
or dimensions of the factory request. Any stop remains explicit evidence.

Training generation/action seed bases are 171,000,000 / 181,000,000; held-out
bases are 191,000,000 / 201,000,000. The harness adds initialization-seed and
attempt offsets and checks disjointness. Training factories are drawn freshly
for each episode; no fixed initial factory is repeatedly reset for training.

## Evaluation and decision rule

The primary metric is mean retained-best bounding-box area reduction from each
factory's starting layout, reported separately per initialization seed. Also
report paired change from the same candidate's untrained checkpoint. Use one
common admitted evaluation seed cohort across all twelve candidates and all
three evaluation checkpoints within an initialization seed, with request and
initial-layout hashes equal. Show cohort size and independent admission rates.
An empty cohort produces no fabricated comparison value.

Use equal weight for the two initialization seeds when displaying a compact
candidate mean. Include individual seed results and regressions; two seeds and
short training do not establish a statistically reliable winner. Do not select
hyperparameters or extend only promising candidates using these held-out results.
This screen measures the declared four-batch endpoint; it does not establish
long-run convergence or claim that more informative inputs must learn faster.

W&B is primary: a stable candidate/seed run in one protocol group, with outcomes
against actions, batches and elapsed seconds, plus PPO diagnostics. Shared-cohort
outcomes appear when all candidates for a seed finish; earlier independent curves
are provisional. A local PNG/SVG and compact JSON summary mirror committed data.
