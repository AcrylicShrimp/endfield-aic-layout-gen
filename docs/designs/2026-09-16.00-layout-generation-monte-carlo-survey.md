# Layout generation Monte Carlo survey

Status: completed as a baseline, then superseded by the
[scale-aware generator design](2026-09-16.01-scale-aware-layout-generator.md).

This document records the original mechanism-level survey. Its retry and
configuration contract is historical; the current one-attempt semantic survey
contract is defined by the successor design.

## Goal

Measure the procedural generator's attainable spatial-occupancy region before
choosing a replacement pretraining distribution. The survey explores facility
count, records generation yield and cost, and observes successful layouts. It
does not prescribe the final facility or occupancy buckets and does not launch
training.

Spatial occupancy means `(facility body cells + unique transport cells) /
bounding-box area`, the metric already computed by independent physical layout
validation. Connection count per facility is a nuisance generator variable, not
the density being selected for learning.

## Survey model

Use provisional facility-count buckets:

`2-3`, `4-5`, `6-7`, `8-11`, `12-15`, `16-23`, `24-31`, `32-39`, `40-49`,
`50-63`, `64-79`, and `80-100`.

Every bucket receives exactly the same number of requested Monte Carlo slots.
Within a bucket, the existing seeded generator samples an exact facility count
uniformly from the inclusive endpoints. It continues to randomize eligible
facility definitions, rotations, sequence-pair placement, gaps, compatible
directed connections, transport and routing according to the supplied generator
configuration. Those variables need broad diversity; the survey does not balance
or optimize their individual values.

Create one validated generator per bucket and reuse it across that bucket's
slots. Catalogs are loaded and validated once. Seeds occupy a deterministic,
non-overlapping namespace by bucket and slot, independent of worker scheduling.

## Evidence contract

Record every requested slot, including failure. A successful row records its
actual facility and connection counts, width, height, area, body cells,
transport cells, occupancy, generation time, attempt count and total routing
work. A failed row records its diagnostic, last attempted facility and target
connection counts, connected count, attempt count, routing work and elapsed
time. Never replace a failed large-bucket slot with a sample from another bucket.

Summarize each bucket with requested/completed counts, yield, elapsed cost,
failure reasons and occupancy quantiles. The report must distinguish rare
extremes from repeatedly populated occupancy regions. Preserve the complete
compact report under ignored `artifacts/`; commit only the configuration,
reproduction command and later findings.

The invocation has an explicit wall-time bound. Reaching it produces an
incomplete survey with retained completed rows, not an infeasibility claim.
Normal generation and online pretraining behavior remain unchanged.

## Decision after the survey

The provisional facility buckets may be revised after inspecting yield and
cost. Spatial-occupancy bucket boundaries will be proposed from the measured
support and agreed with the user before implementation or retraining. A
high-facility bucket that has inadequate yield triggers generator investigation;
it is not silently removed.

Recipe semantics are outside this survey. The outcome encoder predicts physical
edit consequences from geometry, ports, transport obligations and current
routes, so recipe-free randomized layouts remain suitable for this experiment.

## Rejected approaches

- Reusing the hidden fixed 8/16 split.
- Selecting occupancy bands before measuring the generator.
- Treating `edges_per_block` as spatial occupancy.
- Keeping only successes or redistributing failed bucket quota.
- Starting another long pretraining run before reviewing the survey.

## First implementation slice

Add a compact `aic-layout-gen survey` CLI that accepts a versioned survey
configuration, reuses the existing generator and validation metrics, publishes
all rows and per-bucket summaries, and exercises one bounded release-mode pilot
over the provisional equal-attempt buckets.
