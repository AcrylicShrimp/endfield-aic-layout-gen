# Deployment-matched entity checkpoint retention

Status: removed from the required research path before outcome execution. The
implementation is retained as an optional strict reliability diagnostic; its
all-case/all-seed gates are not a prerequisite for curriculum growth or exploratory
transfer measurement. No selection, confirmation or transfer outcome in this
protocol has been sampled. Reports 46 and 54 keep their original failed results.

The user questioned whether every input must succeed before research may advance.
The coordinator reassessed the progression rule: 26/32 is an 81.25% per-cell
criterion, but requiring it simultaneously in every case and training seed is a
much stricter joint requirement. It was an experiment-specific choice, not a
standard curriculum-learning requirement. Preserve strict layout validity and
incumbent score acceptance, while treating stochastic improvement frequency and
weak cases as measured performance rather than a universal exploration stop.

Next declare a bounded transfer-measurement procedure with policies frozen before
new outcomes, complete case/seed reporting and separate development/confirmation.
Do not relabel existing failures as passes or lower the final factory objectives.
The unused strict protocol below remains reproducible if explicitly needed.

This distinction is consistent with curricula that adapt task sampling to learning
progress ([Portelas et al., 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/portelas20a.html))
or focus on intermediate-success start states while retaining old starts
([Florensa et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/florensa17a.html)). Neither
paper establishes a universal numeric progression threshold for this project.

## Input and selection contract

Use all six audited width-one/two, seed-17/29/43 training streams and their original
nine root-evaluation checkpoints: zero and every 393216 commands through 3145728.
This is the complete original root-checkpoint grid, not the denser set of 97 stored
assessment checkpoints. The source matrix, all 54 checkpoint hashes and report-54
comparison hash are frozen in `entity-restart-selection.json`. Export every actor
before evaluating any of them. Keep the corrected native admission and trimmed
sampling provenance from design 41; require unchanged policy and game inputs.

For each checkpoint evaluate all sixteen admitted training roots, 32 episodes at
fresh bank 1170000. Use four 32-command attempts, each reset to the original valid
root, one continuous categorical generator per episode and CUDA waves of sixteen.
Charge all 128 commands and three resets; keep the lexicographically best valid
incumbent. No setup, calibration, free repair or early-success termination.

A checkpoint qualifies only if every case has at least 26/32 translated valid
area improvements. For each stream select the qualifier with lowest exact mean
per-case upper-median area ratio, then mean median physical tiles, then turns,
then earlier training commands. If no checkpoint qualifies, retain none. A width
is eligible only if all three original training seeds have a selected checkpoint.
Complete all 864 selection cells before making or publishing selections. Publish
every checkpoint curve, including zero-policy and failed cases; no cell-only retry.

Selection is development work on already used training geometries, not independent
confirmation. Report 54 demonstrates why continuous-inference checkpoint quality
cannot stand in for restarted-inference quality. Selecting earlier weights here
does not relabel its failed final-checkpoint experiment or demonstrate a repair of
continued learning. The new selection procedure still needs fresh confirmation.

## Frozen confirmation and transfer

Persist all six selections, including missing ones, and eligible widths before
opening any subsequent outcome. Keep selections fixed through all later stages.

1. For every eligible width evaluate its three actors on all sixteen original
   roots at fresh banks 1180000 and 1190000, 32 episodes each. Require 26/32 in
   every cell. Complete every eligible width's root cells before advancing. A
   width failing root confirmation cannot open development outcomes.
2. Evaluate every root-confirmed width on all twelve admitted development cases,
   64 episodes at the original unopened bank 940000. Require 52/64 in every cell.
   Complete all such widths before choosing among passing widths by lowest exact
   mean median area ratio, tiles, turns, then fewer policy parameters.
3. Only the chosen width's three frozen actors see the eight held-out cases at
   original unopened banks 950000 and 960000, 32 episodes each. Require 26/32 in
   all 48 cells. A failed held-out result does not trigger another width or policy.

All stages use the same 4x32 procedure and score priority. Report actual quality as
well as success; passing frequency is not a density or optimization-quality claim.
Failures stop dependent stages without reselecting checkpoints or changing banks.
No downstream stage is allowed to evaluate an empty set and count that as passing.
