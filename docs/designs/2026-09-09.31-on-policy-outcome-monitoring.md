# On-policy outcome monitoring

## Contract

Read the frozen report-30 protocol and a snapshot of its training progress, final
report or partial report. Do not execute environment actions, train a policy, read
teacher labels, open future requests or change the running learner. The user asked
to track whether actual outcomes improve as RL continues.

Only include completed update groups whose four completed episodes match the
recorded case, attempts and terminal scores. A point describes the policy **before**
that group's update: group zero samples the initial policy. Plot each fixed original
or prepared reset case separately. Different-sized factories and raw/prepared starts
must not silently replace one another along a single learning curve.

Primary observations are mean incumbent area reduction relative to that fixed reset
and the observed minimum/maximum across its four episodes. Also retain exact-score
improvement counts, final empty-XY percentage, reported improving pose/reroute action
counts and separate reward/entropy gradient norms. The interval is an observed range,
not a confidence interval. Mean within-group relative return is always zero by
construction and is not a learning-progress metric.

For an optional balanced summary, compare only complete curriculum cycles containing
every training case once, with equal case weighting. Keep raw and prepared means
separate. Record incomplete episode/group counts and retain source-file hashes in
each snapshot. Group-time sums omit arm setup/finalization and are not exact wall
timestamps. Completed-update count is the primary horizontal coordinate.

Write English JSON and standalone PNG/SVG plots under ignored artifacts. Atomic
replacement keeps the previous readable snapshot while rendering a new one. A
bounded watch command refreshes only when completed groups or terminal status change
and stops when the trainer publishes a final/partial report. Missing or partially
written progress is retried; missing output alone never proves a process stopped.
Read-only plotting is lightweight monitoring added after training began, not another
timed environment workload. Record its duration rather than claiming zero overhead.

## Interpretation and verification

These are exploratory on-policy training outcomes with four sampled episodes per
visit and changing sampling seeds. First/latest differences are descriptive, not
paired-seed evaluations, statistical significance or held-out generalization. Check
whether improvement persists across visits and factories; do not interpret a larger
gradient or finite parameter change as a better factory. Independent full-trace
audits and the already specified frozen initial/final evaluation remain required.

Monotonic improvement at every update or visit is not an acceptance criterion.
Exploration and sampling variation can produce declines during useful learning. A
few declining or flat observations do not establish failure; inspect longer-window
mean outcomes, the fraction of episodes that improve the exact score, and variation,
then compare the frozen policies under the same evaluation conditions. Do not treat
the initial five-cycle fluctuation alone as evidence that RL cannot learn this task.

Test case separation, exclusion of incomplete groups, update indexing, equal-case
cycle aggregation and lexicographic improvements with unchanged area. Inspect an
actual generated plot and verify the CLI against the live training records.
