# Batched policy evaluation and recorded-command assessment audit

Status: contract declared after the frozen-actor timing diagnosis, before implementation.

Replace serial episode inference in original-root evaluation and curriculum assessments
with bounded waves of independent native environments. `evaluation_batch_size` is a
positive external setting (default 16); the final wave may be smaller. Every episode
keeps its original CPU generator seeded by its declared case bank, horizon, prefix
and complete edit budget. Batch only policy computation; native domain/transition and
acceptance remain authoritative. No free repair or additional attempts. Prepare ragged
observations with the existing schema-2 masks and perform float32 inference on the
actor's device. Transfer log probabilities to the host before each independent draw.

The case-curriculum runner accepts `evaluation_device` (default CPU); the entity study
sets CUDA after a bounded end-to-end verification. Keep a frozen evaluation actor,
refreshing its parameters/buffers only after a completed PPO update. Copy state directly
to the destination actor device instead of forcing a GPU-to-CPU-to-GPU round trip.
Record actual device/batch width and separated evaluation/assessment costs. Model
architecture, PPO sampling/rehearsal, reward and accepted edit domain are unchanged.

Assessment DTO version 2 contains a frozen policy hash and each episode's actual
command list, seed, level, return, score and counters. Every command count must equal
the declared horizon. The independent audit replays those commands in the Python
reference from the declared prefix, checks legal domain membership, applied transitions,
valid retained output and exact reward/score/counters, then reconstructs controller
advancement and exposure. Match the gate policy hash to its saved checkpoint. Reject
legacy assessment DTOs explicitly; use their original frozen revision to audit history.
Do not resample the policy as a prerequisite for accepting a mechanically valid trace.
Policy output parity under batching/device changes is a separate numerical check.

Keep existing root-evaluation report fields and representative best/median artifacts;
case/episode row order and seed identity remain stable. Frozen-policy held-out restart
studies are outside this bounded evaluator change. No policy-quality comparison may
count a differently batched repeat as independent training evidence.

Verify ragged final waves, prefix and original-root semantics, seeded episode accounting,
unchanged policy/BN state, independent replay and tamper rejection. Re-run both short
width cost protocols on fresh streams solely for total-cost comparison and executable
validation. Preserve old pilot evidence; small numerical/training-trajectory differences
are allowed and do not establish quality superiority. Declare the full learning curves
only after costs and audit behavior are verified.
