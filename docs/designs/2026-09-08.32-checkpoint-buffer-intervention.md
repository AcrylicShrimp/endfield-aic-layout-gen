# Checkpoint weight and normalization-buffer intervention

Status: declared before intervention outcomes. Training-only diagnosis, no updates.

Width-one seed-43 qualifies at 1179648 commands, then its next 16-command
assessment at 1212416 drops from 1006/1024 to 849/1024 successes. The intervening
32 PPO updates report approximate KL before their final normalization calibration.
Calibration changes running statistics outside that reported loss calculation.
This is a measurement gap, not proof that calibration causes the regression.

## Contract

Inputs: audited width-one seed-43 checkpoints at exactly 1179648 and 1212416
commands, unchanged policy/native/catalog sources, sixteen admitted training cases,
and `tools/factory-edit-pilot/buffer-intervention.json`. Verify both hashes against
the completed audit and checkpoint metadata before inference. Preserve originals.

Construct four diagnostic actors: old weights/old buffers, new weights/new buffers,
new weights/old buffers, and old weights/new buffers. Weights mean all named
parameters, including normalization affine parameters. Buffers mean only the three
normalization layers' `running_mean`, `running_var`, and `num_batches_tracked`.
Reject any unexpected buffer or unclassified state key. Freeze and hash all four
actors before observing outcomes. Synthetic combinations are diagnostic artifacts,
not production policies or capacity-retention candidates.

Evaluate every actor on every original training root at 128 native-clock commands,
with 32 paired seeds starting at 1025000, CPU, one thread, batches of sixteen.
All 2048 incumbents must pass native and independent reference acceptance. Replay
best and median traces per cell independently, including candidate-domain and
transition checks. Charge all 262144 commands, without reset, prefix or free repair.

Output: schema-one report with exact source/checkpoint/actor hashes, per-case
episode scores and counts, translated successes, changed completions, score
medians, paired wins/ties/losses and timings. Retain all 128 selected traces and
layouts. Include the intervening saved update-metric summary and its input hash;
do not label that approximate KL as a post-calibration policy-distance measure.
Verify all four actor hashes are unchanged at the end.

Reject incompatible protocols, changed sources, incomplete audits and existing
outputs. Failures use `buffer_intervention.*` codes, error severity,
`checkpoint_buffer_intervention` stage and the active case when available.

## Interpretation

Compare matched seeds among all four conditions. If replacing only new buffers
with old buffers recovers lost performance, and old weights with new buffers lose
performance, normalization state is a supported contributor in this selected
checkpoint interval. Weight and buffer interactions may make the result mixed.
The interval contains 32 updates, so this does not identify an individual update
or prove that the old buffers remain correct for new convolutional features.

This is post-selected diagnosis of one observed regression, not held-out evidence
or a general normalization comparison. Any calibration change or training restart
requires a separate declared intervention and matched evaluation. Keep both server
source checkouts and the six-stream matrix untouched.
