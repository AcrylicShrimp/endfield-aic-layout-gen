# Increase initiating-move exposure and retain original-large contraction behavior

## Evidence and controlled change

Report 23 replays the entire evaluation and finds both adapted models fail to enter
the known large compression trajectory. Root-balanced sampling gives its initiating
pose only 19 of 512 updates. One model also replaces useful contractions on the
uncompressed original with repeated valid worsening moves. Neither observation
proves an operator or representation impossibility.

Keep the parent weights from report 13, model, objective, optimizer, learning rate,
gradient clip, two adaptation seeds, 512 updates and all fourteen evaluation starts
unchanged. Change curriculum exposure and explicitly admit an original-large
contraction lesson. Do not continue training report 23's adapted checkpoints.

## Additional teacher and schedule

Reuse report 23's ten independently audited lesson states verbatim, including their
original episode contexts. On the uncompressed 116-facility original, enumerate all
ordinary contractions and execute each independently through native acceptance under
the existing route/time bounds. The new target set is every tied minimum full-score
outcome among completed improving contractions. This is a minimum over contractions,
not a proof that an unmeasured pose cannot do better. Independently replay all of
these candidates against the complete original graph before optimization.

Within each sixteen-update block, shuffle eight initiating-pose lessons, four large
continuation lessons, two original-large contraction lessons and two smaller-root
rehearsal lessons. Cycle the three continuation states and the six smaller roots;
the latter's shuffled order also uses the declared seed. Each 512-update adaptation
therefore gets 256 initiating-pose, 128 continuation, 64 original-large and 64 smaller
rehearsal updates. This deliberately changes weighting; report retention rather
than assuming the smaller allocation preserves prior performance.

admission audit plus an independent audit of all added contraction candidates and
new schedule quotas. The shared training/evaluation runner must preserve incumbent,
score and budget semantics. Each of the parent, both learned checkpoints and geometry
receives the same two banks, temperature 0.05, eight selections, 30 seconds, 5000 route
expansions and a fresh runtime on every one of the fourteen cases.

Replay all evaluated actions and bests independently. Report teacher ranks alongside
actual translation, area changes and full-score comparisons against both the parent
and report 23's matching learned seeds. Inspect native-validated complete views for
new learned large outcomes. A different contraction order can be useful even when
it does not match the recorded teacher sequence exactly.

All data remains opened development or training-family evidence. The schedule seeds
share one pretrained parent. Do not claim unopened generalization or production
